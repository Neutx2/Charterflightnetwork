Lac Louise - Manic 5 is located in south central Quebec, north of Baie Comeau. There is a hydro power generating station here, however there is no airport. Charter flights are available with float planes from the local charter service operated by Air Saguenay. The closest land airport would be to the south at Baie-Comeau.
