### What Is A Private Charter Flight?

####

A Private Charter Flight Is Like Hiring A Limo Service. You Choose Your Pick-Up Time, You Choose Your Destination, You and Your Associates Are The Only Passengers On Board. The Cost Is Not By The Seat, But Rather The Service Is Charged Out Either By The Mile or Per Flight Hour, Plus Other Fees As May Be Outlined In The Charter Quote.

When You Request A Charter Quote, You Will Receive Pricing For The Most Appropriate Aircraft Based On: The Number Of Passengers You Have, The Flight Distance, The Anticipated Weather Conditions, Airport Runway Length and Conditions / Lake or River Availabilithy / Heli Pad Availability, etc.

When You Request A Quote Through Our Charter Quote Request Service, You May Specify Your Aircraft Type Preference. For Example, Jet, Turbo Prop, Float Plane, Helicopter.
