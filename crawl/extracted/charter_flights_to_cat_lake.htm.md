##### About Flights To Cat Lake

##### Location

Cat Lake is a remote First Nations Community accessible only by aircraft and is located about 180 km north of Sioux Lookout. The Airport is located less than a mile north of the community. If you are planning a charter flight to Cat Lake and require ground transportation or accommodations, you should first call the Band Office for information as there is no regular taxi service nor motels in Cat Lake

If you need to fly 10 - 18 passengers to or from Cat Lake or across Canada, our Charter Network Members have the aircraft with the seating capacity that will meet your needs We can source out aircraft that can pick your group up from most gravel strips located throughout the north and fly you to virtually any airport in Canada or USA
