##### Location

Goose Bay is located in east central Labrador on the south west shore of Lake Melville which is actually a saltwater tidal extension of Hamilton Inlet which stretches 140 km inland from the Atlantic Ocean and part of the Churchill River and Naskaupi River watersheds.

##### Goose Bay Airport

The local airport is primarily a Canadian Forces Base which it shares with civilian aircraft. The longest runway is 11,000 feet. There is also a busy float plane base located on Lake Melville which operates from June - November each year. Local float plane charter companies cater to hunting and fishing groups as well as numerous resource companies

##### Charter Airlines

Based in Goose Bay Labrador, Innu Mikun Airlines provides air charter services to communities and remote regions throughout Labrador and northern Quebec. The airline operates a fleet of 4 deHavilland Twin Otters which are available on wheels and floats during the summer months.

##### Transportation Hub:

Goose Bay serves as a transportation hub for Labrador, with connections by air, road, and water. Goose Bay Airport provides domestic and international flights, while the Trans-Labrador Highway connects the town to other communities in Labrador and Newfoundland.

##### Economy:

Goose Bay's economy is diverse, with sectors including transportation, government services, retail, and tourism. The presence of Goose Bay Air Base, as well as its status as a transportation hub, supports employment and economic activity in the area.

##### Military Presence:

Following World War II, Goose Bay Air Base continued to be used by the Canadian and United States military for training exercises, air patrols, and other military activities. The base remains operational today, although its military significance has diminished over the years.
