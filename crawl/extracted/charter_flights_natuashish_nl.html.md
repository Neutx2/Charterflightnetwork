Natuashish is a small Innu community located on the northern coast of Labrador, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada.

##### Location:

Natuashish is situated on the shores of the Natuashish River, near the mouth of the North West River. It is located approximately 15 kilometers northwest of the former community of Davis Inlet, which was relocated to Natuashish in 2002.

##### History:

Natuashish was established as a replacement community for Davis Inlet, which faced significant social and infrastructure challenges, including inadequate housing, substance abuse, and health issues. The relocation was part of a government-led initiative to address these issues and improve living conditions for residents.

##### Economy:

The economy of Natuashish is primarily based on fishing, hunting, and trapping, with residents relying on traditional activities for sustenance and income. Commercial fishing, particularly for species such as cod, salmon, and Arctic char, is an important economic activity in the area.

##### Transportation:

Natuashish is accessible primarily by air and water, as there are no roads connecting it to other communities. The town has a small airstrip that provides regular air service to other parts of Labrador and Newfoundland. Additionally, Natuashish has a wharf that serves as a transportation link for marine traffic.

##### Community Life:

Natuashish has a close-knit community with a strong sense of cultural identity and pride. Residents often participate in community events and gatherings, including traditional ceremonies, festivals, and celebrations. The town's cultural heritage is preserved through storytelling, music, and other cultural practices.

##### Services and Amenities:

Despite its small size and remote location, Natuashish offers basic services and amenities to its residents. These include schools, healthcare facilities, a community center, and a local store. The town is also served by government services and agencies.

##### Community Formation:

The relocation of Davis Inlet to Natuashish was completed in 2002, with the construction of new housing, infrastructure, and community facilities. The move aimed to provide residents with better access to essential services such as healthcare, education, and social support.

##### Challenges:

Like many remote communities in Labrador, Natuashish faces challenges such as limited access to essential services, economic opportunities, and infrastructure. The relocation process and ongoing social issues have presented challenges for residents and community leaders.

Overall, Natuashish is a community that has undergone significant changes in recent years due to its relocation from Davis Inlet. Despite challenges, it remains an important cultural and social hub for its residents, who continue to maintain their traditional way of life and cultural practices.
