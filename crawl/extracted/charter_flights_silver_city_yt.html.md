##### About Silver City Yukon and Location

Silver City, a hidden gem nestled in the heart of Yukon, offers a serene escape into nature. Known for its scenic beauty, rich history, and outdoor recreational activities, Silver City attracts adventurers and history buffs alike. Its proximity to pristine lakes, rugged mountains, and historic mining sites makes it a prime location for those seeking adventure and exploration in the Yukon wilderness.

##### Silver City Airport

Silver City Airport serves as a crucial gateway to Yukon's remote destinations, featuring a runway that caters to various aircraft sizes. Located just a few miles from the city, it offers essential services such as fuel, maintenance, and charter flights to adventurers looking to explore the Yukon's vast wilderness.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Dawson City Airport**

Offering access to the heart of the Klondike Gold Rush, Dawson City Airport has a 5,000-foot runway and provides services for tourists and prospectors alike.

**Old Crow Airport**

As the only airport in Yukon's Arctic region, Old Crow Airport is essential for accessing the Vuntut National Park and experiencing the culture of the Gwich'in people.

**Mayo Airport**

Mayo Airport, with its 4,000-foot runway, is the doorway to the Silver Trail region and offers unique opportunities for fishing, hiking, and exploring historic sites.

**Haines Junction Airport**

Located at the edge of the Kluane National Park, Haines Junction Airport provides an unparalleled starting point for exploring some of Canada's most stunning wilderness areas.

##### Silver City Hotels / Accommodations

**Silver Inn**

The Silver Inn offers cozy accommodations with stunning views of the surrounding wilderness, making it a perfect base for exploring the area's natural beauty.

**Yukon Lodge**

Yukon Lodge provides guests with comfortable lodging and easy access to outdoor adventures, ensuring a memorable stay in Silver City.

##### Silver City Outdoor Adventures

**Kayaking & Canoeing**

Explore the tranquil waters around Silver City by kayak or canoe, offering unique perspectives of Yukon's breathtaking landscapes.

**Mountain Hiking**

Take to the trails for some of the best mountain hiking in Yukon, with routes offering everything from gentle walks to challenging climbs.
