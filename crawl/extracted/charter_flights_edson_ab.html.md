About Edson Alberta

Edson is a town of around 8500 residents located 120 miles west of Edmonton along the Yellowhead Highway. The town's economy is based on the energy sector such as oil and natural gas in addition to the forestry industry.

Charter Flights To The Edson Airport

There is a real nice airport here in Edson for those wishing to arrange a charter flight. The local airport features a 6000 foot asphalt runway making it accessible to a wide range of charter aircraft. There are no scheduled flights to Edson Airport, however with a runway length of 6000 feet with an asphalt surface you can arrange charter flights direct to Edson using a wide range of aircraft types.
