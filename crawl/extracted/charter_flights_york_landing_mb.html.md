## York Landing Charter Flight FAQ

### **What airport serves York Landing, Manitoba?**

York Landing is served by York Landing Airport, using the codes ZAC and CZAC. It is a public airport located adjacent to the community and used for regional passenger and charter traffic.

### **What type of runway does York Landing Airport have?**

York Landing Airport has a crushed-rock runway measuring about 3,397 feet. That makes it suitable for many northern turboprop and utility aircraft used on remote Manitoba routes.

### **How close is the airport to the community?**

The airport is directly adjacent to York Landing, so ground transfers are short and practical for arriving passengers, cargo, medical travel, and essential service flights.
