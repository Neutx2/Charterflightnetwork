##### About Flights To Sioux Narrows

##### Location

Sioux Narrows is located in North Western Ontario along Highway 71 which runs along the eastern side of Lake of the Woods and connects the Fort Frances area with Kenora and Highway 17. Sioux Narrows is a popular tourist destination and is found at the end of a long bay which extends from the eastern shores of Lake of the Woods. Here you will find rustic resorts that are accessible by car or boat.

##### Sioux Narrows Airport

There is no airport in Sioux Narrows. Your closest airports are locted north in Kenora or to the south at Fort Frances

#### Sioux Narrows Wheel Plane Charter Flights

Sioux Narrows is a very popular tourism destination located on a channel off the eastern side of Lake of the Woods. There is no land airport here, so if are looking for a wheel plane charter flight, we suggest a direct flight to the Fort Frances Airport.
