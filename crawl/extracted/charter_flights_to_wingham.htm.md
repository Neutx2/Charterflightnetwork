##### [Charter Flights To Charter Flights To Wingham](#collapseOne1)

Wingham is located east of Lake Huron about halfway between Lucknow and Listowel on Hwy 86. The community is located in the heart of prime agricultural land which is the main supporting industry of the area. The town has its own local airport with a 4000 foot asphalt runway making it accessible to a wide range of charter and corporate aircraft. The airport is staffed by officers of the CBSA making it an airport of entry for flights from the USA.
