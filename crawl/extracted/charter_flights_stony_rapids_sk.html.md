Stony Rapids is located in the far northern regions of Saskatchewan at the far eastern end of Lake Athabasca. The single runway is 5,052 feet long with a treated gravel surface - making it accessible to a wide range of charter aircraft types. The airport supports charter flights, offering fuel and basic amenities to ensure a comfortable travel experience for those exploring this remote region of Saskatchewan.
