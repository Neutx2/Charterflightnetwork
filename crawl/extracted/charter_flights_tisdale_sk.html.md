Tisdale, Saskatchewan, renowned for its rich agricultural land and as the "Land of Rape and Honey," is a beacon of community and natural beauty. ##### Tisdale Airport

The community of Tisdale SK is located in East Central Saskatchewan and has a public use airport located adjacent to the town. The main runway is 3,013 feet in length with an asphalt surface - making it accessible to a wide range of charter aircraft. Contact the charter operators listed on this page for details on the types of aircraft available for charter flights to the Tisdale Airport.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Melfort Airport**

Located about 30 miles south of Tisdale, Melfort Airport offers additional options for charter flights with its 3,000-foot runway and basic services.

**Prince Albert (Glass Field) Airport**

Approximately 80 miles west, this airport is a regional hub with more extensive services and a longer runway, facilitating wider travel options.

**Nipawin Airport**

Just 40 miles northeast of Tisdale, Nipawin Airport provides another convenient point for charter flights into and out of northeastern Saskatchewan.

**Hudson Bay Airport**

About 100 miles north, offering services for regional flights and acting as a northern gateway for travelers and adventurers alike.
