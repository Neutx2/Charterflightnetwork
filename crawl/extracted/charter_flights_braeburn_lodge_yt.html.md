##### About Braeburn Lodge Yukon and Location

Braeburn Lodge, renowned for its gigantic cinnamon buns, is not just a pit stop on the Klondike Highway but a destination in itself. Nestled in the vast wilderness of Yukon, it serves as a gateway to unparalleled natural beauty and outdoor adventures. Accessible predominantly by charter flights, Braeburn Lodge offers an exclusive peek into the remote landscapes and culture of Yukon, making it a must-visit for those looking to explore the less trodden paths of Canada.

##### Braeburn Lodge Airport

The Braeburn Lodge airstrip, a compact runway of 1,800 feet, lies conveniently close to the lodge. Despite its modest size, it offers essential services including charter flight arrangements, making it an integral part of the adventure tourism in Yukon. Its proximity to Braeburn Lodge allows for quick transfers and an immediate immersion into the wilderness.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Erik Nielsen Whitehorse International Airport**

This major hub, with a runway of 9,500 feet, serves as the primary gateway to Yukon, offering comprehensive services and connectivity to various parts of Canada and beyond.

**Mayo Airport**

Located in the heart of Yukon, Mayo Airport, with a 4,000-foot runway, provides access to the central regions of the territory, supporting both tourism and local industries.

**Dawson City Airport**

As a key airport in the historic town of Dawson City, this airport offers a 5,000-foot runway and serves as a crucial link for tourists exploring the Klondike Gold Rush heritage sites.

**Old Crow Airport**

Accessible only by air, Old Crow Airport is vital for the remote community of Old Crow, offering a 4,500-foot runway and connecting the northernmost part of Yukon to the rest of the territory.
