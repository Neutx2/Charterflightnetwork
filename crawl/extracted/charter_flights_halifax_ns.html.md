About Halifax Stanfield International Airport Nova Scotia

The Halifax Stanfield International Airport is located several miles north of Halifax and is the 8th busiest in Canada handling an average of 3,660,000 passengers each year. The two Asphalt runways - the longest at 10,500 feet, can handle large jets. The airport serves as a hub for Air Canada Express, Cougar Helicopters, Maritime Air Charter and Provincial Airlines.

##### What to See:

**Halifax Citadel National Historic Site:** This historic fortification offers a glimpse into Halifax's military past. Visitors can explore the grounds, interact with costumed interpreters, and enjoy panoramic views of the city and harbor.
**Peggy's Cove:** Located just outside of Halifax, Peggy's Cove is a picturesque fishing village known for its iconic lighthouse and rugged coastal scenery. It's a popular spot for photography and exploring the quaint shops and galleries.
**Halifax Waterfront:**The waterfront area is bustling with activity, featuring shops, restaurants, museums, and attractions like the Maritime Museum of the Atlantic and the Canadian Museum of Immigration at Pier 21. Visitors can also take a stroll along the boardwalk or embark on a harbor cruise.
**Point Pleasant Park:** This sprawling urban park offers walking trails, picnic areas, and scenic views of the harbor. It's a great place to enjoy outdoor activities and escape the hustle and bustle of the city.
**Cultural Events:**
Halifax hosts a variety of cultural events and festivals throughout the year, including the Halifax International Busker Festival, Halifax Jazz Festival, and Halifax Fringe Festival, showcasing local and international talent.
**Outdoor Recreation:** From hiking and biking to kayaking and sailing, Halifax offers plenty of opportunities for outdoor enthusiasts to explore its natural beauty and scenic landscapes.
**Culinary Delights:**
Halifax is known for its vibrant food scene, with a wide range of restaurants, cafes, and eateries serving up delicious local cuisine, including fresh seafood and traditional Maritime dishes.
**Shopping:** The city is home to several shopping districts, including Spring Garden Road, Barrington Street, and Historic Properties, where visitors can browse boutiques, galleries, and markets for unique gifts and souvenirs.

Overall, Halifax offers a perfect blend of history, culture, and outdoor adventure, making it an ideal destination for travelers seeking to experience the best of the Maritimes.
