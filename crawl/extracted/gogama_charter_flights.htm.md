##### About Flights To Gogama

##### Location

Gogama is a small town located just off hwy 144 about half way between Sudbury and Timmins, Ontario. The area is well known for its exceptional hunting and fishing. Each year hundreds of sportsmen come to the area and take float plane charter flights to remote outpost camps and wilderness lodges located throughout the Gogama area. Not only will you discover dozens of great facilties, but you will have access to numerous air charter services that provide flights in and out of the various area lodges.

##### Gogama Airport

The closest airports to Gogama are Chapleau / Wawa / Sudbury and Timmins.

Air Ivanhoe is based 50 miles north of Gogama near Foleyet Ontario and provides float plane charter services as well as fly-in fishing trips throughout the region.

There is no airport in Gogama. We can suggest wheel plane flights to the Timmins Airport then ground transportation to Gogama which is a 1 hour 20 minute drive via Ontario hwy 144. We can set up flights to Timmins from virtually any airport throughout Canada or USA

You can fly float plane charter direct to Gogama from various departure points such as Wawa, Sudbury, Chapleau and even Toronto. Let us know how many passengers and gear, when you want to travel and where you want to fly from. We will source out for competitive quotes.

The closest helicopter charter service is located in Cochrane which is 100 air miles north. We have a large data base of helicopter charter services operating in Northern Ontario and can get you competitive quotes. Just tell us what you need.
