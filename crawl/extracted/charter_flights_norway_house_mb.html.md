## Flights to Norway House, Manitoba

### **About Norway House:**

Norway House Airport sits beside the community and offers practical access for fixed-wing charter traffic using its crushed-rock runway. The airport can accommodate many northern passenger and utility aircraft, making it a dependable option for regional travel, cargo movement, and time-sensitive charter trips into this northern Manitoba service centre.

##### Norway House Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* The airport is positioned right beside the community, so transfers after landing are generally short, and the crushed-rock runway supports many northern turboprop and utility aircraft.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Norway House functions as a major regional centre, so visitors can typically access practical local services, stores, fuel, and community facilities, with travel often organized through local hosts or business contacts.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Visitors are often drawn by the Norway House National Historic Site, nearby Rossville Mission, York Boat history, fishing waters, and the community's strong cultural and historical importance in northern Manitoba.
