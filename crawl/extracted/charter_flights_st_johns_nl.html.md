##### Top Attractions in St. John's:

**Signal Hill:** Explore historic Signal Hill National Historic Site, offering panoramic views of the city and the Atlantic Ocean. Don't miss the iconic Cabot Tower and the site's rich military history.
**The Battery:** Wander through the colorful streets of The Battery, a charming neighborhood steeped in maritime heritage and local culture.
**Cape Spear:** Visit Cape Spear, the easternmost point in North America, and marvel at the breathtaking coastal scenery.

##### Outdoor Adventures:

**Hiking Trails:** Discover scenic hiking trails like the East Coast Trail, offering stunning coastal vistas and opportunities for wildlife spotting.
**Whale Watching:** Embark on an unforgettable whale-watching tour from St. John's harbor, where you can witness majestic humpback whales and other marine life up close..

##### Accommodation and Dining:

From boutique hotels to cozy bed and breakfasts, St. John's offers a range of accommodations to suit every traveler's needs. Indulge in delicious cuisine at local restaurants and seafood eateries, showcasing the region's fresh seafood and culinary delights.
