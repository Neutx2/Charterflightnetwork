##### About Flights To Kingston, Ontario

##### Location

Kingston is located on the far north east shore of Lake Ontario at the mouth of the St Lawrence River. If you plan to travel to Kingston for business meetings or vacation, you can fly private charter flight direct to the Norman Rogers Airport located 5 miles west of downtown. The airport has a 4900 foot asphalt runway making it accessible to a wide range of charter and corporate aircraft.

##### Kingston Airport

If you require wheel plane charter service out of Kingston, the closest air charter service is 90 air miles west in Peterborough. President Air Charter operates a Beechcraft King Air and can fly to Kingston's Norman Rogers Airport, pick up your group and fly you to destinations across Ontario, throughout Canada as well as the USA. You can if you wish, depart in the morning, conduct your business and arrive back home that afternoon.

The Kingston Airport has two runways a 6,001 foot Asphalt and 3,909 foot Asphalt runway
