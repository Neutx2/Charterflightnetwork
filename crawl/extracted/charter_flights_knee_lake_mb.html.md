## Flights to Knee Lake, Manitoba

### **About Knee Lake:**

Knee Lake Airport is a private airstrip serving this remote lodge destination and is located about 1 nautical mile north of Knee Lake. Its 3,950-foot crushed-rock runway can support many practical charter turboprops and utility aircraft, giving guests direct fixed-wing access to one of northern Manitoba’s best-known fly-in fishing areas.

##### Knee Lake Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Knee Lake is accessible by air only, with a private 3,950-foot crushed-rock landing strip serving the lodge area. This setup allows direct charter arrivals from Winnipeg and other regional gateways without overland transfer requirements.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* North Star Resort offers modern lakefront cabins, dining in the main lodge, guides, boats, and on-site guest services tailored to fly-in anglers. This is a self-contained lodge destination rather than a town with general public lodging options.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Knee Lake is known for trophy northern pike, strong walleye fishing, and fly-out opportunities for lake and brook trout. Guests come for low fishing pressure, remote wilderness scenery, and exclusive access to the lake.

## Knee Lake Charter Flight FAQ

### **What airport serves Knee Lake Lodge, Manitoba?**

Knee Lake is served by Knee Lake Airport, a private airport identified by the codes YKE and CJT3. It supports direct charter arrivals for this remote lodge destination.

### **How long is the runway at Knee Lake?**

Knee Lake Airport has a 3,950-foot crushed-rock runway. That runway length is suitable for many practical charter turboprops and other utility aircraft used for fly-in lodge operations.

### **Is Knee Lake Lodge accessible only by air?**

Yes. Public lodge listings describe North Star Resort at Knee Lake as accessible by air only, with round-trip airfare from Winnipeg included in published package details.

### **What is Knee Lake known for?**

Knee Lake is best known for trophy northern pike, strong walleye fishing, and low fishing pressure. The resort also promotes fly-out opportunities for lake trout and brook trout.
