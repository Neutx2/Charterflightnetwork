##### About Flights To Savant Lake

##### Location

The town of Savant Lake is located in Northwestern Ontario along Highway 599 and is about half way between Ignace and Pickle Lake. The town is also near the junction of Highway 516 which connects with Sioux Lookout. Each year hundreds of sportsmen and vacationers come to the Savant Lake area to enjoy a remote wilderness experience. Many take a float plane charter flight further north to a secluded outpost or fishing lodge. Use the links on our adjacent map to search for accommodations, charter flight services, resorts and outfitters

##### Savant Lake Airport

The closest airport with scheduled or charter flights is located in Sioux Lookout which is a 1 .5 hour drive west via highway 516

The main base for Rusty Myers Flying Service is Fort Frances. For many years however, they have operated a satellite base out of Savant Lake which is located on hwy 599, 130 km or a 2 hour drive north of Ignace, Ontario.

#### Savant Lake Wheel Plane Charter Flights

There is no airport in Savant Lake. We can however arrange direct charter flights to Sioux Lookout. Upon arrival in Sioux Lookout you could connect with ground transportation for the 1 hour 30 minute drive to Savant Lake. We could also set up a float plane charter that could get you direct to one of the lodges on Savant Lake in about 30 minutes.

#### Savant Lake Float Plane Charter Flights

If you are driving highway 599 up to Savant Lake, you can connect with Rusty Myers Flying Service based just minutes from town. They specialize in fly-in fishing and hunting trips to remote outpost camps and lodges in the Savant Lake area. They can also provide general charter service or fly-out camping trips.

#### Savant Lake Helicopter Charter Flights

There is little demand for helicopter charter service out of Savant Lake. If you are developing a project in the area that requires helicopter charter support, let us know your requirements. We will source out to all companies operating in the regioin, hopefully resulting in a few competitive options for your consideration. Let us know your requirements.
