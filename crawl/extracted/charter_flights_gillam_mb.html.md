##### Gillam Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Charter flights to Gillam use Gillam Airport, YGX / CYGX, which features a gravel runway measuring about 5,034 feet. That runway length supports efficient northern charter access for business travel, crew movements, cargo, and regional transportation.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Gillam offers more services than many smaller northern communities. Travelers can usually find local lodging and basic trip-support amenities in town, making Gillam a practical overnight or staging point for work crews, regional passengers, and outdoor visitors moving through northeastern Manitoba.

**Things to Do and Local Attractions**

* Gillam is known more for outdoor access than for conventional tourism, but visitors can enjoy fishing, boating, and shoreline recreation in the area. Stephens Lake Municipal Park is a notable local recreation spot, and Gillam is also one of the northern access points used for travel toward York Factory.
