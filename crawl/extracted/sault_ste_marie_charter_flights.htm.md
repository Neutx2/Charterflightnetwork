##### About Flights To Sault Ste Marie

##### Location

Sault Ste Marie is located in Northern Ontario on the far eastern shores of Lake Superior and shares the canada / USA border with Sault Ste Marie Michigan. Because of its close proximity to the USA, the city is a popular destination for thousands of US sportsmen and tourists each year who come to Northern Ontario to experience a true wilderness experience. The city is served by scheduled airlines, however business and vacation travelers can also arrange direct charter flights to Sault Ste Marie as well.

##### Sault Ste Marie Airport

The Sault Ste Marie Airport is an airport of entry with customs services provided by the Canadian Border Services Agency. The airport features two 6000 ft ansphalt runways making it accessible to all air charter aricraft in the region.

##### Air Charter Options

If you are planning a remote fly-in fishing adventure in Northern Ontario, you can eliminate a full days driving by taking a direct charter flight from Sault Ste Marie to Hearst, Nakina, Wawa where you will connect with your float plane service for direct flight to the lodge or outpost camp. Charter Flights are also available from US Airports to remote Canadian Adventure Destinations. #### Sault Ste Marie Wheel Plane Charter Flights

There is no wheel plane charter operation based in Sault Ste Marie. When we receive quote requests for Sault Ste Marie Charter flights we need to source out to airlines in Sudbury or Wawa. We can however get you competitive quotes for charter flights toSault Ste Marie from any departure point across Canada.

#### Sault Ste Marie Float Plane Charter Flights

There are no float plane charter services based in Sault Ste Marie. The closest float plane charter services are located in Blind River which is a 1 hour and 40 minute drive east via Hwy 17 / Elliot Lake wich is 2 hour and 20 minute drive east via hwy 17 or Wawa which is a 2 hour and 35 minute drive north via Hwy 17. We can get you quotes from all departure points.
