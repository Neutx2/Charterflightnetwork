##### About Flights To Temagami

##### Location

Each year hundreds of tourists visit the remote Temagami area of Northern Ontario. The region is conveniently located just a short drive north of North Bay via Highway 11. There are numerous resorts and fishing lodges located throughout the area which are accessible by road or boat. However, many of these visitors are sportsmen who take float plane charter flights to the dozens of remote wilderness lakes and rivers throughout the area, for a week of spectacular fishing.

##### Temagami Airport

There is no airport located in Temagami. The closest airport would be the Earlton Timiskaming Airport located 1 hour north via highway 11.

From their base in Temagami Ontario, Lakeland Airways offers float plane charter service to a number of remote lakes and rivers throughout Northern Ontario, plus fly-in fishing and hunting packages to a select group of remote outpost camps featuring exceptional fishing for Walleyes, Northerns, Bass and Speckled Trout. Cochrane Ontario located 150 miles north of Temagami is the closest full time helicopter charter base. If you need helicopter support in the Temagami region, we will source out to our data base of operators in Northern Ontario and find you a few competitive options for your consideration
