##### About Flights To Moosonee

##### Location

Moosonee is located on the southern shores of James Bay on the Moose River. The only access to this community is by rail or air. The community is served by scheduled flights, but you can also arrange charter flights to Moosonee out of Timmins or other airports in Northern Ontario. One of the more unique travel alternatives to a charter flight is an exciting train ride to Moosonee called the Polar Bear Express. Use our featured air charter links to request a no obligation Moosonee charter quote

##### Moosonee Airport

Thunder Air and Air Creebec provide flights to Moosonee Airport which is located 2.5 miles north east of the community and features a 4,000 foot asphalt runway as well as a 3,500 foot gravel / asphalt runway.
