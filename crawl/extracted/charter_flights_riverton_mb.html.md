##### Riverton Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Charter flights to Riverton use Riverton Airport, CKG2, a registered public aerodrome with an elevation of 725 feet. Current aerodrome data lists a 3,000 x 100 runway with grass, gravel, and snow operating surfaces.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Riverton has practical lodging for short stays, with the Riverton Motor Inn as a verified in-town option. Municipal accommodation listings also point travelers to nearby choices such as Zan's Family Inn, Travelling Inn, and the Arborg Hotel.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Visitors to Riverton can enjoy Sandy Bar Beach, see Lundi the Moose, and explore heritage displays in the restored train station museum. These attractions make Riverton a worthwhile stop for both leisure visits and regional travel.

Riverton is served by Riverton Airport, ICAO code CKG2. Aerodrome data lists it as a registered public airport south of town.

Current aerodrome data lists a runway measuring about 3,000 feet by 100 feet with grass, gravel, and snow operating surfaces.
