##### About Points North Saskatchewan and Location

Points North, Saskatchewan, stands as a beacon for those seeking the serenity and adventure offered by the province's remote northern destinations. With charter flights, accessing Points North has never been easier, opening doors to unique attractions, thrilling activities, and compelling reasons for visiting. From untouched wilderness to breathtaking northern lights, Points North is a must-visit for adventurers and nature lovers.

##### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Points North to Saskatoon**

The journey from Points North to Saskatoon is 435 air miles, via charter flights, and brings travelers from the remote beauties of the north to the vibrant city life of Saskatoon, connecting two distinct worlds within Saskatchewan.

**Points North to Regina**

Charter flights from Points North to Regina covers a distance of 540 air miles and offer a seamless connection to Saskatchewan's capital, highlighting the convenience and efficiency of air travel in exploring the province's vast landscapes.

**Points North to La Ronge**

The distance from Points North to La Ronge is 225 air miles and is a testament to the expansive wilderness of Saskatchewan, easily navigated through the flexibility of charter flights, linking remote regions with key northern cities.

**Points North to Yorkton**

Traveling from Points North to Yorkton by charter flight covers 500 air miles and showcases the diverse geographical features of Saskatchewan, from its northern frontiers to its agricultural heartlands.

##### Points North Airport

Points North Airport is a public use airport located adjacent to the town. The single runway is 5,848 feet in length with a gravel surface and serves as a vital gateway to the northern reaches of Saskatchewan. With a runway accommodating a variety of aircraft, the airport is conveniently located, offering services essential for travelers using charter flights to explore the province's remote destinations.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Saskatoon John G. Diefenbaker International Airport**

As a major hub in Saskatchewan, this airport offers comprehensive services and a strategic location for travelers looking to explore the region further.

**Regina International Airport**

Providing a wide range of services, Regina's airport is a key entry point for those venturing into Saskatchewan's southern and central areas.

**La Ronge (Barber Field) Airport**

This airport is an essential stop for accessing the northern wilderness, offering services that cater to adventurers and explorers alike.

**Yorkton Municipal Airport**

Yorkton's airport facilitates access to the eastern parts of Saskatchewan, providing essential services for regional travel.
