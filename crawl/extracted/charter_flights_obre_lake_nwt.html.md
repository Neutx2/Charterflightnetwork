##### About Obre Lake Northwest Territories and Location

Obre Lake, a serene and untouched gem, is located in the heart of the Northwest Territories. Known for its breathtaking landscapes, rich wildlife, and unique cultural heritage, Obre Lake is a paradise for adventurers and nature lovers. The area offers a multitude of activities ranging from fishing, kayaking, to viewing the majestic northern lights. Its remote location makes charter flights an ideal mode of transportation, offering an exclusive and memorable journey to this secluded haven.

##### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Obre Lake to Yellowknife**

The distance from Obre Lake to Yellowknife is approximately 200 miles (321 kilometers), making charter flights a convenient option for travel.

**Obre Lake to Edmonton**

Traveling from Obre Lake to Edmonton covers a distance of about 800 miles (1287 kilometers), highlighting the importance of charter flights for efficient and comfortable access.

**Obre Lake to Fort Resolution**

Obre Lake is roughly 150 miles (241 kilometers) from Fort Resolution, a journey easily bridged by the convenience of charter flights.

**Obre Lake to Fort Simpson**

Connecting Obre Lake to Fort Simpson, the distance is around 250 miles (402 kilometers), with charter flights offering a direct and scenic route.

##### Obre Lake Airport

The Obre Lake Airport serves as a gateway to this stunning region. The runway stretches over 6,000 feet, accommodating various types of aircraft certified for a sand / gravel runway. Located just a few miles from the town, it provides easy access to Obre Lake's natural wonders. The airport boasts essential services for travelers, ensuring a smooth and welcoming arrival to or departure from Obre Lake.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Tulita Airport**

Located in the scenic town of Tulita, this airport features a 1,200-meter runway. It offers basic services, making it a vital hub for accessing the surrounding wilderness.

**Fort Smith Airport**

With a longer runway of 1,800 meters, Fort Smith Airport supports larger aircraft, providing comprehensive services for travelers exploring the Northwest Territories.

**Hay River Airport**

This airport is a crucial link to the South of the Northwest Territories. Its 2,000-meter runway and full-service terminal cater to a wide range of travel needs.

**Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport**

A gateway to the Arctic, this airport has a 1,800-meter runway and offers extensive services, including charter flights to remote locations.

##### Obre Lake Hotels / Accommodations

**The Obre Lake Lodge**

Offering cozy accommodations and stunning lake views, the Obre Lake Lodge is a haven for those seeking tranquility and adventure in the heart of nature. Guests can enjoy fishing, hiking, and aurora viewing.

**Wilderness Cabins**

For a more secluded experience, the Wilderness Cabins around Obre Lake provide a rustic yet comfortable stay, ideal for connecting with nature and enjoying outdoor activities.

##### Obre Lake Outdoor Adventures

The area around Obre Lake is renowned for its outdoor adventures. Whether it's the thrill of fishing in pristine waters, hunting in the vast wilderness, kayaking through serene lakes, or embarking on a guided tour to witness the northern lights, Obre Lake has something for everyone. Local companies like Obre Lake Adventures and Northern Lights Outfitters offer personalized experiences to explore the natural beauty of the Northwest Territories, making it a must-visit destination for those seeking adventure and serenity.
