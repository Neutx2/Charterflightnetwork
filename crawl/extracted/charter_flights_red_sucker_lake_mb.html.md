### **About Red Sucker Lake, Manitoba**

Red Sucker Lake is a remote northern Manitoba community located on the north side of the lake of the same name. The area is part of Manitoba's boreal region and is reached primarily by air, making charter flights an important option for passengers, freight, and time-sensitive travel.

##### Red Sucker Lake Services / Accommodations / Contacts

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Charter flights use Red Sucker Lake Airport, YRS / CYRS, which serves the community with a runway of roughly 3,500 feet. Because permanent road access is limited, the airport remains one of the most important transportation links for passengers, supplies, and essential travel.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Accommodation options in Red Sucker Lake itself appear limited, so some travelers may need to coordinate lodging in nearby regional communities when planning multi-day itineraries. Local services include basic retail, community facilities, and practical services that support short-stay northern travel.

**Things to Do and Local Attractions**

* Red Sucker Lake is best suited to visitors interested in northern landscapes, surrounding waterways, and remote community access rather than formal tourism infrastructure. Its appeal is tied to the boreal setting, local culture, and access to wilderness-oriented travel throughout the wider region.
