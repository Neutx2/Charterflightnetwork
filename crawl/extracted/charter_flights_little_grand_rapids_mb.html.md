### **About Little Grand Rapids:**

Little Grand Rapids Airport is located on the edge of the community and serves as the main fixed-wing gateway for this remote area. Its roughly 2,800-foot crushed-rock runway can handle many northern utility aircraft, including turboprops used for passenger travel, freight service, and essential year-round access into both Little Grand Rapids and nearby Pauingassi.

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Little Grand Rapids Airport is on the edge of the community and offers practical year-round air access. The crushed-rock runway supports many northern turboprop and utility aircraft used for passenger, cargo, and charter service.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Visitor services are limited and should be confirmed before departure. Community information identifies two local stores, a nursing station, RCMP presence, and other essential services, but travelers should not assume hotel-style accommodation is available.

## Little Grand Rapids Charter Flight FAQ

### **What airport serves Little Grand Rapids, Manitoba?**

Little Grand Rapids is served by Little Grand Rapids Airport, using the codes ZGR and CZGR. It is the main airport for Little Grand Rapids and also supports nearby Pauingassi First Nation.

### **What type of runway does Little Grand Rapids Airport have?**

The airport has a crushed-rock runway measuring about 2,800 feet. That runway is suitable for many northern turboprop and utility aircraft used for passenger travel, cargo service, and community resupply.

### **How close is the airport to the community?**

Little Grand Rapids Airport is located on the edge of the community, so local transfers after landing are short and practical for passengers, freight, and essential travel movements.

### **Why is air service so important to Little Grand Rapids?**

Air service is critical because the community depends heavily on the airport for year-round travel and cargo, while winter road access is available only for a short seasonal period.
