###### If you have booked a Cabot Links Golf Vacation and you want to choose a transportation option that minimizes travel time and maximizes time on the course. This travel option is the most cost efficient and requires the least amount of travel time

In 2024, Air Canada is offering a non stop flight that departs Toronto at 2:15 pm and arrives Sydney Nova Scotia at 5:38 pm local time.

Return flight to Toronto, departs Sydney at 6:40 pm in the evening and arrives in Toronto at 8:30 pm

Sydney has a population of 31,000 and is located on the north eastern tip of Cape Breton Island. Sydney is one of the most popular destinations or jumping off points for tourists visiting the Cape Breton region. Sydney's local J A Douglas McCurdy Airport is the primary airport and transportation center for Cape Breton and handles an average of 180,000 passengers each year. The airport has two asphalt runways - 7,000 feet and 6,000 feet, making it accessible to all corporate jet aircraft as well. Gateway Aviation is the local full service FBO.

Whether you are flying commercial airline from Canada or the USA, all flights to Sydney will first stop in Montreal, Quebec, where passengers will take a connecting Air Canada flight to Sydney.

#### Upon Arrival In Sydney, You Have 3 Travel Options To Cabot Cape Breton

##### Travel Option #1 - Helicopter

The helicopters are operated by Breton Air, and will seat up to 4 passengers with light baggage and clubs. In order to accommodate passengers, baggage and clubs, only soft bags are accepted, not the hard shell travel cases.

The Cabot Golf Resort is 50 air miles west of Sydney with an average flight time of 20 minutes.

You also have the option of taking the helicopter to Cabot Cape Breton on arrival day and then taking the passenger van service back to Sydney on your departure day.

---

###### Round Trip Helicopter Flight

The round trip cost for the helicopter is $8,740 Canadian funds including the 15% tax and fees.

This will equal between $6,100 - $6,300 in US funds depending on the exchange rate at the time

###### Helicopter In / Passenger Van Out

The round trip cost for this combination service, Helicopter In, Passenger Van Out is $5,205.00 Canadian funds including the 15% tax and fees.

This will equal between $3,650 - $3,770 in US funds depending on the exchange rate at the time

---

##### Travel Option #2 - Passenger Van Shuttle

Breton Air also offers round trip Passenger Van Service between Sydney and Cabot Cape Breton for golfer looking for a more cost efficient travel option vs the helicopter charter. Also, it is good to know that you have an "Option B" available in the event that weather conditions prevent the helicopter from operating.

Drive time from Sydney to the Cabot Golf Resort averages 2 hours.

###### Passenger Van Shuttle

The round trip cost for the Passenger Van Shuttle is $1,975 Canadian funds including the 15% tax and fees.

This will equal between $1,390 - $1,431 in US funds depending on the exchange rate at the time
