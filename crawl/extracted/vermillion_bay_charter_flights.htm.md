##### About Flights To Vermillion Bay

##### Location

Vermillion Bay is a picturesque tourist town located on the north end of Eagle Lake and just 20 minutes west of Dryden on Highway 17. Vermillion bay is also located at the junction of Highway 105 which heads north to Red Lake. The Eagle Lake area is famous for its exceptional Musky fishing and attracts hundreds of sportsmen each year. Many hunters and fishermen who come to the area arrange to take a float plane charter flight out of Vermillion Bay to remote lakes and rivers further north - where they can enjoy an exceptional week of wilderness fishing.

##### Vermillion Bay Airport

Dryden is the closest airport with scheduled flight services.

From their float plane base in Vermillion Bay, Wilderness Air Escapes provides fly-in fishing trips to 15 outposts as well as dozens of seldom fished lakes throughout Northwestern Ontario. In addition, Clark's Air Service float plane fleet includes a Cessna 185, three DeHavilland Beavers and a DeHavilland Otter all based at the Northern Lights Resort located 25 miles north of Vermillion Bay. #### Vermillion Bay Wheel Plane Charter Flights

If you are heading to Vermillion Bay and plan on taking a wheel plane charter, your best option is to arrive in nearby Dryden, Ontario and take ground transportation for the 40 minute drive to Vermillion Bay. You can stop in Dryden to pick up groceries or supplies if needed.

#### Vermillion Bay Float Plane Charter Flights

Vermillion Bay is home to Wilderness Air Escapes. They operate a first class float plane air charter service as well as several remote, fly-in outpost camps. They also provide float plane service to many other lodges. Let us know what you need and we will get you the quotes to make the best decision.

#### Vermillion Bay Helicopter Charter Flights

There are no helicopter charter services in Vermillion Bay, but the Kenora Airport is just 45 air miles west and home to Forest Helicopters. If you need helicopter support in the Vermillion Bay area we can put you in touch with Forest Helicopters which would be your most cost efficient option.
