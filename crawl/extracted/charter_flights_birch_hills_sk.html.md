##### About Birch Hills, Saskatchewan and Location

Birch Hills, nestled in the picturesque landscape of Saskatchewan, offers visitors a unique blend of natural beauty, historical significance, and recreational activities. Its strategic location makes it an ideal spot for charter flights, providing easy access to Saskatchewan's remote and beautiful destinations for both leisure and business travelers.

##### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Birch Hills to La Ronge**

Approximately 145 air miles. A quick charter flight takes you over breathtaking northern landscapes, connecting you with the wilderness.

**Birch Hills to Regina**

About 180 air miles. Enjoy the convenience of charter flights from Birch Hills to the capital, showcasing the vast beauty of Saskatchewan.

**Birch Hills to Saskatoon**

Just 80 air miles to the province's largest city, offering a swift journey through the heart of Saskatchewan.

##### Birch Hills Airport

The town of Birch Hills Sk has a public use airport located adjacent to the town. The main runway is 2,660 feet in length with an asphalt surface - making it accessible to most small to medium size charter aircraft. Contact the charter companies on this page for details.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Prince Albert (Glass Field) Airport**

Located about 30 miles west of Birch Hills, offering additional services and longer runways to accommodate larger aircraft and more destinations.

**Saskatoon John G. Diefenbaker International Airport**

Approximately 90 miles south, it's a major hub for international and charter flights, providing extensive services to travelers.

**Melfort Airport**

Just 40 miles east, Melfort Airport serves as another option for charter flights in and out of the region, with essential services for pilots and passengers.

**Nipawin Airport**

Around 70 miles northeast, Nipawin Airport offers accessibility to the eastern parts of the province, with facilities for charter flights and general aviation.
