##### Watrous Airport

Watrous is located in South Central Saskatchewan and has a public use airport located 5 miles east of town. The airport offers 3 runways, however two are grass strips and the third has a clay/gravel surface. The longest runway is 3,300 feet and is grass. Charter operations would be very limited to the types of aircraft that can access this airport. Contact the charter operators listed on this page for details on the types of aircraft available for charter flights to the Watrous Airport.
