## Flights to Big Sand Lake Lodge

### **About Big Sand Lake:**

Big Sand Lake Lodge is a true fly-in destination with its own private 3,450-foot landing strip, allowing guests to arrive directly by chartered turboprop aircraft. Because there are no road connections, the airstrip is central to lodge operations and can accommodate practical northern charter aircraft used for passenger service into remote fishing country.

##### Big Sand Lake Lodge Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Big Sand Lake Lodge is reached by air and uses its own private landing strip. Guests typically arrive by chartered twin turboprop aircraft from Winnipeg, making the lodge a practical option for efficient access into a remote part of northern Manitoba.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Big Sand Lake Lodge operates as a full-service fly-in fishing and hunting destination with guest cabins, meals, guides, boats, and on-site trip support. Unlike a community destination, the lodge itself provides the main accommodations and core visitor services.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Big Sand Lake is designed for anglers and wilderness travelers seeking trophy northern pike, lake trout, walleye, and Arctic grayling. The setting also appeals to guests interested in fly-in hunting, scenic wilderness, and a true remote northern adventure.

### Does Big Sand Lake Lodge have its own landing strip?

Yes. Big Sand Lake Lodge uses its own private airstrip, which supports the charter aircraft used to bring guests directly into this remote northern Manitoba fishing destination.
