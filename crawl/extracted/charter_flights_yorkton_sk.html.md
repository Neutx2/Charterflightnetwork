##### About Yorkton, Saskatchewan and Location

Yorkton, a vibrant city nestled in the heart of Saskatchewan, is renowned for its rich cultural heritage and breathtaking natural landscapes. This dynamic city offers a plethora of attractions, activities, and events that cater to visitors of all ages. From its historical sites and art galleries to lush parks and recreational facilities, Yorkton is an ideal destination for those seeking adventure and relaxation. Accessible via charter flights, Yorkton serves as a gateway to exploring the untapped beauty of remote Saskatchewan destinations.

##### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Yorkton to La Ronge**

The air distance from Yorkton to La Ronge is approximately 290 air miles (470 km), offering an aerial perspective of Saskatchewan's diverse landscapes.

**Yorkton to Regina**

Connecting Yorkton with the provincial capital, Regina, spans about 115 air miles (180 km), facilitating easy access to central Saskatchewan.

**Yorkton to Saskatoon**

The journey from Yorkton to Saskatoon covers roughly 190 air miles (310 km), linking the city to Saskatchewan's bustling urban centers.

**Yorkton to Stony Rapids**

Traveling from Yorkton to Stony Rapids is approximately 570 air miles (915 km), extending to the northern reaches of Saskatchewan.

##### Yorkton Airport

Yorkton has a public use airport located 3 miles north of town with a 4,800 foot asphalt runway - making it accessible to a very wide range of charter aircraft types. Airport is a critical asset to the city and the surrounding region.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Melville Municipal Airport**

Just a short drive from Yorkton, Melville Municipal Airport offers additional aviation services, supporting the region's access to charter flights and general aviation.

**Regina International Airport**

As a major hub in southern Saskatchewan, Regina International Airport provides comprehensive services, including international and domestic flights, making it a pivotal point for travelers.

**Saskatoon John G. Diefenbaker International Airport**

Serving as a gateway to northern Saskatchewan, Saskatoon's airport connects the region with the rest of Canada and beyond, offering a wide range of passenger services.

**Prince Albert (Glass Field) Airport**

Located in Prince Albert, this airport facilitates access to the northern parts of the province, with facilities for charter flights and general aviation.

##### Yorkton Hotels / Accommodations

**Home Inn & Suites Yorkton**

This hotel offers guests a comfortable stay with modern amenities, including an indoor pool and spa, conveniently located near Yorkton's top attractions.

**Days Inn by Wyndham Yorkton**

Days Inn provides affordable accommodations with easy access to dining, shopping, and the beauty of Yorkton, ensuring a pleasant stay for all visitors.

##### Yorkton Outdoor Adventures

**Duck Mountain Provincial Park**

Experience the great outdoors at Duck Mountain Provincial Park, with activities ranging from hiking and camping to fishing and wildlife observation, all within a short drive from Yorkton.

**Golfing:**

Yorkton boasts several golf courses where enthusiasts can enjoy a round in scenic surroundings. Deer Park Municipal Golf Course and York Lake Regional Park Golf Course are among the local favorites.
