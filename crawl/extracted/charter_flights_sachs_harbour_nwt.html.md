##### About Sachs Harbour Northwest Territories and Location

Sachs Harbour, located on Banks Island in the Northwest Territories, is a remote community known for its unique Arctic environment and wildlife. It is a haven for adventurers seeking to explore the untouched wilderness of the Arctic, offering opportunities to witness polar bears, muskoxen, and a variety of migratory birds. The community's rich Inuvialuit culture and the spectacular phenomenon of the northern lights make Sachs Harbour a must-visit destination for those looking to experience the beauty and solitude of the North.

##### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Sachs Harbour to Yellowknife**

The journey from Sachs Harbour to Yellowknife covers approximately 1,150 miles (1,850 km), connecting the Arctic community with the capital of the Northwest Territories.

**Sachs Harbour to Edmonton**

Traveling from Sachs Harbour to Edmonton spans about 1,675 miles (2,695 km), illustrating the vast distances between this northern outpost and major urban centers.

**Sachs Harbour to Fort Resolution**

The distance from Sachs Harbour to Fort Resolution is roughly 1,300 miles (2,092 km), bridging the Arctic wilderness with the southern parts of the Northwest Territories.

**Sachs Harbour to Fort Simpson**

Connecting Sachs Harbour with Fort Simpson involves a distance of around 1,400 miles (2,253 km), showcasing the remote nature of Sachs Harbour within the territory.

##### Sachs Harbour Airport

Sachs Harbour Airport, with a runway length of 3,937 feet, is crucial for the community's connection to the rest of the Northwest Territories and beyond. Located a short distance from the town, it provides essential services including passenger and cargo flights, supporting both the residents and the influx of tourists seeking the unique experiences that Sachs Harbour has to offer.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport**

Located in Inuvik, this airport offers a longer runway and a range of services, acting as a gateway to the western Arctic and facilitating travel within the Northwest Territories.

**Tuktoyaktuk James Gruben Airport**

This airport serves the coastal community of Tuktoyaktuk, providing access to the Arctic Ocean and supporting the region with essential air services.

**Ulukhaktok Holman Airport**

Ulukhaktok Airport, serving the community of Ulukhaktok on Victoria Island, offers another point of access to the unique culture and landscapes of the Arctic region.

**Paulatuk Airport**

Paulatuk Airport supports the coastal community of Paulatuk, facilitating access to the northern coast of the mainland and the Pingo Canadian Landmark.

##### Sachs Harbour Hotels / Accommodations

**Arctic Char Inn**

The Arctic Char Inn offers cozy and comfortable lodging in Sachs Harbour, providing guests with a warm place to stay in the cold Arctic climate, along with traditional and locally sourced meals.

**Iceberg Suites**

Located within Sachs Harbour, Iceberg Suites offer modern accommodations with stunning views of the surrounding Arctic landscape, making it an ideal base for exploring the area.

##### Sachs Harbour Outdoor Adventures

Sachs Harbour is the ultimate destination for outdoor enthusiasts, offering unparalleled opportunities for wildlife viewing, ice fishing, kayaking among icebergs, and exploring the vast tundra. Local outfitters such as Arctic Wilderness Adventures provide guided tours, ensuring visitors can safely experience the best of Sachs Harbour's natural attractions, from the polar night in winter to the midnight sun in summer.
