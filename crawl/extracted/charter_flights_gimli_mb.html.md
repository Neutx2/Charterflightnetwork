## Gimli Charter Flight FAQ

### **What airport serves Gimli, Manitoba?**

Gimli is served by Gimli Industrial Park Airport, using the codes YGM and CYGM. It is a public airport used for charter flights, training, helicopter operations, and regional aviation services.

### **How far is Gimli Industrial Park Airport from town?**

The airport is located about 2 nautical miles west of Gimli, which makes it a practical airport for charter passengers heading to the town, harbour, beaches, marina, and nearby Lake Winnipeg destinations.

### **What type of runway does Gimli Industrial Park Airport have?**

Gimli Industrial Park Airport has one active asphalt runway measuring about 6,800 feet. That gives it the runway strength and length to support a broad range of charter aircraft types.

### **Are helicopter charter services available in Gimli?**

Yes. Prairie Helicopters is based in Gimli and operates Bell 212, Bell 427, Bell 407, Bell 206L, and Bell 206B helicopters for charter and related aviation work.
