##### About Flights To Kashechewan

##### Location

Kashechewan is a remote and isolated settlement located near the south western shores of James Bay in Northern Ontario. You can book scheduled as well as charter flights to Kashechewan from several airports across Northern Ontario such as Timmins, Thunder Bay and Pickle Lake.
The community of Kashechewan is located across the river from Fort Albany which at one time was the principal residence of the area until flooding forced evacuation of the community

Timmins Ontario is the closest air charter base to Kashechewan and is located 260 air miles south of the community. You can receive quotes for a 9 passenger King Air out of Timmins. We can also source out quotes out of Sudbury using Pilatus PC12 aircraft.

##### Kashechewan Airport

The Kashechewan Airport is located less than a mile west of the community and features a 3,900 gravel runway.
