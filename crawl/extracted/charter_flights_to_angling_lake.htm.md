##### About Flights To Angling Lake

##### Location

Angling Lake which is also known as Wapekkea is an isolated settlement in Northwestern Ontario located just north East of Big Trout Lake. The only access is by air and you can arrange private charter flights to Angling Lake Airport. Charter flights to Angling Lake are restricted to aircraft approved for gravel strip operations which includes many single engine and twin engine passenger and cargo aircraft.

It is 380 air miles from Thunder Bay to Angling Lake / 285 air miles from Nakina and 170 air miles from Pickle Lake. No matter which departure point you choose, you can receive up to 3 competitive quotes using our Free Charter Quote Request Service.

##### Angling Lake / Wapekeka Airport

Angling Lake / Wapekeka has a 3600 foot gravel runway located about a mile and a half west of the settlement.
