##### About Fort Selkirk Yukon and Location

Fort Selkirk, Yukon, is a historical gem nestled in the vast wilderness of Canada's Yukon Territory. Renowned for its pristine landscapes, Fort Selkirk offers a plethora of attractions and activities such as kayaking, fishing, and hiking. Charter flights to Fort Selkirk provide unparalleled access to this remote destination, making it a must-visit for adventurers and history enthusiasts alike.

##### Fort Selkirk Airport

Fort Selkirk Airport features a 2,000-foot runway, catering to small charter flights and connecting the town with the rest of Yukon and beyond. Located just a few miles from the town, it offers basic services for travelers and pilots, including refueling and parking.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Dawson City Airport**

Located in Dawson City, this airport has a 5,000-foot runway and provides services such as refueling and maintenance for aircraft.

**Mayo Airport**

In Mayo, Yukon, the airport features a 4,000-foot runway and supports both scheduled and charter flights, with essential services available.

**Old Crow Airport**

With a 4,500-foot runway, Old Crow Airport is vital for connecting this remote community to the rest of Yukon, offering scheduled and charter flight services.

**Carmacks Airport**

Carmacks Airport, though smaller with a 3,000-foot runway, plays a crucial role in regional connectivity and supports charter flights.
