About Fox Harbour Nova Scotia - Wheel Plane Access

Fox Harbour is located in northwest Nova Scotia near Tatamagouche and on the south shores of Northumberland Strait. The Airport is a private airport and is part of the Fox Harb'r Resort. Guests of the resort who wish to fly direct can charter an aircraft or fly their private aircraft to the airport with prior arrangements. With a 4,885 Asphalt runway the airport can accommodate medium size business jets
