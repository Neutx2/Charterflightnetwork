##### [Exodus Charters](https://exodusstlucia.com/)

Exodus Charters is a reputable yacht charter company based in St. Lucia, offering a range of sailing experiences in the Caribbean. Here are some details about Exodus Charters:

###### Location:

Exodus Charters operates from Rodney Bay Marina in Gros Islet, St. Lucia. This marina is strategically located in a well-protected bay on the northwest coast of the island, offering easy access to the Caribbean Sea and nearby sailing destinations.

###### Destinations:

With Exodus Charters, guests can embark on sailing adventures around St. Lucia and its neighboring islands.

###### Types of Boats:

Exodus Charters provides a fleet of well-maintained sailing vessels, including catamarans and monohull sailboats. These boats vary in size and accommodation options, catering to different group sizes and preferences. Whether you're looking for a cozy couples' getaway or a spacious vessel for a family vacation, Exodus Charters has options to suit your needs.

###### Bareboat Charters:

Experienced sailors can opt for bareboat charters, where they have the freedom to captain the vessel themselves. These charters are ideal for those with sailing experience who want to explore the Caribbean at their own pace.
