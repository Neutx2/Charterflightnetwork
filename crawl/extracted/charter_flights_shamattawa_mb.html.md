**Airport and Flight Access**

* Shamattawa Airport is adjacent to the community, so ground transfers after arrival are short. Its 4,006-foot gravel runway supports many northern turboprop and utility aircraft used for charter, cargo, and passenger service.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Visitor services are limited and should be confirmed before departure. The community has health and support services, but travelers should not assume hotel-style accommodation is available unless it has been arranged in advance.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Shamattawa appeals to travelers interested in remote northern rivers, wilderness scenery, boating, fishing, hunting, and community visits. It is more of a practical northern destination than a conventional tourism centre.

## Shamattawa Charter Flight FAQ

### **What airport serves Shamattawa, Manitoba?**

Shamattawa is served by Shamattawa Airport, using the codes ZTM and CZTM. It is a public airport located adjacent to the community and used for passenger, cargo, and charter traffic.

### **What type of runway does Shamattawa Airport have?**

Shamattawa Airport has a gravel runway measuring about 4,006 feet. That runway supports many northern turboprop and utility aircraft used for remote community access and resupply.

### **Is Shamattawa accessible by road year-round?**

No. Shamattawa is mainly accessed by air, with winter road access available only seasonally when conditions allow. That makes the airport especially important for year-round travel and freight.

### **How close is the airport to the community?**

The airport is adjacent to Shamattawa, so transfers after landing are short and practical for passengers, cargo, and essential service trips into the community.
