##### About Flights To Kasabonika

##### Location

Numerous airlines provide charter flights to Kasabonika which is a remote First Nations Community located in NorthWestern Ontario, 360 air miles north of Thunder Bay / 250 air miles north of Nakina and 230 air miles north of Armstrong. You can request wheel plane, float plane or helicopter charter flights direct o Kasabonika from any departure point across Ontario or throughout Canada

##### Kasabonika Airport

The Kasabonika Airport is located just over a mile west of the community and features a 3,500 foot gravel runway. Wasaya Airways offers scheduled flights to Kasabonika from Sioux Lookout.

#### Kasabonika Wheel Plane Charter Flights

Your most cost efficient departure point for a wheel plane charter to Kasabonika is Pickle Lake which is located 160 air miles south. We can however, get you competitive quotes for charter flight departures out of Thunder Bay, Nakina, Armstrong, Sioux Lookout or any departure point you choose.

#### Kasabonika Float Plane Charter Flights

If you need a float plane such as a Beaver or Otter, based in Kasabonika for a few days for Spring or Fall set outs for fishing, moose or goose hunting, let us know. We will source out to all float plane charter airlines in the region to find you a selection of competitive pricing and availability.

#### Kasabonika Helicopter Charter Flights

Pickle Lake is your best option for finding Helicopter support for any project in the Kasabonika area. There would be a bit of logistics involved as a fuel cache would need to be established ahead of time and pilot and support crew accommodations arranged. Let us know ahead of time if we can be of assistance.
