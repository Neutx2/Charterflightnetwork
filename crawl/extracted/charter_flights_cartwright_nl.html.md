Cartwright is a small coastal community located on the southern coast of Labrador, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada.

##### Location:

Cartwright is situated on Sandwich Bay, which is part of the Labrador Sea. It is located approximately 400 kilometers southeast of Happy Valley-Goose Bay, one of the larger towns in Labrador.

##### History:

Cartwright has a long history of Indigenous occupation, with the Inuit and Innu people traditionally inhabiting the region. European contact in the area began in the 18th century with the arrival of European fur traders and missionaries. Cartwright was named after British explorer John Cartwright, who explored the area in the late 18th century.

##### Economy:

The economy of Cartwright is primarily based on fishing, hunting, and trapping, with residents relying on traditional activities for sustenance and income. Commercial fishing, particularly for species such as cod, salmon, and Arctic char, is an important economic activity in the area.

##### Transportation:

Cartwright is accessible primarily by boat or by air, as there are no roads connecting it to other communities. The town has a small airstrip that provides regular air service to other parts of Labrador and Newfoundland. Additionally, Cartwright has a wharf that serves as a transportation link for marine traffic.

##### Community Life:

Cartwright has a close-knit community with a strong sense of cultural identity and pride. Residents often participate in community events and gatherings, including traditional ceremonies, festivals, and celebrations. The town's cultural heritage is preserved through storytelling, music, and other cultural practices.

##### Services and Amenities:

Despite its small size and remote location, Cartwright offers basic services and amenities to its residents. These include schools, healthcare facilities, a community center, and a local store. The town is also served by government services and agencies.

##### Outdoor Recreation:

Cartwright is surrounded by stunning natural landscapes, including rugged coastlines, fjords, and pristine wilderness areas. Outdoor enthusiasts can explore hiking trails, go fishing or boating, and enjoy wildlife viewing in the surrounding wilderness.

##### Challenges:

Like many remote communities in Labrador, Cartwright faces challenges such as limited access to essential services, economic opportunities, and infrastructure. The harsh climate and geographic isolation can also present logistical challenges for residents.

Overall, Cartwright is a picturesque community with a rich cultural heritage and a close connection to the land and sea. Despite its challenges, it remains an important cultural and historical center in Labrador, cherished by residents and visitors alike.
