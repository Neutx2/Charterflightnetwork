About Baddeck Nova Scotia - Privately Owned Airport

Baddeck is located on the northern shore of Bras d'Or Lake on Cape Breton Island, which puts it in the most central region of Cape Breton Island at the north end of Nova Scotia. The local airport is located about 4 miles north of town and is a privately owned airport, operated by Crown Jewel Aviation. It has a single gravel runway, 3,000 feet long.
