##### About Flights To Sioux Lookout

##### Location

Sioux Lookout is a small community in Northwestern Ontario located 170 air miles north west of Thunder Bay and 240 air miles north east of Winnipeg, Mb. The town is an important air transportation hub for both wheel and float planes. A very busy float plane base is located on Pelican Lake on the edge of town with most of the flights taking sportsmen to and from remote fishing lodges and outpost camps.

##### Sioux Lookout Airport

The Sioux Lookout Airport which is located just north of town features a 5300 foot asphalt runway making it easily accessible to all charter aircraft in the region.

If you are looking for a float plane charter out of Sioux Lookout, Slate Falls Airways offers your choice of a DeHavilland Turbo Otter / DeHavilland Beaver / Cessna U206G Stationair. In addition, Bamaji Airways offers year round air charter service out of Sioux Lookout with a fleet of aircraft consisting of a Dehavilland Beaver, Cessna 185, Found Bush Hawk and a Cessna Caravan
