##### Direct Flights To MontTremblant From Toronto

One of Quebec's most popular Laurentian Mountains destination is Mont Tremblant located 80 miles northwest of [Montreal](charter_flights_Montreal_qc.html) and about 90 miles northeast of Ottawa. Although Mont Tremblant is a year round destination for tourists from all parts of North America, it is most popular as a winter ski resort destination.

Every winter, ski enthusiasts fly and drive to this quaint village from around the world. However, once the snow melts, and the ice is off the surrounding lakes and rivers the town still hosts thousands of tourists throughout the summer and fall, who come here for the golf, fishing, canoeing and dozens of outdoor activities including hiking, and ziplining.

##### Golf Courses

There are dozens of top rated golf courses within a 25 mile radius of Mont Tremblant designed to match all skill levels and challenge desires. We will assume however that you are in the area for just a few days and your time is short - so we will feature the most popular and most conveniently located within the immediate Mont Tremblant area. You will find a selection of 9 hole and 18 hole courses. There are stand alone courses, as well as courses that are part of a resort complex.

You will find a nice 9 hole course - Club de Golf les Ruisseaux along with a challenging 18 hole course - Club de Golf Royal Laurentien in nearby St Jovite. Located closer to the center of Mont Tremblant there is the 18 hole Golf Manitou which is a stand alone course. The Gray Rocks Resort features 36 holes of golf with its very popular La Belle and La Bête courses. As well, Mont Tremblant Resort features 36 challenging holes with its Le Diable and Le Géant Golf Courses

##### Winter Ski Vacations

Fly direct to Mont Tremblant in the speed, comfort and security of a dedicated "Skier Friendly" charter flight. Your pre arranged ground or helicopter shuttle service will be waiting to quickly transfer you to your resort. Be on the slopes within hours of leaving home.

Mont Tremblant may be best known as a winter ski resort destination, but visitors to the area have come to know that Tremblant has so much more to offer 12 months a year. With direct charter flights available to the local international airport - you can easily be experiencing world class activities within hours from leaving home. Check out the video below on exciting river rafting tours - definitely not for the faint of heart. However, there are many more passive activities to enjoy from golf, sailing, canoeing, hiking, snowmobiling, zip lining and the list goes on.

##### Mont Tremblant Airport

The Mont Tremblant International Airport which is officially known as La Macaza is located about 30 miles north west of the village of Mont Tremblant, Quebec. The single runway is asphalt surface and 6000 feet in length, making it easily accessible by virtually all charter aircraft in the region from light twin engine aircraft up to large corporate jets. The airport is a port of entry and is staffed by the Canadian Border Services Agency - so if you are chartering a flight from the USA, you can fly direct to Mont Tremblant from many USA airports and clear customs upon landing. The only restriction is that there can be no more than 15 passengers. If you exceed this number, you will need to stop at a larger center for customs clearance - such as Toronto.
