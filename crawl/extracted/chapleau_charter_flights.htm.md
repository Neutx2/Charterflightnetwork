##### About Flights To Chapleau

##### Location

Chapleau is a very popular destination for sportsmen who come from all parts of North America each year to sample the exceptional fishing and hunting that the area has to offer. Many fishermen take a float plane charter flight from Chapleau to a remote outpost or wilderness lodge. There is no cost nor obligation when you use our Free Charter Quote Services

##### Chapleau Airport

The Chapleau Airport is located 3 miles south east of town and has two runways - one at 5,000 foot asphalt and the other is 3,000 foot asphalt

Chapleau is located just east of Wawa and southwest of Timmins. Chapleau is a popular jumping off point for exceptional fly-in fishing trips to dozens of remote lakes, rivers, lodges and outpost camps throughout Northern Ontario. Watson's Skyways is based in nearby Wawa and serves the air transportation needs of the mining and exploration industry throughout Northern and northwestern Ontario as well as Labrador.
