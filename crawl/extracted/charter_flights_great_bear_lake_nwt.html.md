##### About Great Bear Lake Northwest Territories and Location

Great Bear Lake, situated in the serene wilderness of the Northwest Territories, is renowned for its pristine beauty and the array of outdoor activities it offers. From the majestic Northern Lights to the rich indigenous culture, this destination provides a perfect backdrop for adventure seekers and nature lovers alike, making it ideal for those looking to explore its vast landscapes through charter flights.

##### Distance From Major Cities

**Great Bear Lake to Yellowknife**

Approximately 512 miles (824 kilometers) separate Great Bear Lake from Yellowknife, a journey that showcases the spectacular wilderness accessible by air.

**Great Bear Lake to Edmonton**

Connecting the remote beauty of Great Bear Lake to Edmonton covers a distance of around 1,098 miles (1,767 kilometers), a testament to the vast expanses of the Canadian North.

**Great Bear Lake to Norman Wells**

The distance from Great Bear Lake to Norman Wells is about 200 miles (322 kilometers), highlighting the lake's accessibility to surrounding communities and natural wonders.

**Great Bear Lake to Fort Simpson**

Travelers can reach Fort Simpson from Great Bear Lake after covering 380 miles (611 kilometers), journeying through some of the most breathtaking landscapes the North has to offer.

##### Great Bear Lake Airport

The airport at Great Bear Lake is well-equipped to welcome adventurers from all over, with a runway that accommodates various aircraft sizes. Its proximity to the town and availability of ground transportation options, including taxi and rental cars, makes it a convenient starting point for exploring the region.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Fort Smith Airport**

Located in Fort Smith, NWT, this airport boasts a 5,500-foot runway, offering services such as fuel, maintenance, and lodging for transient aircraft, making it a strategic stopover for charters heading to Great Bear Lake.

**Norman Wells Airport**

With its 6,000-foot runway, Norman Wells Airport serves as a critical connector within the Northwest Territories, providing scheduled and charter flight services, alongside cargo handling and passenger facilities.

**Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport**

This airport features a 6,001-foot runway and is equipped to handle a variety of aircraft, offering charters, fuel services, and maintenance in the heart of the Western Arctic.

**Yellowknife Airport**

As the primary gateway to the Northwest Territories, Yellowknife Airport has a runway length of 7,500 feet and offers comprehensive services including charters, logistics support, and passenger amenities to facilitate travel to remote locations like Great Bear Lake.

##### Great Bear Lake Hotels / Fishing Lodges

**Great Bear Wilderness Lodge**

This lodge offers unrivaled access to some of the best fishing spots on Great Bear Lake, coupled with cozy accommodations and top-notch services, making it a favorite among visitors.

**Northern Lights Resort & Spa**

Experience the magic of the Northern Lights in luxury at this resort, which offers spa services alongside adventure tours, including kayaking and wildlife viewing.

##### Great Bear Lake Outdoor Adventures

The area around Great Bear Lake is a haven for outdoor enthusiasts. From world-class fishing and hunting expeditions to kayaking through tranquil waters and hiking in untouched wilderness, there's no shortage of adventures. Companies like Arctic Adventures and Northern Wilderness Outfitters offer guided tours that take advantage of the region's natural beauty, providing unforgettable experiences in this unique corner of the world.
