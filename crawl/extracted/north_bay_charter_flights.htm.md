##### About Flights To North Bay

##### Location

North Bay is located on the north east shore of Lake Nipissing about 1 hour east of Sudbury on Highway 17. Highway 11 runs north of the city providing access to hundreds of lakes and rivers many of which are accessible only by float plane. You can fly scheduled airlines to North Bay or you can arrange a private charter flight. Use the links on our map to request a no obligation quote for both wheel aircraft and float plane charters.

##### North Bay Airport

The North Bay Jack Garland Airport is located about 5 miles north east of the city. The airport has 3 runways, the longest of which is 10,000 feet asphalt. The airport is served by Air Canada Express and Bearskin Airlines.
