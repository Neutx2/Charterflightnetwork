##### About Watson Lake Yukon and Location

Watson Lake is a serene gateway to the Yukon's spectacular wilderness, offering unique attractions such as the Sign Post Forest, the Northern Lights Space and Science Centre, and access to pristine natural parks. Its remote location makes charter flights an ideal mode of transportation for visitors seeking adventure and tranquility in the heart of Yukon.

##### Watson Lake Airport

Watson Lake Airport features a 5,500-foot runway, located just a short drive from downtown. It offers various services, including fuel, maintenance, and charter flight arrangements, serving as a crucial hub for travelers exploring Yukon's vast wilderness.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Erik Nielsen Whitehorse International Airport**

Located in the capital, it boasts a 9,500-foot runway and serves as a major gateway for international and domestic flights, including charters to remote Yukon destinations.

**Dawson City Airport**

This airport offers a 5,000-foot runway and is a vital link for tourists visiting the historic gold rush town of Dawson City and the surrounding wilderness.

**Old Crow Airport**

As the only air access point to the remote village of Old Crow, this airport has a 4,000-foot runway and provides essential connections to the rest of Yukon.

**Mayo Airport**

Serving the Silver Trail region, Mayo Airport features a 3,934-foot runway and supports regional travel with charter and scheduled flights.
