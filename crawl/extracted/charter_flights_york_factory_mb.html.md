##### York Factory Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* York Factory does not function like a regular town with scheduled commercial access. Visitors typically reach the site by float plane or helicopter charter aircraft, boat, or a difficult paddling route, with Churchill or Thompson Manitoba staging points often used when planning the trip.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Visitor services at York Factory are extremely limited. Standard hotel-style accommodation and meals are generally not available on site except through licensed tours, though camping is allowed within the fenced compound and basic restroom facilities are available for visitors.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* York Factory is best known for self-guided walks, boardwalk exploration, historic interpretation, and the rare chance to experience an isolated fur-trade landscape in polar bear country. The site appeals more to history and wilderness travelers than conventional tourists.
