##### About Great Slave Lake Northwest Territories and Location

Great Slave Lake, nestled in the vast landscapes of the Northwest Territories, is a prime destination for those seeking solitude and adventure in Canada's northern wilderness. Its crystal-clear waters and untouched forests offer a sanctuary for fishing, boating, and aurora viewing. The lake's remote location makes it a perfect spot for accessing via charter flights, providing an exclusive gateway to the heart of the North.

##### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Great Slave Lake to Yellowknife**

Just 250 miles (400 kilometers) south of Yellowknife, Great Slave Lake is within easy reach, offering a quick escape to nature's tranquility.

**Great Slave Lake to Edmonton**

At approximately 1,100 miles (1,770 kilometers) from Edmonton, the lake is a testament to the beauty that lies in the remoteness, accessible through the convenience of charter flights.

**Great Slave Lake to Fort Resolution**

Only 200 miles (322 kilometers) to Fort Resolution, it serves as a gateway to the southeastern shores of Great Slave Lake, showcasing the interconnectedness of the region's communities.

**Great Slave Lake to Fort Simpson**

Standing 400 miles (644 kilometers) from Fort Simpson, the lake offers a serene escape into the wilderness, accessible by air for those looking to explore the vast Northern Territories.

##### Great Slave Lake Airport

Direct Charter Flights can be arranged to a total of Seven airports located on Great Slave Lake - Yellowknife, Behchoko, Fort Providence, Hay River, Fort Resolution, Lutselke, and the Taltheilei Narrows Airport. The Taltheilei Narrows Airport is only maintained during the summer months and is a private use airport, owned and operated by Plummers Great Slave Lodge. The single gravel runway is 5,748 feet long and is located on the north shore of the eastern arm of Great Slave Lake.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Yellowknife Airport**

As a major hub in the Northwest Territories, Yellowknife Airport provides comprehensive services and acts as a critical link for those traveling further into the northern wilderness.

**Fort Smith Airport**

Located on the southern edge of the Northwest Territories, Fort Smith Airport is a gateway to both the northern forests and the historical sites of the South Slave Region.

**Hay River Airport**

Hay River Airport, on the south shore of Great Slave Lake, supports regional travel and supplies, connecting the local communities with the larger Northern network.

**Fort Simpson Airport**

This key regional airport offers another vital access point to the vast wilderness of the Northwest Territories, facilitating exploration and adventure in the region.
