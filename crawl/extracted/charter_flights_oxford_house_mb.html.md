##### Oxford House Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Oxford House Airport is located about half a mile west of the community, making arrivals and departures fairly direct. Its crushed rock runway supports a range of wheel-equipped charter aircraft used for passenger, freight, and essential northern service.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Oxford House offers core community services, but visitors should arrange lodging and local transportation before departure. Travelers coming for work, family visits, or regional travel should expect practical northern services rather than a large hotel market.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Oxford House stands in a scenic northern setting along Oxford Lake and the Hayes River, making it appealing for wilderness travel, fishing country access, photography, and travelers interested in the history and landscape of northern Manitoba waterways.
