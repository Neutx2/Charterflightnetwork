#### Flights From Toronto to Teterboro Airport

##### Average Flight Times Direct To Teterboro Airport

Teterboro Airport is 340 air miles south east of Toronto, Ontario. Flight time will average between 1 hour and 30 minutes flying in a turbo prop to about one hour flying in a light jet.

Turbo prop charter flights are primarily arranged from either Toronto's downtown Billy Bishop Airport on the Island. Jet charters are not available out of Billy Bishop and are primarily arranged out of Toronto's Pearson Airport. Jet charters however are also available out of the Barrie and Oshawa Airport.
