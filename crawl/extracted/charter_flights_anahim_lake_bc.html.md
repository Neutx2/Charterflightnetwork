# Charter Flights To Anahim Lake

### About Anahim Lake Airport (YAA)

Anahim Lake Airport (YAA) is located just over one mile south of Anahim Lake,
providing year-round access for turboprops, light jets, float planes (via nearby lakes),
and helicopter operations. The airport features a paved 3,900-foot runway capable of
handling a wide range of charter aircraft for both business and recreational travel.

The community lies 320 km west of Williams Lake and 140 km east of Bella Coola along Highway 20,
making charter flights the fastest and most reliable option for reaching the region.
Scheduled service is provided by Pacific Coastal Airlines.

#### Artemis Gold & Blackwater Project

With the development of the Artemis Gold Blackwater Project near Anahim Lake,
charter flights play a key role in transporting workers on rotational schedules.
Many companies charter turboprop aircraft from Prince George or Vanderhoof to move crews
efficiently. Dash-8 and King Air class aircraft are best suited for these operations.

Future plans propose an airstrip directly at the mine site to support long-term project access.
