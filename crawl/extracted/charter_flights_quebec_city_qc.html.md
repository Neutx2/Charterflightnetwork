Quebec City is home to several fixed wing and helicopter air charter services including Chrono Aviation which operates a fleet of Beech 1900 and Pilatus PC12 aircraft. Kudlik Aviation is also based here and operates the Pilatus PC12. There are a few helicopter charter services such as Heli Express with a fleet of Aerospatiale AS350 / Bell 205 and MBB BK 117 helicopters.

##### Location

Quebec City, which is the capital city of the Province, is located along the St Lawrence River, 65 air miles east of [Trois Rivieres](charter_flights_trois_rivieres_qc.html) and 145 air miles east of [Montreal](charter_flights_Montreal_qc.html).

##### Quebec City Airport

The Jean Lesage International Airport is of course a major transportation center for many domestic, regional and international airlines. Quebec City is the starting point for many charter flights to the remote communities of Northern Quebec. The two major runways are both asphalt surface with the longest at 9,000 feet and the other at 5,700 feet.
