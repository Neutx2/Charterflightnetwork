##### About Nain Newfoundland / Labrador

Nain is a small Inuit community located in northern Labrador, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada. It is the northernmost permanent settlement in the province and is situated on the northern coast of Labrador. Here are some key points about Nain:

---

##### Location:

Nain is located at approximately 56° 32' N latitude and 61° 41' W longitude, on the northern coast of Labrador. It is accessible primarily by air and sea, as there are no roads connecting it to other communities.

##### Population:

The population of Nain is relatively small, with around 1,100 residents as of recent estimates. The majority of the population is of Inuit descent, and the community has a strong cultural identity..

##### History:

Nain has a long history, with indigenous peoples inhabiting the area for thousands of years. European contact began in the 16th century with the arrival of European explorers and traders. Moravian missionaries established a mission in Nain in 1771, which later became an important center for Inuit culture and education..

##### Economy:

The economy of Nain is primarily based on subsistence activities such as hunting, fishing, and trapping. Additionally, there is some employment in government services, education, and healthcare. Tourism also plays a small role in the local economy, with visitors attracted to the area for its natural beauty and cultural heritage.

##### Culture:

Nain has a rich cultural heritage, with strong ties to Inuit traditions and customs. Traditional activities such as hunting, fishing, and crafting are still practiced by many residents. The community also celebrates events such as Nunatsiavut Day, which commemorates the establishment of the Nunatsiavut Government in 2005.

##### Infrastructure:

Despite its remote location, Nain has basic infrastructure including schools, healthcare facilities, and government services. The community is served by Nain Airport, which provides air connections to other parts of Labrador and Newfoundland..
