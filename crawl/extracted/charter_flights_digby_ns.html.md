##### History:

Digby has a fascinating history dating back to its founding in the 18th century. Originally inhabited by the Mi'kmaq First Nations people, it later became a significant Acadian settlement. The town flourished as a fishing and shipbuilding hub and played a vital role in maritime trade.

##### Culture:

Digby's culture is deeply rooted in its maritime heritage. Visitors can explore local museums, enjoy traditional seafood cuisine, and experience the warmth and hospitality of its residents.

##### How to Get There:

Digby is accessible by road via Highway 101 from Halifax, Nova Scotia's capital, and other major cities in the region. Additionally, ferry services connect Digby to Saint John, New Brunswick.
