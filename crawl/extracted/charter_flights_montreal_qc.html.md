##### About Montreal Charter Flights

##### Location

Montreal is located on an island in the St Lawrence River in south western corner of Quebec.

##### Air Charter Services

There are several air charter companies located in Montreal that offer a wide variety of aircraft, both fixed wing and helicopter. Air Inuit is based in Montreal and offers both scheduled as well as charter services primarily with a Beechcraft 300, but on occasions can offer charters with much larger aircraft such as a Boeing 737 or Dash 8 aircraft.

Aviation Starlink provides charter service with their fleet of turbine and jet aircraft all designed with the corporate traveler in mind

The longest established charter provider is Skyservice Business Aviation offering an extensive line of aircraft from turbo props such as the King Air 300 to larger jet aircraft which include the Bombardier 700 and the Gulfstream G280.

Helicopter charter services include Estrie Helicopters operating a Bell 505 / Foxair Heliservice operating a Robinson R44 and Heli Mistral Service operating Bell 206 and Eurocopter AS350.

##### Montreal Airport

The Montreal-Trudeau International Airport is located 12 miles from downtown and serves as the major airport for passengers throughout southwestern Quebec, Eastern Ontario as well as passengers from Vermont and Northern New York.

The airport is served by numerous scheduled as well as charter airlines providing flights throughout Canada, the USA and dozens of European and other interantional countries.

The airport has 3 runways, the longest of which is 11,000 feet allowing the airport to accommodate the largest jumbo jets from around the world
