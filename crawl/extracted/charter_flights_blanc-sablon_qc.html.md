##### Location

Blanc-Sablon is located in the far south eastern region of Quebec and is the most easterly community in the province. The town is on the north shore of the Gulf of St Lawrence on the border with Labrador and just across the channel from Newfoundland. The town is 412 air miles east of [Sept Iles](charter_flights_Sept-Iles_qc.html), 190 air miles south of Goose Bay, Labrador and 465 air miles south east of [Schefferville, Quebec](charter_flights_Schefferville_qc.html).
