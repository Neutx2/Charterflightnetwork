Pukatawagan Airport sits about 2.5 nautical miles east of the community and provides essential year-round access for residents, work crews, cargo, and medical travel. Its crushed rock runway can handle a range of northern fixed-wing aircraft commonly used for scheduled service, charters, medevacs, and practical community resupply operations.

##### Pukatawagan Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Pukatawagan Airport, XPK / CZFG, is located east of the community and has a crushed rock runway of about 3,000 feet. It supports northern turboprop and utility aircraft used for scheduled service, charters, medevacs, freight, and community access.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Pukatawagan is primarily a remote community destination rather than a conventional tourism centre, so visitor lodging choices can be limited. Travelers often rely on local arrangements, community contacts, work-camp style accommodation, or pre-booked stays coordinated before arrival.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* The surrounding lakes, forests, and waterways offer strong appeal for fishing, boating, wildlife viewing, and backcountry travel. For visitors with local connections or organized trip plans, the region can be attractive for outdoor adventure and northern wilderness experiences.
