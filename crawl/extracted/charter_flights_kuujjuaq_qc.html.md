##### About Flights To Kuujjuaq

##### Charter Airlines

There are two air charter services operating out of Kuujjuaq which is located in the far northern Nunavik region of Quebec. Johnny May's Air Charters operates a single engine turbine De Havilland Otter aircraft on wheels, skis and floats and depending on the configuration and distance to be flown, will carry 9 passengers or up to 2,800 lbs of payload.

The second air charter service is Nunavik Rotors Inc and operates an Aerospatiale AS350 B2 Helicopter out of Kuujjuaq serving the mining, exploration industries, government and tourism clients.

Use our Free Charter Quote Request Service for pricing and availability on either of these services

##### Location

Kuujjuaq is a remote community located in the far north central region of Quebec on the southern shores of Ungava Bay.

##### Kuujjuaq Airport

In spite of this isolated location, the town has a very busy airport with an average of 12,000 aircraft movements a year. The airport has two runways - a 6,000 foot asphalt strip and a 5,000 gravel strip. The airport can accommodate a wide range of aircraft including jet aircraft in the 737 class.
