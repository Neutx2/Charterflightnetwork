##### About Flights To Emo

##### Location

You will find the community of Emo located about a 30 minute drive west of Fort Frances on Hwy 11 on the banks of Rainy River. Because of its proximity to the US border the town and surrounding area is a very popular tourist destination especially for American fishermen heading for the many resorts, fishing lodges and fly-in services located in the region.

##### Emo Airport

The closest airports to Emo are Fort Frances which is a 30 minute drive to Emo or Kenora which is just over a 2 hour drive north

#### Emo Wheel Plane Charter Flights

Emo is primarily a starting point for fly-in fishing and hunting adventures. There is no airport here. You could fly private charter flight to Fort Frances and arrange either ground transportation to Emo ( 30 minute drive via hwy 11) or connect with a float plane for the very short flight to the water base near Emo. #### Emo Float Plane Charter Flights

There are a couple of float plane charter flight options out of the Emo area. They specialize in fly-out fishing and hunting trips to remote outpost camps and lodges, but can also provide you with general charter services that meet your needs as well.

#### Emo Helicopter Charter Flights

There is no helicopter services out of Emo and very little if any demand. The closest full time helicopter charter base is located in Kenora, 85 air miles to the north. We can source out competitive quotes if you like....
