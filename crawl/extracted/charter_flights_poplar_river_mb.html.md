## Flights to Poplar River, Manitoba

### **About Poplar River:**

Poplar River is a remote Anishinaabe community on the east side of Lake Winnipeg at the mouth of the Poplar River, about 450 kilometres from Winnipeg. It is also part of the Pimachiowin Aki UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape.

##### Poplar River Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Charter flights to Poplar River use Poplar River Airport, XPP / CZNG, which is located adjacent to the community. Airport references list a runway measuring about 2,500 feet with a crushed rock surface, making it suitable for northern charter aircraft that can operate from shorter unpaved runways.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Verified local accommodation appears limited, but Manitoba planning documents identify Sagatay Lodge in Poplar River. Community planning materials also reference local dining and essential services, indicating basic practical amenities for short-stay northern travel.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Poplar River is best known for wilderness, culture, and land-based travel rather than mainstream tourism infrastructure. Its appeal is closely tied to boreal forest scenery, major waterways, and the broader Pimachiowin Aki cultural landscape.

### Flights to Poplar River, MB FAQ

### What airport serves Poplar River, Manitoba?

Poplar River is served by Poplar River Airport, with airport codes XPP and CZNG. Airport references list a 2,500-foot crushed rock runway adjacent to the community.

### Can direct wheel plane charter flights be arranged to Poplar River?

Yes. Direct wheel plane charter flights can be arranged to Poplar River for passengers, freight, and community travel, provided the aircraft is suitable for the runway and current conditions.

### Is Poplar River part of Pimachiowin Aki?

Yes. Poplar River First Nation is one of the Manitoba First Nations within the Pimachiowin Aki UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape.

### Are float plane charter flights available for the Poplar River region?

Seasonal float plane charter flights may be arranged for the Poplar River region during open-water periods, especially for routes where lake or river access is more practical than airport access.
