##### About Assiniboia, Saskatchewan and Location

Assiniboia, located in the heart of southern Saskatchewan, is renowned for its expansive landscapes and rich cultural heritage. An ideal destination for those seeking adventure and relaxation, Assiniboia offers a unique blend of attractions, making it accessible and attractive for charter flights from across the region and beyond.

##### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Assiniboia to Regina**

Just over 80 air miles, a quick charter flight connects you to the capital's bustling atmosphere.

**Assiniboia to Saskatoon**

Approximately 180 air miles north-east, linking Assiniboia with Saskatchewan's largest city through scenic skies.

**Assiniboia to Yorkton**

Direct flights cover about 190 air miles east, showcasing the province's vast agricultural lands.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Moose Jaw Municipal Airport**

Located approximately 70 miles north of Assiniboia, providing additional access for charter flights within the region.

**Swift Current Airport**

About 110 miles west, this airport serves as a key point for accessing southwestern Saskatchewan.

**Regina International Airport**

A major hub about 100 miles to the northeast, offering extensive services for international and domestic travelers.

**Weyburn Regional Airport**

Approximately 90 miles east, facilitating charter and local flights in southeastern Saskatchewan.

##### Assiniboia Hotels / Accommodations

**Assiniboia Union Hotel**

A historic hotel offering guests a comfortable stay with modern amenities and a taste of local heritage.

**Prairie Pride Motel**

Known for its welcoming atmosphere and convenient location, the perfect base for exploring Assiniboia and beyond.

##### Assiniboia Outdoor Adventures

**Grasslands National Park**

Discover the rugged beauty of one of Canada's national parks, a short charter flight or drive away, offering unique hiking and wildlife viewing.

**Lake of the Prairies**

Enjoy fishing, boating, and breathtaking sunsets at this nearby lake, easily accessible from Assiniboia for a day trip or weekend getaway.
