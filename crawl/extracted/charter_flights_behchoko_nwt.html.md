##### About Bechoko Northwest Territories and Location

Bechoko, nestled in the heart of the Northwest Territories, is a community rich in culture and surrounded by breathtaking natural landscapes. This serene location offers visitors a unique blend of traditional lifestyle and outdoor adventure. From the pristine waters of Great Slave Lake to the endless boreal forests, Bechoko is the perfect starting point for exploring the vast wilderness of the North.

##### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Bechoko to Yellowknife**

Located just 74 miles (119 km) from Yellowknife, Bechoko is easily accessible, offering a scenic route that showcases the beauty of the Northern landscape.

**Bechoko to Edmonton**

The journey from Bechoko to Edmonton covers approximately 920 miles (1,480 km), connecting adventurers to the heart of the Northwest Territories from a major urban center.

**Bechoko to Fort Resolution**

Traveling from Bechoko to Fort Resolution spans about 350 miles (563 km), a journey through diverse ecosystems and historical sites.

**Bechoko to Fort Simpson**

The distance to Fort Simpson is around 470 miles (756 km), offering access to the spectacular Nahanni National Park Reserve.

##### Bechoko Airport

Bechoko Airport features a modest runway length, sufficient for small aircraft and charter flights, which are essential for connecting the community with the rest of the Northwest Territories. Located conveniently close to the town, the airport provides essential services to residents and visitors, including freight, mail delivery, and emergency services.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Yellowknife Airport**

As the primary gateway to the Northwest Territories, Yellowknife Airport offers a full range of services, including international flights, making it a crucial hub for visitors to Bechoko.

**Fort Simpson Airport**

This airport serves as a key access point to the South Nahanni River and Nahanni National Park Reserve, with a runway that accommodates a variety of aircraft.

**Hay River Merlyn Carter Airport**

Located in Hay River, this airport is essential for transportation and cargo services in the South Slave Region, supporting the economic and tourism sectors.

**Fort Smith Airport**

Fort Smith Airport provides another vital link for accessing the Northwest Territories' spectacular natural resources and recreational areas, including the Wood Buffalo National Park.

##### Bechoko Hotels / Accommodations

**Northern Lights Inn**

The Northern Lights Inn in Bechoko offers cozy accommodations with stunning views of the aurora borealis during the winter months, providing a comfortable base for all adventurers.

**Bechoko Wilderness Lodge**

Perfect for those seeking a rustic experience, Bechoko Wilderness Lodge offers direct access to outdoor activities, including fishing, hiking, and wildlife observation.

##### Bechoko Outdoor Adventures

Bechoko is a haven for outdoor enthusiasts, offering a wide range of activities from ice fishing and dog sledding in the winter to canoeing and bird watching in the summer. Local outfitters, such as Bechoko Adventure Tours, specialize in guided expeditions to remote locations, ensuring visitors have a safe and memorable experience in the Northwest Territories.
