##### About Flights To Timmins

##### Location

Timmins is a vital mining and tourism destination in Northern Ontario. You can arrange charter flights direct to the Timmins airport or you can book float plane charters to many of the remote lakes and rivers north of town. Timmins is the gateway to many of the remote communities along James Bay both in Quebec and Ontario and offers a wide variety of charter flights from light twins to large cargo aircraft. To arrange a charter flight or request a no obligation charter quote

##### Timmins Airport

The Timmins Airport is a major air transportation hub for northern Ontario and the James Bay lowlands. The Timmins Airport which is known as the Victor M Power Airport is located 7 miles north west of town and has a 6,000 foot runway and 4,900 foot asphalt runway.

##### Scheduled & Charter Air Services

In addition to air charter services, Thunder Air operates scheduled passenger service to several communities in Ontario located along the James Bay Coast with departures out of Timmins. Use our Charter Quote Request Service to receive a no obligation price quote directly from Thunder Airlines as well as other charter airlines in the region that meet your requirements.

#### Timmins Float Plane Charter Flights

The closest float plane charter service to Timmins is located in Cochrane which is a 30 minute drive east of Timmins. Ask us to find the best options for your float plane requirements anywhere in the Timmins region.

Expedition Helicopters is based in Cochrane, which is a 30 minute drive from Timmins.
