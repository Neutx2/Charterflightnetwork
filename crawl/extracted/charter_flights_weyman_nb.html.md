The Weyman Airpark is located just northwest of Fredericton near the Keswick River. This is a public use airport with a 3,000 foot sand/gravel/chipseal runway - which may not be accessible to all charter aircraft in the region. Since the much bigger Fredericton airport is just a few miles away - charter passengers will have much more flexibility in aircraft types and fewer chances of operation delays due to weather and runway conditions.
