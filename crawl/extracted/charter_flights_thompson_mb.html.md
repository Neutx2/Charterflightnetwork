### **About Thompson:**

Thompson Airport is located about three nautical miles north of the city and serves as one of the most important aviation gateways in northern Manitoba. With a 5,800-foot asphalt runway plus a secondary gravel runway, the airport can handle regional airline service, many charter aircraft, cargo flights, helicopters, and northern support operations.

##### Thompson Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Thompson Airport sits just north of the city and functions as a major northern air hub for passenger travel, cargo, mining support, helicopters, and charter flights. Its runway infrastructure is suitable for a broad range of regional and private aircraft.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Thompson has one of the strongest visitor service bases in northern Manitoba, with hotels such as Best Western Thompson Hotel & Suites and Thompson Inn, plus restaurants, vehicle rentals, retail services, and airport ground transportation for arriving travelers.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Thompson draws visitors for northern lights viewing, fishing, hiking, skiing, snowmobiling, and day trips to Pisew Falls and Paint Lake Provincial Park. It also works well as a staging point for wilderness adventures and northern Manitoba lodge travel.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Charter Flights to Thompson

### What airport serves Thompson, Manitoba?

Thompson Airport serves the city and is located about three nautical miles north of Thompson. It is one of the most important aviation gateways in northern Manitoba and supports a wide mix of charter and scheduled operations.

### Can I arrange a charter flight to Thompson from Winnipeg?

Yes. Winnipeg is one of the most common starting points for Thompson charter flights, and other Manitoba airports can also be used depending on passenger count, payload needs, aircraft type, and trip timing.

### What makes Thompson important for northern charter travel?

Thompson functions as a major hub for northern Manitoba travel, supporting mining and business aviation, cargo shipments, helicopter work, and onward access to many remote communities, lodges, and industrial locations.

### What can visitors do in Thompson?

Visitors come to Thompson for northern lights viewing, fishing, snowmobiling, hiking, and trips to attractions such as Paint Lake Provincial Park and Pisew Falls. The city also has hotels, restaurants, and transportation services for overnight stays.
