### **About Flin Flon:**

Flin Flon Airport is located near Bakers Narrows about 8 nautical miles southeast of town and serves as the main fixed-wing airport for the area. Its 5,004-foot asphalt runway can accommodate regional airline traffic, charter turboprops, business aircraft, and practical passenger or cargo operations serving northern Manitoba and nearby Saskatchewan.

##### Flin Flon Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Flin Flon Airport at Bakers Narrows is the main scheduled and charter airport for the area, while Channing Lake is associated with local floatplane activity closer to town. This gives travelers both fixed-wing and water-access flexibility.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Flin Flon offers stronger visitor services than many northern communities. Area lodging includes destination properties such as Bakers Narrows Lodge, and airport services include car rental availability, making the town practical for anglers, business travelers, and regional visitors.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Visitors come to Flin Flon for fishing, boating, lake scenery, and northern outdoor recreation. Lake Athapapuskow and Bakers Narrows Provincial Park are nearby highlights, with trophy fishing, camping, viewpoints, and easy access to classic northwest Manitoba landscapes.
