##### Location

Montmagny is located on the south shore of the St Lawrence River and is just a few miles east of Quebec City.

##### Montmagny Airport

The town has a public use airport located 2 miles northeast of town. The single asphalt runway is 3,000 feet long - making it accessible by most of the charter aircraft operating in the region.
