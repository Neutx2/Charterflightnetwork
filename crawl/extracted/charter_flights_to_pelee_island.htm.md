#### About Pelee Island

##### Location Of Pelee Island

Pelee Island is located near the south end of Lake Erie just south of Leamington Ontario. The Island is a popular pheasant hunting destination in the fall, but each year thousands of tourists are attracted to the island to view the unique eco system and wildlife. There is also a wide variety of accommodations from motels, bed & breakfast and camping.

##### Charter Flights From Windsor
