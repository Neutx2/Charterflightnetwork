##### Molson Lake Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Molson Lake Lodge can be reached directly by wheel plane using the private airstrip beside the lodge, which helps simplify arrivals for guests. Float plane access may also be arranged when seasonal operating conditions are suitable.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Molson Lake Lodge is itself the primary accommodation, offering guests an all-in-one fly-in fishing base with meals, lodge facilities, and direct access to the surrounding wilderness and fishing waters.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Molson Lake Lodge is best known for trophy fishing, wilderness scenery, and classic fly-in lodge experiences. Guests are drawn by walleye, northern pike, lake trout, and the quiet setting of remote northern Manitoba.
