### About Stettler Alberta Charter Flights

About Stettler Alberta

Stettler is located in central Alberta less than an hour's drive from Red Deer and is a modest sized town of about 5700 residents.

Charter Flights To The Stettler Airport

There are no scheduled flights to Stettler however the town does have a local airport with a 3000 asphalt surface runway making it ideal for a good cross section of charter flight aircraft.
