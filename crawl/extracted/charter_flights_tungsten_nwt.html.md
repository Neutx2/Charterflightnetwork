##### About Tungsten Northwest Territories and Location

Tungsten is located in the west central region of NWT on the border with Yukon and just outside the Nahanni National Park.

##### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Tungsten to Yellowknife**

The journey from Tungsten to Yellowknife spans approximately 500 miles (805 kilometers), illustrating the seclusion of Tungsten and the convenience offered by charter flights for travelers.

**Tungsten to Edmonton**

Connecting Tungsten with Edmonton involves covering a distance of roughly 900 miles (1448 kilometers), further emphasizing the role of charter flights in bridging the vast expanse.

**Tungsten to Fort Resolution**

The distance from Tungsten to Fort Resolution is about 600 miles (965 kilometers), showcasing the accessibility challenges that are elegantly solved by charter flights.

**Tungsten to Fort Simpson**

Traveling from Tungsten to Fort Simpson requires navigating about 450 miles (724 kilometers), a testament to the utility of charter flights in connecting remote locations.

##### Tungsten Airport

The local airport is a private use airport, owned and operated by North American Tungsten Corp, which supports and services its local mining operations, The single runway is 3,700 feet long with a Gravel surface..

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Nahanni Butte Airport**

Located near the majestic Nahanni National Park Reserve, Nahanni Butte Airport serves as a crucial access point for adventurers and explorers, with a runway suitable for small to medium aircraft.

**Fort Liard Airport**

This airport lies in the serene community of Fort Liard, offering flights to various parts of the Northwest Territories. Its services cater to the needs of both tourists and residents.

**Canol Road Airport**

A gateway to the historic Canol Trail, Canol Road Airport is known for its picturesque surroundings and facilitates access to one of the most scenic hikes in the region.

**Wrigley Airport**

Wrigley Airport connects the small community of Wrigley with the rest of the Northwest Territories. Though modest, it plays a significant role in the transportation network of the area.
