##### About Pelly Crossing Yukon and Location

Pelly Crossing in Yukon is a captivating destination, known for its rich cultural heritage and stunning natural landscapes. Situated along the Pelly River, this remote community serves as a gateway to outdoor adventures. With charter flights, accessing Pelly Crossing has never been easier, inviting visitors to explore its historic sites, vibrant wildlife, and engaging seasonal activities like fishing, hunting, and Northern Lights viewing.

##### Pelly Crossing Airport

The Pelly Crossing Airport, with its 3,300-foot runway, is ideally situated a short drive from the town center. Although modest, the airport is equipped to handle charter flights, providing essential services like fuel and maintenance, making it a critical hub for travel within Yukon and beyond.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Dawson City Airport**

Located in the historic town of Dawson City, this airport features a 4,000-foot runway and offers a range of services including refueling and mechanical repairs.

**Mayo Airport**

Mayo Airport, serving the Silver Trail region, boasts a 3,500-foot runway and accommodates both commercial and charter flights, with basic pilot amenities.

**Old Crow Airport**

This vital link to Old Crow, the only Yukon community above the Arctic Circle, includes a 4,500-foot runway and supports the lifeline of goods and people to and from the area.

**Carmacks Airport**

Carmacks Airport, although smaller with a 2,800-foot runway, plays a crucial role in connecting this rural community with the rest of Yukon.
