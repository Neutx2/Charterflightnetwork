##### The town of Merritt is located in south central interior region of BC - west of Kelowna, South of Kamloops and 270 km northeast of Vancouver.

Charter flights can be arranged to the Merritt Airport which is also known as Saunders Field and is 2 miles east of town. The airport has a 4000 foot asphalt runway which makes it accessible to a wide range of charter aircraft from small single engine, high performance turbine and even small corporate jets.

If you are flying charter flights to Merritt for business or vacation and need accommodations, you will find a nice selection of hotels to choose from. Some of the popular hotels include: The Comfort Inn, Travelodge. Ramada Limited, Quality Inn and Corbett Lake Lodge.
