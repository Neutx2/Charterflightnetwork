##### About Flights To Earlton

##### Location

Earlton is located in northern Ontario close to the Quebec Border. The area offers plenty of exceptional hunting and fishing and you can book float plane charter flights to wilderness regions north of Earlton and into northern Quebec. You can also arrange private charter flights direct to the Earlton Airport.

##### Earlton Airport

The Earlton Temiskaming Regional Airport is located just over a mile south west of the town. The airport has two runways, one of which is a 6,000 foot asphalt runway and the other is a 3,000 foot gravel snow runway.

There are no wheel plane air charter services base in Earlton.
