##### About Whati Northwest Territories and Location

Whati, nestled in the heart of the Northwest Territories, is a hidden gem waiting to be explored. This charming community is surrounded by stunning natural landscapes, offering a plethora of activities and attractions for every type of traveler. From fishing in crystal-clear lakes to hiking through untouched forests, Whati is the perfect destination for those seeking adventure and tranquility. Its remote location makes charter flights an ideal mode of transportation, providing unparalleled access to this breathtaking part of Canada.

##### Distance From Major Cities

**Whati to Yellowknife**

Connecting Whati to Yellowknife, the journey spans approximately 160 miles (257 kilometers), offering a quick and scenic charter flight over the vast wilderness of the Northwest Territories.

**Whati to Edmonton**

The distance from Whati to Edmonton is around 920 miles (1,480 kilometers), a testament to the remote yet accessible beauty of Whati through charter aviation.

**Whati to Norman Wells**

Travelling from Whati to Norman Wells covers a distance of about 450 miles (724 kilometers), showcasing the diverse landscapes of the region.

**Whati to Tuktoyaktuk**

The route from Whati to Tuktoyaktuk stretches approximately 1,100 miles (1,770 kilometers), offering adventurers a gateway to the Arctic.

##### Whati Airport

The Whati Airport serves as a vital link between Whati and the rest of the Northwest Territories. With a runway length of just under 3,000 feet. it accommodates various types of aircraft, facilitating travel and cargo services. Situated just a short drive from the town, the airport provides essential connectivity for residents and visitors alike. Available services include flight charters, making it easier than ever to explore the natural wonders of the area.

##### Other Nearby Airports

**Behchokǫ̀ Airport**

Behchokǫ̀ Airport, located near the Tlicho community, offers another entry point into the region with essential services for travelers and residents.

**Fort Smith Airport**

Fort Smith Airport features a longer runway, capable of handling larger aircraft and providing a range of services to support the local community and tourism.

**Hay River Merlyn Carter Airport**

This airport acts as a critical transportation hub in the South of the Northwest Territories, with facilities to support both commercial and recreational flights.

**Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport**

As a gateway to the Arctic, Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport offers unique access to northern communities and landscapes, complete with comprehensive traveler services.

##### Whati Hotels / Accommodations

**Lakeside Lodge**

Lakeside Lodge in Whati offers guests a serene setting with stunning lake views, modern amenities, and easy access to outdoor activities.

**Whati Wilderness Retreat**

Experience the ultimate escape at Whati Wilderness Retreat, providing cozy accommodations amidst the natural beauty of the Northwest Territories.

##### Whati Outdoor Adventures

Whati is an outdoor enthusiast's paradise, featuring activities such as ice fishing, dog sledding, and aurora viewing in the winter, alongside kayaking, bird watching, and hiking in the summer months. Local outfitters, like Whati Adventure Tours, offer guided excursions to explore the region's natural beauty, ensuring an unforgettable experience in the wilds of the Northwest Territories.
