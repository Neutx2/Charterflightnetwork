##### [East Island Excursions](https://eastislandpr.com/charters/luxury-private-catamaran/)

Sail to the island of Icacos with a private catamaran supplied by East Island Excursions. The Island of Icacos near Puerto Rico is a popular excursion for visitors looking to experience the natural beauty and tranquility of the Caribbean. Here's what you can expect:

###### Location:

Icacos Island is located approximately 3 miles east of Fajardo on the eastern coast of Puerto Rico. It's part of the Cordillera Keys Nature Reserve and is known for its pristine white sand beaches, crystal-clear waters, and vibrant marine life.

###### Day Trips:

Day trips to Icacos Island are common, with boats departing in the morning and returning in the afternoon. Depending on the tour or charter company, the trip may include other nearby attractions such as snorkeling spots, mangrove forests, or additional islands in the area..

###### Access:

The island is accessible primarily by boat. Visitors typically depart from marinas in Fajardo or nearby areas on the eastern coast of Puerto Rico. Private boat charters, catamaran tours, and water taxi services are available for those who wish to visit the island.

###### Activities:

Icacos Island offers excellent opportunities for swimming, snorkeling, sunbathing, and beachcombing. The surrounding waters are teeming with colorful coral reefs and tropical fish, making it an ideal spot for underwater exploration. Many visitors also enjoy picnicking on the beach and simply soaking in the scenic beauty of the island.

###### Facilities:

While Icacos Island is largely undeveloped and uninhabited, there are typically no facilities or amenities available on the island itself. Visitors should bring their own food, water, sunscreen, and other essentials for their day trip.
