##### About Flights To Poplar Hill

##### Location

Poplar Hill is a remote and isolated First Nation`s community located north of Red Lake. There are no roads to Poplar Hill, except for a winter road that is available for several weeks throughout the winter. The only access is by air and travelers can book scheduled flights with Wasaya Airways or book a direct charter flight from Red Lake, Sioux Lookout or Pickle Lake

##### Poplar Hill Airport

The Poplar Hill Airport is located adjacent to the community and has a 3,500 foot clay/gravel runway.

Sandy Lake is located 80 air miles north of Poplar Hill. Sandy Lake Seaplanes can provide float plane charter flights between Sandy Lake and Poplar Hill or other remote communities in the region. For a quick quote, use our Free Charter Quote Request Service and receive a no obligation quote directly from Sandy Lake Seaplanes as well as other charter services in the region.
