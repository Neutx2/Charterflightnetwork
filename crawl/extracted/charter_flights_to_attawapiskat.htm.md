##### About Flights To Attawapiskat

##### Location

Attawapiskat is 230 St air miles north of Hearst and 305 air miles north of Timmins Ontario. The community is accessible by wheel and float plane charter flights. There are no charter services based in Attawapiskat,

##### Attawapiskat Airport

The community does have a 3,500 foot gravel surface runway which makes it suitable for aircraft up to large twin engine aircraft (piston or turbine). When the regional mine was in operation, Dash 8 aircraft operated out of the Attawapiskat airport on a regular basis performing crew changes for mining operations

Nakina is 265 air miles south of Attawapiskat and has both wheel plane and float plane charter services based in the community. Contact us today and we will source out competitive quotes.

Pickle Lake is a major air transportation hub for northwestern Ontario and is 350 air miles south west of Attawapiskat. We can get you competitive quotes for wheel plane, float plane and helicopter charters.

##### [Flights From Sudbury](sudbury_charter_flights.htm)

Sudbury is 445 air miles south of Attawapiskat. Your most cost efficient charter option would be a high performance turbine that will fly above weather systems that may be encountered along the way.

##### [Flights From Thunder Bay](thunder_bay_charter_flights.htm)

Thunder Bay is 440 air miles south of Attawapiskat. We can get you competitive quotes that can get you up to Attawapiskat in the morning, wait for a few hours, and then return you to Thunder Bay that afternoon, in time to catch an evening flight back to Toronto. Contact us with your requirements and we will get you competitive quotes.

##### [Flights From Timmins](timmins_charter_flights.htm)

Timmins is the closest community to Attawapiskat that has a full time wheel plane charter operation, highway access as well as scheduled flight service to Toronto. If this is a convenient departure point for your travel needs, contact us for competitive quotes.

It would be more cost efficient to take a scheduled flight to Timmins and then charter from there. However, if you want to fly direct from Toronto, contact us and we will get you competitive quotes for turbo prop charters.
