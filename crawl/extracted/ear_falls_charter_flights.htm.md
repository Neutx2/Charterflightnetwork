##### About Flights To Ear Falls

##### Location

Ear Falls is located on Highway 105 just south of Red Lake and north of Vermillion Bay. There are numerous fly in lodges, outpost camps and outfitters throughout the Ear Falls area. If you would like to visit this area, you can arrange private Charter Flights direct to Ear Falls Airport or you can drive to Ear Falls and arrange float plane charter flights to dozens of remote outpost camps and fishing lodges

##### Ear Falls Airport

The closest airport to Ear Falls is located just under an hours drive north to Red Lake
