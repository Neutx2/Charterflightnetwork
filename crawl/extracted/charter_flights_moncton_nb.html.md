About Moncton New Brunswick

Discover The Convenience and Affordability of Flying Direct to or From Moncton New Brunswick In The Speed Comfort and Security of A Private Charter Flight
Moncton is located in the south eastern region of New Brunswick and is considered as a transportation hub for the Maritime Provinces. The Greater Moncton International Airport is located 5 miles northeast of downtown and handles close to 650,000 passengers each year. The airport has two asphalt runways, the longest of which is 10,000 feet long.
