# Charter Flights To Fort Hope

## This Page Lists Airlines That Provide Charter Flights To Fort Hope From Departure Points Across Ontario and Manitoba

**About Fort Hope**

Fort Hope is an isolated and remote First Nation's Community located on the north shore of Eabamet Lake, which is part of the Albany River in North Western Ontario. The community is north of Nakina and East of Pickle Lake. Except for a brief period during the winter months, there is no road access to Fort Hope, so all travel is by aircraft. The community is served by regular scheduled commuter flights with departures from Thunder Bay and Sioux Lookout. Charter flights to Fort Hope are also available from dozens of departure points across the north, the most popular of which are listed below.

The two main departure points for charter flights to Fort Hope are Pickle Lake which is located 100 air miles west of the community and Nakina which is located 110 air miles south of the community. Charter flights are also conducted out of Thunder Bay which is 230 air miles south.

**Fort Hope Airport**

The Fort Hope Airport is located adjacent to the town and has a 3,500 foot gravel runway
