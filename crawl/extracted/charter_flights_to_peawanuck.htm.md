##### About Flights To Peawanuck

##### Location

Peawanuck is one of the most northerly settlements in Ontario and can be reached by scheduled or charter flights from various departure points across the North including Thunder Bay and Timmins. There are no roads to Peawanuck. If only one or two passengers are traveling and there is no urgency with scheduling, your most cost efficient option is scheduled flights to Peawanuck. However, if you have bulk cargo, or scheduled flights do not meet your travel needs, you can arrange private charter flights direct to Peawanuck.

##### Peawanuck Airport

The Peawanuck airport is conveniently located next to the community and features a 3,518 foot gravel runway. The airport is served by Air Creebec and Thunder Airlines, each providing flights to and from Attawapiskat, Fort Albany, Kashechewan, Moosonee and Timmins.

#### Peawanuck Wheel Plane Charter Flights

Charter flights to Peawanuck are fairly expensive. The closest departure point for wheel plane charters is Pickle Lake which is 315 air miles south of Peawanuck. If you can put together 6 - 8 passengers, the cost per person would be a little more reasonable.

#### Peawanuck Float Plane Charter Flights

If you are seeking world class Brook Trout fishing near Peawanuck just south of the Hudson Bay coastline , contact us about setting up a float plane charter flight direct to remote outposts on the on the Winisk and Shamattawa Rivers. Outfitters can set you up with canoes, motors, gas and kitchen supplies.
