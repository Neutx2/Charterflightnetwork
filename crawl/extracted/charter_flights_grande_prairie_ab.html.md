##### About Grande Prairie Alberta

Grande Prairie is located in northwestern Alberta about 280 miles north west of Edmonton. The city is considered as the economic and agricultural center for northern Alberta. **Major Economic Industries:**
Grande Prairie's economy is diverse, with several key industries driving growth and development in the region. Some of the major economic sectors include:
**Oil and Gas:** Grande Prairie is located in the heart of Alberta's oil and gas region, and the energy sector plays a significant role in the city's economy. The extraction, production, and distribution of oil and natural gas contribute to employment and economic growth in the area.
**Forestry and Agriculture:** Agriculture and forestry are important industries in Grande Prairie and the surrounding Peace River region. The area's fertile soil and abundant forests support a thriving agricultural sector, including crop farming, cattle ranching, and timber harvesting..
**Construction and Manufacturing:** Grande Prairie's booming construction and manufacturing industries contribute to the city's economic prosperity. With ongoing infrastructure projects, residential developments, and industrial construction, there is a high demand for skilled labor and manufacturing expertise.

##### Charter Flights To The Grande Prairie Airport

The Grande Prairei Airport is located 3.5 miles northwest of the city and is a major transportation hub for northwestern Alberta. The airport is served by scheduled flights operated by Air Canada and WestJet. The airport also serves a very busy air charter market as well.

The airport has two asphalt runways 6,200 feet and 8,500 feet making it accessible to virtually any charter aircraft available in the region.
