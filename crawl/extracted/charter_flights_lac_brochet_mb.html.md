## Flights to Lac Brochet

### **About Lac Brochet:**

Lac Brochet is home to the Northlands Denesuline First Nation, a Dene-speaking Indigenous community with a population of approximately 800 people. The surrounding region is rich in boreal forest, pristine lakes, and abundant wildlife, offering a peaceful and culturally rich northern experience for visitors.

##### Lac Brochet Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Lac Brochet is served by the Lac Brochet Airport, located just a few kilometers southeast of the community. The gravel runway, measuring approximately 3,300 feet in length, accommodates a range of charter aircraft—from small single-engine aircraft to larger twin turboprops.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Visitors should expect limited services typical of a remote northern community. Community amenities are available, but travelers should confirm accommodation, meals, and local transportation before departure rather than depending on walk-in options.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Fishing and hunting are a way of life here, and for those seeking an authentic northern adventure, Lac Brochet provides an ideal launching point for fly-in fishing expeditions, Indigenous cultural experiences, and wilderness photography.

## Lac Brochet Charter Flight FAQ

### **What airport serves Lac Brochet, Manitoba?**

Lac Brochet is served by Lac Brochet Airport, using the codes XLB and CZWH. It is the main airport for the community and a key transportation link for northern passenger and cargo movements.

### **How far is Lac Brochet Airport from the community?**

Lac Brochet Airport is located about 1 nautical mile east of town, which makes local transfers short and practical for passengers, freight, and time-sensitive trips.

### **What type of runway does Lac Brochet Airport have?**

The airport has a crushed-rock runway measuring about 3,500 feet. That runway is suitable for many northern turboprop and utility aircraft used on remote Manitoba routes.

### **Is Lac Brochet connected by permanent roads?**

No. There are no permanent roads connecting Lac Brochet to other parts of Manitoba, so air service plays a major role in regular travel, freight movement, and community access.
