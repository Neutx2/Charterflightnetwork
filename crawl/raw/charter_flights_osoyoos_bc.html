About Osoyoos

Location Of Osoyoos

Book direct Charter Flights to the town of Osoyoos which is located in south central region of British Columbia on Lake Osoyoos and just a few miles north of the US border with the state of Washington

Osoyoos Airport The town has a local airport with a 2,477 foot asphalt runway which might make it inaccessible to large charter aircraft - and jets. Many piston and turbo prop aircraft however may be able to operate from this airport. There are no scheduled flights to this airport, however visitors to Osoyoos can book direct charter flights from airports across Canada. Osoyoos is 170 air miles from Vancouver and 280 air miles south west of Calgary. There is no Canadian Customs clearance services at this airport, so if your charter flight originates in the US, you will need to land at a designated airport of entry prior to landing at Osoyoos. Alternative Airports If you are searching for an alternative travel option to commercial airlines and want to avoid sitting shoulder to shoulder with other passengers - you best option may be private charter flights. The only other passengers on board are the friends, family memebers or business associates you have invited to join your flight. If Osoyoos is not the most convenient airport for your travel needs, youmay find one of the following nearby loctions better suited to your requirements. Penticton Grand Forks Trail Kelowna Scheduled Flights If you wish to fly commercial scheduled airlines to Osoyoos British Columbia, your best option is a flight to Penticton located just over 60 km north of Osoyoos. Upon arrival in Penticton you can arrange ground transportation to Osoyoos. There are two car rental agencies available at the Penticton Airport: Budget and Enterprise. Drive time from Penticton to Osoyoos is just under one hour. However at the time of writing this information there is a forest fire currently burning near Okanagan Falls located on Hwy 97 at the south end of Skaha Lake. This may cause some delays depending on the current state of the fire.
