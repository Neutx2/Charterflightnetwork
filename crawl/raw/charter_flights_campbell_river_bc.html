You Will Fly By Private Charter From Vancouver To Campbell River In The Aircraft That Best Matches Your Group Size And Preferences
  • Campbell River Airport (YBL/CYBL) - Wheel Plane Charters arrive at Campbell River Airport, located 7 miles south of town. The airport features a 6,500-foot paved runway suitable for light twins through to executive jets. Van shuttle and taxi services are readily available for the short drive to your hotel or resort.
  • Floatplane operations in Campbell River are centered at Tyee Spit, a small peninsula situated just north of the downtown waterfront near Discovery Harbour Marina. From this location, charter operators including CorilAir Charters and Vancouver Island Air provide both sightseeing flights and scheduled floatplane transportation to destinations throughout the surrounding coastal region and nearby communities.
  • Helicopter Charter Flights to Campbell River operate from Tyee Spit as well as Campbell River Airport. The heliport on the spit is located a short distance north of the downtown core adjacent to Discovery Harbour Marina. Helicopter charter companies such as West Coast Helicopters operate from the spit, while Grizzly Helicopters operate from Campbell River Airport.

Vancouver to Campbell River Charter Flight FAQ

How long is a charter flight from Vancouver to Campbell River?

Most charter flights from the Vancouver area to Campbell River take approximately 45 to 60 minutes, depending on the aircraft type, departure airport, and weather conditions.

Which Vancouver airports can charter flights depart from?

Depending on aircraft type and operator availability, charter flights may depart from Vancouver International South Terminal, Vancouver Harbour, Boundary Bay, Pitt Meadows, Langley, or Abbotsford.

What types of charter aircraft are available for Campbell River?

Clients may request wheel-plane charters, float planes, helicopters, turboprops, piston aircraft, and in some cases executive jets, depending on passenger count and airport requirements.

How much does it cost to charter a flight to Campbell River?

The cost depends on the number of passengers, aircraft type, departure airport, routing, and whether you require one-way or return service. Charter Flight Network helps clients compare up to 3 competitive quotes from operators best suited to the trip.

Can I receive more than one charter quote?

Yes. When available, Charter Flight Network sources your request to multiple operators so you can compare aircraft options, schedules, and pricing.

Are Campbell River charter flights suitable for fishing trips?

Yes. This route is ideal for anglers and small groups who want to minimize travel time and maximize time on the water, especially for short salmon fishing getaways on Vancouver Island.
