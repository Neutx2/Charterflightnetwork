Flights to Cross Lake, Manitoba

About Cross Lake:

Cross Lake is one of northern Manitoba’s most important Indigenous communities, located on the Nelson River where it flows into Cross Lake. The community serves as a regional centre for local services, education, shopping, and transportation, making it more accessible than many fly-in destinations.

Cross Lake Services / Accommodations / Contacts:
Airport and Flight Access
  • Charter flights to Cross Lake arrive at Cross Lake (Charlie Sinclair Memorial) Airport, YCR / CYCR, located about one nautical mile east of the community. The airport’s 3,993-foot crushed rock runway supports a range of northern charter operations.
Accommodations and Local Services
  • Cross Lake has local lodging for visitors who need to stay overnight for work, family visits, or community events. Cross Lake Inn & Entertainment Centre advertises free WiFi, parking, pet-friendly rooms, and guest laundry in the heart of the community.
Things to Do in Cross Lake
  • Visitors to Cross Lake are drawn by boating, fishing, shoreline scenery, and community-centred northern outdoor life. Cross Lake also functions as a regional service centre, making it a useful base for travel throughout the surrounding Nelson River and lake country.

Flights to Cross Lake, MB FAQ

What airport serves Cross Lake, Manitoba?

Cross Lake is served by Cross Lake (Charlie Sinclair Memorial) Airport, with airport codes YCR and CYCR. The airport is about one nautical mile east of the community.

How long is the runway at Cross Lake Airport?

Cross Lake Airport has a crushed rock runway measuring about 3,993 feet by 98 feet, which supports a range of northern charter aircraft.

Are there hotel options in Cross Lake?

Yes. Cross Lake Inn & Entertainment Centre offers accommodations in Cross Lake and advertises free WiFi, parking, pet-friendly rooms, and guest laundry.

Can float plane or helicopter charters be arranged for Cross Lake?

Yes. Seasonal float plane service may be arranged for the Cross Lake region during open-water periods, and helicopter quotes can also be sourced from operators serving northern Manitoba.
