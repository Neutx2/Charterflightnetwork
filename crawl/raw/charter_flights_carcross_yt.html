About Carcross Yukon and Location

Carcross, Yukon, is a hidden gem located in the vast wilderness of Canada's Yukon Territory. Known for its stunning landscapes, historic significance, and plethora of outdoor activities, Carcross is an ideal destination for adventure seekers and nature enthusiasts. Access to Carcross is made convenient and efficient through charter flights, connecting visitors to this breathtakingly remote destination.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Carcross to Whitehorse

The flight from Carcross to Whitehorse covers a distance of 40 air miles, offering passengers a unique perspective of Yukon's natural beauty. This brief journey is a gateway to the wilderness, facilitated by convenient charter flights.

Carcross to Watson Lake

Spanning approximately 210 air miles, the flight from Carcross to Watson Lake is a scenic adventure over Yukon's diverse landscapes, quickly connecting two key locations.

Carcross to Yellowknife, NWT

Traveling from Carcross to Yellowknife involves covering around 690 air miles, providing an aerial view of the vast northern wilderness, a journey made seamless by charter flights.

Carcross to Faro, Yukon

The distance of 150 air miles from Carcross to Faro is traversed with ease thanks to charter flights, offering a unique opportunity to see the rugged beauty of Yukon from above.

Carcross to Anchorage, Alaska

A journey of about 520 air miles, the flight from Carcross to Anchorage bridges the scenic landscapes of Yukon and Alaska, making it an unforgettable experience through charter services.

Carcross Airport

The Carcross Airport, featuring a 2,500-foot runway, is conveniently situated just a short distance from the heart of Carcross. It serves as a vital link for travelers and offers essential services such as fueling and charter flight bookings, making it an integral part of the journey to and from this unique Yukon destination.

Other Nearby Airports
Whitehorse International Airport

As the largest airport in Yukon, Whitehorse International offers a 9,800-foot runway and comprehensive services, serving as a primary gateway to the territory.

Watson Lake Airport

Watson Lake Airport provides essential connectivity to southern Yukon, featuring a 5,500-foot runway and basic amenities for travelers.

Dawson City Airport

This airport connects the historic city of Dawson with the rest of Yukon, boasting a 4,000-foot runway and services tailored to tourists and residents alike.

Mayo Airport

Serving the central Yukon region, Mayo Airport has a 3,500-foot runway and supports the local mining industry as well as adventure tourism.

Carcross Hotels / Accommodations
The Carcross Cabin

Offering cozy accommodations nestled within the wilderness, The Carcross Cabin provides guests with a comfortable base to explore the surrounding natural beauty and engage in outdoor adventures.

Carcross Outdoor Adventures
Kayak Trips on Bennett Lake

Experience the serene beauty of Bennett Lake by kayak. These guided tours offer a unique way to explore the crystal-clear waters and scenic surroundings of Carcross.

Mountain Biking on Montana Mountain

For the adventure enthusiasts, Montana Mountain offers some of the best mountain biking trails in Yukon, with routes suitable for all skill levels.
