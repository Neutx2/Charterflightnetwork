About Summer Beaver Airport

The airport lies southwest of the community with a gravel runway suitable for turboprops such as the PC-12 and King Air 200. Floatplanes are common for set-outs to nearby lakes and camps in peak season.

Summer Beaver Charter Flights — FAQ

What aircraft are best for Summer Beaver?

Pilatus PC-12 and King Air 200 handle gravel runways well. For lakes/remote camps, a Beaver or Caravan on floats is common.

How long is the flight from Pickle Lake?

About 115 air miles—roughly 40–50 minutes in a turboprop depending on winds and payload.

Can you arrange floatplane set-outs?

Yes. Tell us dates, passenger count, and gear weight and we’ll source set-outs and camp moves.

Do you operate the aircraft?

No. We connect you with licensed operators who send quotes directly; you book with your preferred operator.
