BC Air 

Request a charter quote from BC Air for direct flights to Powell River, located on the Sunshine Coast of British Columbia on the mainland side of the Strait of Georgia, facing Vancouver Island. The surrounding waters are ideal for kayaking, canoeing, and boating. Desolation Sound Marine Provincial Park, north of Powell River, is a popular destination for boating and kayaking, known for its warm waters, sheltered coves, and stunning mountain backdrops.
