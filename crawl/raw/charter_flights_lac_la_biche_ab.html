About Lac La Biche Alberta and Location

Lac La Biche is a picturesque community in northeastern Alberta, known for its stunning lake views and abundant wildlife. This tranquil region offers a plethora of outdoor activities such as fishing, kayaking, and hiking in Lakeland Provincial Park. Its rich history and cultural diversity are showcased through local museums and heritage sites, making Lac La Biche a compelling destination for air charter and helicopter charter visitors seeking a blend of adventure and cultural exploration.

Distance From Major Cities
Lac La Biche to Calgary

Connecting Lac La Biche to Calgary, the journey spans approximately 560 kilometers (348 miles), a distance well-suited for private jet charters seeking direct and efficient travel options.

Lac La Biche to Edmonton

The distance from Lac La Biche to Edmonton is around 220 kilometers (137 miles), making it an ideal short trip for air charter services providing access to Alberta's capital.

Lac La Biche to Grande Prairie

Traveling from Lac La Biche to Grande Prairie covers a distance of about 650 kilometers (404 miles), a route easily navigated by charter flights connecting these northern communities.

Lac La Biche Airport

The Lac La Biche Airport features a 1,219-meter (4,000-foot) runway and is conveniently located just 5 kilometers (3 miles) west of the town. Offering facilities such as taxi services and car rentals, the airport is well-equipped to welcome visitors arriving by private jet or helicopter charter, facilitating effortless transitions to their local destinations.

Other Nearby Airports
St. Paul Municipal Airport

Approximately 95 kilometers (59 miles) from Lac La Biche, St. Paul Municipal Airport offers a 1,219-meter (4,000-foot) runway, accommodating a range of air charter services.

Cold Lake Regional Airport

Cold Lake Regional Airport, located about 150 kilometers (93 miles) northeast of Lac La Biche, features a 2,591-meter (8,500-foot) runway, supporting both military and civilian charter flights.

Athabasca Airport

Located 120 kilometers (75 miles) to the southwest, Athabasca Airport provides another convenient option for air travel, with a 914-meter (3,000-foot) runway.

Conklin (Leismer) Airport

About 200 kilometers (124 miles) south of Lac La Biche, Conklin (Leismer) Airport serves the region with a 1,220-meter (4,003-foot) runway, facilitating access to Alberta's oil sands and northern communities.
