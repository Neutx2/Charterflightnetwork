About Athabasca Alberta and Location

Athabasca, nestled along the scenic Athabasca River, is a vibrant community rich in history and natural beauty. Located in the heart of Alberta, it serves as a gateway to the great outdoors, offering visitors a plethora of activities ranging from river kayaking to historical exploration at the Athabasca Heritage Society. With easy access via air charter services, Athabasca is a prime destination for those seeking adventure or relaxation in Alberta's picturesque landscapes.

Distance From Major Cities
Athabasca to Calgary

The journey from Athabasca to Calgary covers approximately 335 kilometers (208 miles), making air charter services a convenient option for traversing the beautiful Alberta countryside to reach its largest city.

Athabasca to Edmonton

Located just 145 kilometers (90 miles) north of Edmonton, Athabasca is easily accessible by private jet charter, offering a quick and luxurious route to Alberta's capital.

Athabasca to Grande Prairie

Connecting Athabasca with Grande Prairie involves a distance of around 550 kilometers (342 miles), highlighting the efficiency of helicopter charters for direct and scenic travel across Alberta's vast landscapes.

Athabasca Airport

Athabasca Airport, with a runway length of 3,500 feet, supports a variety of air charter operations, including private jets and helicopters. Conveniently located close to the town, the airport offers travelers swift access to Athabasca's attractions. Although direct taxi and rental car services may be limited, arrangements can be made in advance to ensure smooth transportation to and from the airport.

Other Nearby Airports
Edmonton International Airport (YEG)

About 150 kilometers (93 miles) from Athabasca, Edmonton International Airport serves as a major hub for travelers looking to explore northern Alberta, with comprehensive facilities for a range of air charter services.

Slave Lake Airport (CYZH)

Approximately 200 kilometers (124 miles) northwest of Athabasca, Slave Lake Airport offers another convenient entry point for visitors to the region, supporting various charter flight operations.

Athabasca Hotels
Athabasca Lodge Motel

Offering comfort and convenience, the Athabasca Lodge Motel is known for its welcoming atmosphere and quality accommodations, making it a perfect base for exploring the surrounding area.

Days Inn by Wyndham Athabasca

The Days Inn provides guests with modern amenities and comfortable lodging, ensuring a pleasant stay whether you're in Athabasca for business or leisure.
