Charter Flights From Abbotsford

It is just over 500 air miles from Abbotsford to Prince Rupert, with an average flight time of  between 2 - 3 hours depending on the type of aircraft.  When you charter a flight, you pay for the aircraft to take you to Prince Rupert and then after dropping you off, you pay for the aircraft to return back to its base.  So for a one way flight from Abbotsford,  you would be paying for over 1000 miles of flying

Charter Flights From Dease Lake

It is just under 300 air miles from Prince Rupert to Dease Lake, with an average flight time of 1.5 - 2 hours depending on the type of aircraft.Float plane charters are available from both Prince Rupert and Dease Lake. If you need a wheel plane charter, the closest wheel plane charter service is located in Smithers.

Charter Flights Between Prince Rupert and Smithers

It is 140 air miles from Prince Rupert to Smithers with an average flight time of 45 minutes to 1 hour. You can arrange wheel plane, float plane as well as helicopter charter flights between Prince Rupert and Smithers.  Prince Rupert is well known for salmon fishing, whale watching as well as a jumping off point for grizzly bear tours in the Khutzeymateen Grizzly Bear Sanctuary.  Smithers is well known for exceptional steelhead fishing.

Charter Flights To Prince Rupert From Vancouver

Its 470 air miles from Vancouver to Prince Rupert with an average flight time of around two hours. The most practical aircraft type for this distance is a high performance, pressurized aircraft that will fly you well above most weather systems.

Business travellers can depart Vancouver in the morning, conduct a few hours of business in Prince Rupert and return home that evening.
