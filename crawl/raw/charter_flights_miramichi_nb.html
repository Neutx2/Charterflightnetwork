About Miramichi New Brunswick

Discover The Convenience and Affordability of Flying Direct to or From Miramichi New Brunswick In The Speed Comfort and Security of A Private Charter Flight Miramichi is located in the northeastern region of New Brunswick along the province's coastline and is about half way between Bathurst to the north and Buctouche to the south. The Miramichi airport is home base for the province's DNR and Air Tanker Operations. It is a public use airport 2 miles south of town with a 10,000 foot asphalt runway.
