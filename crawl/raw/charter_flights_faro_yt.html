About Faro, Yukon and Location

Located in the heart of the Yukon, Faro is a gateway to the pristine wilderness that defines Canada's North. This secluded town offers visitors a unique blend of natural beauty, wildlife, and quietude. Faro's stunning landscapes and the opportunity to explore untouched territories make it a perfect destination for those looking to escape the hustle and bustle of city life. With charter flights, reaching this remote haven has never been easier.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Faro to Whitehorse

A short charter flight of approximately 120 air miles connects Faro to Whitehorse, offering breathtaking views of Yukon's wilderness.

Faro to Watson Lake

Travelling the 215 air miles from Faro to Watson Lake by charter flight showcases the expansive beauty of the Yukon from above.

Faro to Yellowknife, NWT

Covering around 610 air miles, the journey from Faro to Yellowknife is a testament to the vastness and beauty of the Canadian North.

Faro to Anchorage, Alaska

The approximately 550 air mile flight to Anchorage, Alaska, crosses some of the most remote and breathtaking landscapes between Canada and the United States.

Faro Airport

Faro Airport, with its 4,000-foot runway, is conveniently located just outside the town. It offers essential services for adventurers and tourists alike, serving as the starting point for many seeking to explore the vast wilderness of the Yukon through charter flights.

Other Nearby Airports
Whitehorse International Airport

This major hub, with a runway length of over 9,500 feet, serves as the gateway to the Yukon, providing numerous services and connections to domestic and international destinations.

Watson Lake Airport

Located in the southeastern Yukon, Watson Lake Airport offers access to the scenic Wye Lake and the Sign Post Forest, a popular tourist attraction.

Dawson City Airport

Dawson City Airport, near the historic gold rush town, facilitates access to the Klondike Gold Fields and the Tombstone Territorial Park.

Mayo Airport

Mayo Airport serves the Silver Trail region and provides access to the beautiful Stewart River, a key location for silver mining history and outdoor adventures.

Faro Hotels / Accommodations
Hotel Faro

Hotel Faro provides cozy accommodations with stunning views of the surrounding wilderness, making it a perfect base for exploring the Yukon's natural beauty.

Faro Lodge

Offering comfortable lodging and local cuisine, Faro Lodge is a warm and welcoming place to stay while experiencing the outdoor adventures Faro has to offer.

Faro Outdoor Adventures
Kayak and Canoe Excursions

Embark on guided kayak and canoe trips through the pristine waterways surrounding Faro. These adventures offer intimate encounters with Yukon's spectacular nature and wildlife.

Aurora Viewing
