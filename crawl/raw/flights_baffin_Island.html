Charter Flight Network connects travelers with private charter flights throughout every province and territory of Canada, offering up to three competitive charter quotes directly from the airlines.

Panorama Aviation

Panorama Aviation’s Beechcraft 1900D is ideal for larger crews, carrying up to eighteen passengers or mixed loads on commuter-style charters connecting regional hubs with remote job sites and communities.

Air Nunavut

Air Nunavut connects remote Arctic communities with dependable King Air service capable of short, gravel-strip landings—ideal for urgent travel, cargo runs, and government missions across Nunavut’s vast northern territory.

North Star Air

With more than 20 aircraft, North Star Air provides cost-effective charter and scheduled flights linking northern communities throughout Canada’s remote regions.

Kenn Borek Air

Kenn Borek Air specializes in remote aviation, supporting scientific expeditions across the Arctic and Antarctic with rugged aircraft capable of landing in extreme environments.

Superior Airways

Superior Airways provides reliable northern travel for business teams needing same-day access to remote communities, offering point-to-point efficiency not found with major airlines.

Fast Air

With a fleet of over 30 jets and turboprops, Fast Air provides charter solutions tailored to every schedule and destination throughout Baffin Island and across Canada.

Perimeter Airlines

With Perimeter Airlines, your charter itinerary is built around your needs — custom routes, flexible timing, and dedicated support from booking to boarding.

Chrono Aviation

Arctic charter operations require aircraft that handle gravel strips, blowing snow, and extreme cold. Our northern fleet supports researchers, mining crews, and community travel across Nunavut, the High Arctic, and remote coastal regions.

Trade North Aviation

Based in Winnipeg and Sandy Lake, Trade North Aviation offers flexible charter flights for business, construction, fishing, and First Nations travel throughout the north.
