Helicocpter Flights To Cabot Links
Location:

Cabot Links is located in Inverness, a picturesque town on the western coast of Cape Breton Island in Nova Scotia, Canada. It overlooks the Gulf of St. Lawrence, offering stunning views of the ocean and the rugged coastline.

History:

Cabot Links opened in 2012 as Canada's first true links golf course, designed to emulate the traditional seaside courses of Scotland and Ireland. The project was part of an effort to boost tourism and economic development in the Inverness area, which was historically known for its coal mining industry. The course quickly gained international acclaim for its scenic beauty and challenging layout, attracting golf enthusiasts from around the world.

Location:

Travelers can reach Cabot Links by air or road. Sydney International Airport is located 60 air miles east of Cabot Links. The Halifax Stanfield International Airport is located approximately 145 air miles southeast Cabot Links.  Helicopter charter flights are available from both Sydney and Halifax.  Golfers can take commercial scheduled airllines to both Halifax and Sydney.  From Halifax, visitors can rent a car and drive along the scenic Cabot Trail to reach Inverness. Alternatively,  guests can fly charter flights or corporate aircraft to the Port Hawkesbury airport, and then take the ground shuttle for the 1 hour 10 minute drive to Cabot Links. Additionally, some visitors may choose to drive to Inverness from other parts of Nova Scotia or take a ferry from Prince Edward Island.

Accommodations:

Cabot Links offers a range of accommodations to suit different preferences and budgets. The resort features luxurious rooms and suites at the Cabot Links Lodge, which overlooks the golf course and the ocean. Guests can also stay in private villas and cottages located near the course, providing additional space and privacy. Additionally, there are several options for dining and relaxation, including restaurants, bars, and spa facilities on-site.

About the Cabot Links Golf Course:

Design: Cabot Links was designed by renowned golf course architect Rod Whitman, with a focus on preserving the natural landscape and incorporating traditional links-style elements. The course features rolling dunes, deep bunkers, and undulating fairways, providing a challenging yet rewarding golfing experience.
Championship Layout: The course spans over 7,000 yards from the championship tees and offers a variety of tee boxes to accommodate players of all skill levels. Golfers can expect to encounter strategic challenges, including wind, elevation changes, and strategically placed hazards, as they navigate the course.
Scenic Beauty: One of the highlights of playing at Cabot Links is the breathtaking scenery, with panoramic views of the ocean and the surrounding coastline from almost every hole. The course's seaside location adds to the overall experience, creating a memorable and immersive golfing environment. Facilities: In addition to the 18-hole championship course, Cabot Links also features practice facilities, including a driving range, putting greens, and short game areas. Golfers can also take advantage of professional instruction and clinics to improve their game during their stay.
Recognition: Since its opening, Cabot Links has received numerous accolades and awards, including being ranked among the top golf courses in Canada and the world by leading golf publications and organizations. Its reputation as a world-class golf destination continues to attract golfers seeking an unforgettable experience on the links.

Travel Option #1 - Departures From Sydney NS

#1. Sydney to Cabot Links

Your Most Cost Efficient Departure Point For Helicopter Charters To Cabot Links

Sydney Nova Scotia is located on the northeast coastline of Cape Breton Island and just 52 air miles east of the Cabot Links Golf Resort, Inverness

Sydney is easily accessible by scheduled airlines from multiple cities throughout Canada and USA, with non stop flights from Montreal, Quebec.

Upon arrival in Sydney, guests connect with the pre arranged helicopter charter for the 20 minute flight direct to Cabot Links.

If you prefer to drive, you can choose between the pre arranged van service with driver or reserving a rental car.  Drive time from Sydney to the Cabot Golf Resort averages 2 hours.

Option #2 - Helicopter Charter From Halifax

#2. Halifax to Cabot Links

Golfers Have A Wide Selection Of Non Stop Flights From Canadian And US Cities

Halifax is located on the east coast of Nova Scotia, 110 air miles south of the Cabot Links Golf Resort.

Halifax is easily accessible by scheduled airlines from multiple cities throughout Canada, with non stop flights from Calgary, Edmonton, Montreal, Ottawa, Toronto, Vancouver and Winnipeg.

If you are travelling from the USA, there are non stop flights to Halifax from Boston, Chicago, New York and Newark.  Upon arrival in Halifax from the USA you will be required to clear Canadian Customs. 

Golfers have the option of taking Helicopter charters from Halifax to Cabot Golf Resort or ground transportation which includes Van Service with driver or rental car.  

Helicopter charters are available for 2, 4, & 8 passengers.  Flight time from Halifax to Cabot Golf Resort averages 1 hr 10 minutes to 1 hour 20 minutes  

If you choose ground transportation, drive time will average between 3.5 - 4 hours.

Option #3 - Port Hawkesbury To Cabot Links

#3. Port Hawkesbury to Cabot Links

Port Hawkesbury Is The Closest Airport To Cabot Links

Although there are no scheduled airline flights to Port Hawkesbury, the airport is a popular choice for golfers who fly in private, corporate or charter aircraft.

Aircraft arriving from the US are able to clear Canadian Customs upon arrival in Port Hawkesbury.

Upon arrival in Port Hawkesbury and after clearing Canadian Customs for US flights, you will connect with the pre arranged van or car service for the 1 hour scenic drive to the Cabot Golf Resort.
