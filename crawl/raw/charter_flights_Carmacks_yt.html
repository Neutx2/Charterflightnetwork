About Carmacks Yukon and Location

Located in the heart of Yukon, Carmacks offers a unique blend of history, nature, and adventure. Known for its stunning landscapes along the Yukon River, Carmacks is a prime destination for those seeking to explore the great outdoors. Charter flights provide exclusive access to this remote gem, offering a seamless journey to the heart of Yukon’s wilderness.

Carmacks Airport

Carmacks Airport features a 1,500-foot runway, strategically located to provide easy access to the town and its surrounding wilderness. Despite its remote location, the airport is equipped with necessary facilities to welcome visitors arriving by charter flights, serving as a gateway to the numerous outdoor adventures Carmacks offers.

Other Nearby Airports
Erik Nielsen Whitehorse International Airport

This major hub with an 8,200-foot runway is your gateway to Yukon, offering flights to and from major cities across Canada and charter flights to remote locations like Carmacks.

Dawson City Airport

Connecting the historic town of Dawson City with the rest of Yukon, this airport features a 4,500-foot runway and services for both commercial and charter flights.

Mayo Airport

Serving the Silver Trail region, Mayo Airport provides essential connectivity with a 3,000-foot runway, accommodating a range of aircraft, including charter flights.

Teslin Airport

Located near the scenic Teslin Lake, Teslin Airport offers a 5,000-foot runway and access to one of Yukon’s most picturesque regions, with charter flights enhancing the travel experience.

Carmacks Hotels / Accommodations
Riverfront Lodge

Overlooking the majestic Yukon River, Riverfront Lodge offers guests a tranquil retreat with easy access to Carmacks’ best outdoor activities, perfect for adventurers and nature lovers alike.

Carmacks Outdoor Adventures

Northern Lights Viewing
