About Tulita Northwest Territories and Location

Tulita, located at the confluence of the Great Bear River and the Mackenzie River in the Northwest Territories, is a community rich in history and natural beauty. Known for its breathtaking landscapes, including the Bear Rock, and its abundant wildlife, Tulita is a haven for outdoor enthusiasts and culture seekers. Accessible primarily by charter flights, it offers a unique opportunity to explore the remote beauty of the North, making it an ideal destination for those looking to escape the hustle and bustle of city life.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Tulita to Yellowknife

The journey from Tulita to Yellowknife is approximately 620 miles (998 kilometers), showcasing the vastness of the Northwest Territories and its diverse ecosystems.

Tulita to Edmonton

Traveling from Tulita to Edmonton covers around 1,080 miles (1,738 kilometers), connecting adventurers to one of Canada's northernmost communities.

Tulita to Norman Wells

The short distance of about 75 miles (121 kilometers) from Tulita to Norman Wells emphasizes the close-knit nature of communities within the Northwest Territories.

Tulita to Fort Simpson

Spanning approximately 300 miles (483 kilometers), the route from Tulita to Fort Simpson traverses through some of the most scenic landscapes in the region.

Tulita Airport

Tulita Airport, with a runway length of 3,500 feet (1,067 meters), is an essential gateway for the community, providing access to and from the area. Located just a short drive from the town center, the airport offers vital services including charter flights that connect Tulita with the rest of the Northwest Territories and beyond. This makes it a key component in promoting tourism and supporting local development.

Other Nearby Airports
Norman Wells Airport

Norman Wells Airport serves as a regional hub for the Sahtu Region, offering flights to various destinations within the Northwest Territories and facilitating the movement of goods and people.

Yellowknife Airport

As the main air transport facility in the Northwest Territories, Yellowknife Airport provides comprehensive services and connections to both national and international destinations.

Fort Simpson Airport

Fort Simpson Airport is crucial for accessing the Nahanni National Park Reserve, offering services that cater to tourists and residents alike, and supporting the local tourism industry.

Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport

Located in the western Arctic, Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport is a key entry point for exploring the Arctic coast and the Beaufort Delta region, offering unique opportunities for Arctic adventures.

Tulita Hotels / Accommodations
River Lodge

River Lodge in Tulita provides cozy and comfortable accommodations, perfect for relaxing after a day of adventure in the wilderness. Its prime location offers stunning views and easy access to local attractions.

Tulita Inn

Tulita Inn offers visitors a warm welcome and a comfortable stay, with amenities designed to make your Arctic experience unforgettable. Located near the town center, it's a convenient base for exploring the area.

Tulita Outdoor Adventures

Tulita is an outdoor enthusiast's paradise, offering a wide range of activities including fishing in the Mackenzie River, hiking around Bear Rock, kayaking, and experiencing the vibrant culture of the local Sahtu Dene. Winter brings the magic of snowshoeing, ice fishing, and northern lights viewing. Local outfitters, such as Great Bear Adventures, provide guided tours and outfitting services.
