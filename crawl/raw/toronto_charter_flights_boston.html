Direct Flights To Bedford (BED)

Charter flights from Toronto to the Boston region (approximately 420 air miles) offer business travelers a fast, direct, and efficient way to reach one of the United States’ most influential commercial and academic centers. Passengers can depart from Toronto Pearson (YYZ) aboard a wide range of business jets, or from Billy Bishop Toronto City Airport (YTZ) using turboprop aircraft, with many flights arriving at Bedford, Massachusetts (BED)—Boston’s most popular executive airport. From Bedford, ground transportation to downtown Boston is simple and efficient, with limousine services, rental cars, and rideshare options covering the 20–25 mile trip in roughly 30–40 minutes depending on traffic.

Boston Logan International Airport (BOS)

Clients may also choose to fly directly to Boston Logan International (BOS); however, Logan’s higher landing fees, heavy airline congestion, and frequent delays can add time and cost to the journey. Many travelers prefer Bedford for its convenience, privacy, and quick turnaround times.
