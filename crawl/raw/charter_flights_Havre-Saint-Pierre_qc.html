Charter Flights From Quebec City To Havre-Saint-Pierre

Charter flights are available from Quebec City to Havre-Saint-Pierre which is a distance of 430 air miles. Depending on the type of aircraft available a one way charter flight will cost between $6000 - $8000 plus fuel surcharges, airport landing fees and tax. The distance to Halifax is just 375 air miles, so it would may be cheaper taking a charter flight to Halifax if you need to connect with a scheduled air service to Toronto or USA.

You can also book float plane charter flights out of Havre-Saint-Pierre direct to dozens of remote fly-in fishing lodges in Eastern Quebec and Labrador. We can provide you with details on how you can fly scheduled airlines into Halifax from Toronto or USA, connect with a waiting twin engine wheel aircraft for a direct flight to Havre-Saint-Pierre. Upon arrival, you then connect with the float plane for the final leg of the journey direct to the remote lodge or outpost camp. Air Tunilik operates a float plane base out of Havre-Saint-Pierre as well as out of nearby Natashquan

Location

Havre-Saint-Pierre is located in south eastern Quebec along the north shore at the mouth of the St Lawrence River and just north of Anticosti Island. Charter flights are regularly operated from Goose Bay Labrador, points within Newfoundland and Quebec.

Havre-Saint-Pierre Airport

The local public use airport is 3.5 miles north of Havre-Saint-Pierre and has a 4,500 foot asphalt runway.
