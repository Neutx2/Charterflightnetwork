Charter Flights To Mayo
About Mayo Yukon and Location

Mayo, located in the heart of Yukon, serves as the gateway to the pristine wilderness of the Silver Trail region. This charming town is a hub for outdoor enthusiasts, offering unparalleled opportunities for fishing, hiking, and the chance to witness the spectacular Northern Lights. Charter flights to Mayo are the ideal way to access this remote destination, offering travelers a unique glimpse into the Yukon's breathtaking landscapes and rich cultural heritage.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Mayo to Whitehorse

The flight from Mayo to Whitehorse covers approximately 200 air miles, providing a quick and scenic route to Yukon's capital.

Mayo to Watson Lake

Traveling from Mayo to Watson Lake spans about 340 air miles, offering passengers views of Yukon's diverse landscapes.

Mayo to Yellowknife, NWT

The aerial distance from Mayo to Yellowknife is roughly 680 air miles, showcasing the vast and untouched northern wilderness.

Mayo to Faro, Yukon

Mayo is just 125 air miles from Faro, with flights providing an intimate look at the rugged beauty of the Yukon Territory.

Mayo to Anchorage, Alaska

Connecting Mayo to Anchorage involves a journey of approximately 480 air miles, crossing some of the most stunning landscapes in North America.

Mayo Airport

Mayo Airport features a 4,800-foot runway, accommodating a variety of charter flights that connect this remote town with the rest of Yukon and beyond. Located conveniently close to the town center, the airport offers essential services to travelers and aircraft alike, making it a key part of Mayo's accessibility.

Other Nearby Airports
Dawson City Airport

This airport, with its 5,000-foot runway, serves as a crucial link to the Klondike Gold Rush city, offering scheduled and charter flight services.

Erik Nielsen Whitehorse International Airport

As Yukon's primary air gateway, this international airport boasts a runway over 9,000 feet long and provides comprehensive services to travelers and aircraft.

Old Crow Airport

Located in the only Yukon community accessible solely by air, Old Crow Airport has a 4,500-foot runway and is essential for the transport of goods and people.

Carmacks Airport

Though smaller, Carmacks Airport plays a vital role in connecting this community to the rest of Yukon, featuring a 3,000-foot runway.

Mayo Hotels / Accommodations
Yukon River Lodge

This lodge offers cozy accommodations with stunning river views, catering to visitors seeking comfort and wilderness adventure.

Mayo Mountain Inn

Located in the heart of Mayo, this inn provides a welcoming stay with easy access to local attractions and outdoor activities.

Mayo Outdoor Adventures
Fishing Expeditions

Explore the rich waters of Mayo for unparalleled fishing experiences, guided by local experts familiar with the best spots.

Hiking Trails

From leisurely walks to challenging hikes, Mayo offers trails that showcase the stunning landscapes and wildlife of Yukon.
