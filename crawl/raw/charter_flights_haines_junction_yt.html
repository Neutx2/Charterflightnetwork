About Haines Junction Yukon and Location

Haines Junction is a captivating gateway to the Kluane National Park & Reserve, offering breathtaking views of the Saint Elias Mountains. This community in Yukon is an ideal spot for nature lovers, providing easy access to outdoor activities such as hiking, wildlife viewing, and glacier exploration. Charter flights to Haines Junction make this remote beauty accessible, inviting adventurers to explore its vast landscapes and rich cultural heritage.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Haines Junction to Whitehorse

The distance in air miles from Haines Junction to Whitehorse is approximately 85 miles. The flight offers stunning aerial views of Yukon's wilderness, making it a memorable journey.

Haines Junction to Watson Lake

Around 305 air miles separate Haines Junction from Watson Lake, with charter flights providing a direct and scenic route between these destinations.

Haines Junction to Yellowknife, NWT

The trip from Haines Junction to Yellowknife covers roughly 770 air miles, featuring views of the vast northern landscapes.

Haines Junction to Faro, Yukon

Traveling from Haines Junction to Faro, Yukon spans about 170 air miles, offering passengers breathtaking views of the untouched wilderness.

Haines Junction to Anchorage, Alaska

The aerial distance to Anchorage from Haines Junction is around 420 air miles, showcasing the rugged beauty of the Alaskan and Yukon territories.

Haines Junction Airport

Haines Junction Airport features a 5,000-foot runway, strategically located just a few miles from the town center. It serves as a pivotal point for charter flights into the area, providing essential services for adventurers and explorers aiming to discover the wonders of Yukon and the surrounding regions.

Other Nearby Airports
Erik Nielsen Whitehorse International Airport

As the largest airport in Yukon, it offers comprehensive services and a runway that accommodates international and domestic flights, including charters to remote regions like Haines Junction.

Dawson City Airport

This airport connects the historic gold rush town with the rest of Yukon, featuring a runway suitable for various types of aircraft and providing essential services for travelers.

Old Crow Airport

Located in the community of Old Crow, this airport is vital for connecting this remote area with the rest of Yukon, offering scheduled and charter flight options.

Mayo Airport

Serving the Silver Trail region, Mayo Airport supports regional travel with a runway fit for small to medium-sized aircraft, facilitating access to Yukon's interior.

Located near the entrance to Kluane National Park, this inn offers convenient access to outdoor adventures, complete with modern amenities for a restful stay.

Haines Junction is located in south western Yukon - west of Whitehorse at the Junction of the Haines Highway and mile 1016 of the Alaska Highway. The town is served by a public use airport located 2 miles northwest of town. The single gravel runway is 5,000 feet long - making it accessible by most of the charter aircraft that operate in the region.
