About Fort McPherson Northwest Territories and Location

Fort McPherson sits as a testament to the rugged beauty of the Northwest Territories. This tranquil town, nestled on the Peel River, is a gateway to the Arctic's splendors, offering a unique blend of cultural heritage and natural beauty. Visitors are drawn to its scenic vistas, rich Gwich'in culture, and the spectacular Dempster Highway. The strategic use of charter flights opens up this remote gem, providing a seamless gateway to untold adventures.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Fort McPherson to Yellowknife

Traveling from Fort McPherson to Yellowknife covers a distance of approximately 650 miles (1046 kilometers), showcasing the expansive wilderness that charter flights navigate to connect these distant communities.

Fort McPherson to Edmonton

The journey stretches over 900 miles (1448 kilometers) to Edmonton, illustrating the critical role of air travel in bridging vast distances across the Northwest Territories.

Fort McPherson to Norman Wells

The aerial route to Norman Wells spans about 350 miles (563 kilometers), offering stunning aerial views of the region's landscape, accessible through charter services.

Fort McPherson to Fort Simpson

Connecting Fort McPherson with Fort Simpson involves a distance of roughly 500 miles (805 kilometers), with charter flights providing vital links between these remote locations.

Fort McPherson Airport

Fort McPherson Airport is a cornerstone for community access and connectivity, featuring a 3,000-foot runway that accommodates a variety of aircraft. Situated merely a short drive from the town, the airport offers essential services including logistics and charter flight arrangements, facilitating both local and international travel.

Other Nearby Airports
Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport

Located in the heart of the Western Arctic, Inuvik Airport serves as a crucial hub with a runway of 6,000 feet, offering services that include car rentals, accommodations, and direct connections to other northern communities.

Tuktoyaktuk James Gruben Airport

This airport, with its 4,500-foot runway, is pivotal for accessing the coastal community of Tuktoyaktuk, providing essential cargo and passenger services amidst the Arctic landscape.

Norman Wells Airport

Norman Wells Airport, with a 5,500-foot runway, is an indispensable link for the Sahtu Region, facilitating the movement of goods and people with its scheduled and charter flight services.

Fort Simpson Airport

As a gateway to the Nahanni National Park Reserve, Fort Simpson Airport boasts a 4,000-foot runway and provides a range of services, including charter flights and adventure tourism connections.

Fort McPherson Hotels / Accommodations
Peel River Inn

The Peel River Inn offers guests a comfortable stay with stunning views of the surrounding landscapes, providing a cozy base for exploring the natural beauty and cultural heritage of Fort McPherson.
