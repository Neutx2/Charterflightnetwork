Location

Kangirsuk is a remote community located in the far north central regions of Quebec. You will find Kangirsuk on the north west shore of Ungava Bay just south of Hudson Strait.

Kangirsuk Airport

Charter flights are available to the local public use airport which is located adjacent to the town. The single grravel runway is 3,500 feet long and sees just under 2,000 aircraft movements each year.
