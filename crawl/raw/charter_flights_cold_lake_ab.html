About Cold Lake, Alberta and Location

Cold Lake, Alberta, renowned for its crystal-clear waters and vibrant community, sits as a jewel in the northeastern part of the province. This picturesque city, surrounded by provincial parks and natural reserves, is a haven for outdoor enthusiasts and adventure seekers. Cold Lake is ideal for charter flights, private jet charter, helicopter charter, and air charter services, offering a myriad of activities such as fishing, boating, and hiking in the summer, and ice fishing and cross-country skiing in the winter. Its blend of natural beauty and modern amenities makes it a perfect destination for both relaxation and adventure.

Distance From Major Cities
Cold Lake to Calgary

The distance from Cold Lake to Calgary is approximately 550 miles (885 kilometers), a testament to the expansive beauty of Alberta, making it a scenic journey for air charter passengers exploring the province.

Cold Lake to Edmonton

About 180 miles (290 kilometers) separate Cold Lake from Edmonton, offering a direct route for those utilizing air charter services to experience the capital's attractions before escaping to the tranquility of Cold Lake.

Cold Lake to Grande Prairie

Linking Cold Lake with Grande Prairie, the distance is around 400 miles (644 kilometers), showcasing Alberta's diverse northern landscapes, perfect for adventurers and professionals seeking to explore by air.

Cold Lake Airport

Cold Lake Airport serves the community with a runway that accommodates a variety of aircraft, making it a key access point for visitors and locals alike. Located near the city, it offers essential connectivity to Cold Lake's attractions. Taxi and rental car services are available, providing convenient transportation options for travelers.

Other Nearby Airports
Lloydminster Airport

About 120 air miles southwest of Cold Lake, Lloydminster Airport offers additional access for travelers to and from the border region of Alberta and Saskatchewan, supporting charter flights and general aviation.

Bonnyville Airport

Located approximately 25 air miles west of Cold Lake, Bonnyville Airport is a smaller facility that caters to air charter services and general aviation, offering another option for accessing the Cold Lake area.

St. Paul Airport

Roughly 60 air miles south of Cold Lake, St. Paul Airport serves the local community and visiting air charter clients, providing a convenient stopover for those traveling to or from the central regions of Alberta.

Elk Point Airport

Just over 50 air miles southeast of Cold Lake, Elk Point Airport accommodates general aviation and charter flights, serving as a gateway to Alberta's northeastern communities and the surrounding wilderness areas.

Cold Lake Hotels
Lakeshore Inn

The Lakeshore Inn in Cold Lake offers guests a 3-star accommodation experience, with stunning views of the lake and easy access to local attractions, making it ideal for those arriving by air charter seeking comfort and convenience.

Marina View Hotel

With its 4-star rating, the Marina View Hotel provides luxurious accommodations and top-tier services, catering to guests looking for an upscale stay in Cold Lake, including those visiting via private jet or helicopter.

Cold Lake Motel

A cozy and affordable 2-star option, Cold Lake Motel offers clean, comfortable rooms for travelers exploring the area’s natural beauty and participating in outdoor activities.

Hamilton House Bed & Breakfast

As a charming 4-star bed & breakfast, Hamilton House offers a unique lodging experience in Cold Lake, featuring gourmet breakfasts and personalized service, perfect for guests seeking a home-away-from-home atmosphere.
