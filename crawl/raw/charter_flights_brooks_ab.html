About Brooks Alberta and Location

Brooks, Alberta, positioned in the expansive Canadian Prairies, is a dynamic city where cultural diversity and rich history converge amidst stunning natural landscapes. Attractions like Dinosaur Provincial Park, Lake Newell for aquatic activities, and the Brooks Aqueduct offer visitors a glimpse into the past and present of this vibrant area. Cultural festivals and local markets underscore Brooks's community spirit, making it an inviting destination for all.

Distance From Major Cities
Brooks to Calgary

At a distance of 186 kilometers (115 miles), Calgary is a short drive from Brooks, offering an easy route to one of Alberta's largest cities and its many urban and natural attractions.

Brooks to Edmonton

Brooks sits approximately 295 kilometers (183 miles) from Edmonton, allowing travelers to explore the diverse landscapes of Alberta from the prairies to the provincial capital.

Brooks to Grande Prairie

Connecting Brooks to Grande Prairie involves a journey of about 720 kilometers (447 miles), showcasing Alberta's vast and varied geography.

Brooks Airport

Brooks Airport (CYBP) serves the local area with a 3,937-foot runway, accommodating private and corporate aircraft. Located just 5 kilometers from downtown Brooks, the airport offers convenient access to the city. While direct taxi and rental car services from the airport are limited, arrangements can be made in advance to ensure seamless travel into Brooks.

Other Nearby Airports
Medicine Hat Airport (YXH)

Located 110 kilometers (68 miles) southeast of Brooks, Medicine Hat Airport features a 5,000-foot runway, serving as a gateway to southeastern Alberta. Approximately 70 air miles from Brooks, it offers scheduled and charter services.

Lethbridge Airport (YQL)

Approximately 160 kilometers (99 miles) southwest of Brooks, Lethbridge Airport has a 5,500-foot runway. Situated 100 air miles from Brooks, it connects the southern region of Alberta with both commercial and charter flights.

Brooks Hotels
Lakeview Inn & Suites Brooks

Offering comfort and convenience, Lakeview Inn & Suites in Brooks provides guests with well-appointed rooms, complimentary breakfast, and a fitness center. This 3-star hotel is a favorite for both business and leisure travelers.

Heritage Inn & Suites Brooks

The Heritage Inn & Suites features a modern design, spacious rooms, an indoor pool, and conference facilities. Its 4-star rating guarantees a luxurious stay in Brooks.

Canalta Brooks

With a commitment to friendly service, Canalta Brooks offers a comfortable stay with amenities like an indoor pool, hot tub, and free Wi-Fi. This 3-star hotel is ideal for families and business travelers alike.

Ramada by Wyndham Brooks

Located conveniently near local attractions, Ramada by Wyndham Brooks provides guests with a relaxing stay, featuring an indoor pool, water slide, and fitness center, all within a welcoming 4-star setting.
