Nova Scotia Charter Flights For Business and Personal Travel

Specializing In Small Groups 4 - 6 Golfers

Although we specialize in groups of 2 - 6 golfers, we can just as easily handle groups of 12 - 20 or more. 90% of our trips however are small corporate groups taking clients or key employees on a 2 - 3 day golf getaway. Typically they want to depart early Friday morning, get in a round of golf Friday afternoon, golf Saturday and Sunday - then return home either Sunday night or Monday morning.

Helicopter Shuttle From Airport To Golf Course

Most of the Cape Breton Highland Links require a 2 hour drive from the Sydney Nova Scotia Airport. For those that prefer to spend more time on the green and less time traveling, you have the option of a helicopter shuttle flight that will meet your group upon arrival at the Sydney Airport and then fly you direct to the Highland Links or other course of your choice. The helicopter shuttle to Highland Links averages just over 20 minutes.

Ground Transportation

Many of Nova Scotia's and Cape Breton's Golf Courses are just a short distance from the airport making it more practical to use ground transportation from the airport.

We can arrange rental car, limo services or van shuttle services and have it ready and waiting upon arrival of your charter flight.
