If you have a business meeting in Vancouver, you can save time with a direct charter flight to the Vancouver Airport that is most convenient to your meeting destination.  You can arrive at the south terminal of the Vancouver International Airport, or if more convenient you can take a direct charter flight to Boundary Bay, Pitt Meadows, Abbotsford or any airport in the lower mainland region.

Kamloops to Calgary

It is is aproximately 280 air miles from Kamloops to Calgary and depending on the aircraft this will take between 1 - 1.5 hours flight time. You can depart Kamloops in the morning, conduct your business and return home that afternoon.

Wheel plane charter flight are now available from the Kamloops airport to destinations across BC, throughout all of Canada as well as into the USA.
