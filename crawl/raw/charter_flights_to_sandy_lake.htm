About Flights To Sandy Lake
Location

Sandy Lake is an isolated community located north of Red Lake. The only access is by air, except for a short few weeks during the winter, when winter roads are accessible. The information on this page is designed to help you make your best decision on available charter flights to Sandy Lake.

If you are looking for float plane charters to remote lakes and rivers north of Red Lake, Sandy Lake Seaplanes may be your best option. They are based in the First Nations Community of Sandy Lake. Use our Charter Quote Request Service for price quotes and availability.

Sandy Lake Airport

The Sandy Lake Airport is located adjacent to the community and has a 3,500 gravel runway
