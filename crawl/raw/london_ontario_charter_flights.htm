About Charter Flights To London, Ontario
Location

London Ontario is located about half way between Toronto and Windsor along Highway 401. The city offers an exceptional variety of hotels, restaurants, attractions and activities for both the business and vacation traveler. Both Air Canada and WestJet offer scheduled flights to London, however if you prefer to set your own schedule and travel times - contact any of the featured Air Charter companies listed on the adjacent map for a no obligation charter quote

London, Ontario Airport

The London Ontario International airport is located about 6 miles northeast of downtown London Ontario and is served by several scheduled airlines including Air Canada, Flair Airlines, Swoop and WestJet. The airport is also classified as an airport of entry and is staffed by the Canada Border Services Agency. This makes it very convenient for business travellers who need to take corporate charter flights between London and USA destinations such as Chicago, Teterboro, Boston, Cleveland, etc. Passengers can depart any USA city and fly direct to London, Ontario and clear Canadian Customs at the London airport.

The airport features two asphalt runways the longest of which is 8,800 feet making the airport easily accessible to all sizes of charter aircraft, turbo props and jets.

Taxis, limo services and rental cars are available for ground transportation to and from the airport. Local taxi services can be contacted for pick-up or drop-off, and rental cars can be arranged through your air charter service provider.

Charter Flights From London to Ottawa

A private charter flight may be your most convenient travel option from London, Ontario to Ottawa - especially if you only have a few hours of business to conduct in Ottawa.  You can expect the flight costs to be more than what you would pay for a scheduled airline seat, but if the convenience of departing in the morning and returning back to London that same afternoon is important to your work schedule, then the extra costs may well be worth it.
