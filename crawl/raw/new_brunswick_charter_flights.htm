Some Helpful Information For Anyone Travelling To New Brunswick

Business And Corporate Charter Flights
When success depends on being in the right place at the right time, a dedicated charter flight can provide you with the confidence of knowing that you will arrive at your appointment on time, refreshed and ready to meet the challenges of the day. Whether your meeting is in Moncton, St John, Grand Falls, Bathurst or any of the more than 2 dozen charter flight destinations across New Brunswick a business friendly charter flight can transport your sales or service team quickly and affordably and have them back home in time for supper that evening.

Golf Charter Flights
Ar you planning a golf vacation on Canada's East Coast in the near future? New Brunswick which has more than 30 challenging golf courses to choose from is a perfect golfing destination for a group of fiends or business associates. And with more than two dozen airports across the province you can save time when you travel in the speed, comfort and security of a Golfer Friendly Charter Flight. Some of the top destinations are Moncton, Petitcodiac, Saint John, St Andrews, Sussex, Miramichi and Grand Falls.

Group Charter Flights
Do you have a meeting or small convention planned that requires travel for 10 or more delegates? If departure for the entire group can be arranged from one airport, you can avoid the hassles of travel logistics, plus save time and money with a group charter flight. Many of our charter network members specialize in groups of 10 - 200 or more and will make the travel portion of your program, the smoothest par of your upcoming meeting or convention.

Sport Fishing Charter Flights
New Brunswick is unique when it comes to catering to the sports fisherman. While many other provinces promote fly-in float plane trips to remote regions in order to experience the best fishing - you don't need to go to all that trouble and expense to enjoy some of North America's most exciting lake, river and coastal waters fishing. Each year thousands of fishermen come here for some of North America's best Atlantic Salmon fishing - but also smallmouth bass, shark, shad, brook trout, landlocked salmon. Many of the rivers are considered as a fly fisherman's paradise. Check out our interactive map below for the best fishing locations and travel details.
