About Dawson City, Yukon and Location

Dawson City, a gem in the Yukon, offers breathtaking wilderness, rich Klondike Gold Rush history, and indigenous culture. Its remote location is perfect for those seeking adventure, offering activities like gold panning, hiking, and viewing the northern lights. Charter flights to Dawson City bring you closer to this unique destination.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Dawson City to Whitehorse

The journey spans approximately 270 air miles, offering an unparalleled view of Yukon's vast landscapes. A charter flight here is both a scenic and convenient gateway to the heart of the Yukon.

Dawson City to Watson Lake

Covering around 445 air miles, this flight path takes you over some of the most untouched wilderness areas of Yukon, providing a unique perspective on the territory's natural beauty.

Dawson City to Yellowknife, NWT

With about 785 air miles to cover, flying from Dawson City to Yellowknife introduces passengers to the sprawling landscapes of the North, making every mile an adventure in itself.

Dawson City to Faro, Yukon

This 230 air mile journey offers an intimate glimpse into the rugged terrain and vast forests of Yukon, showcasing the raw beauty accessible via charter flights.

Dawson City to Anchorage, Alaska

Traversing approximately 390 air miles, this route reveals the majestic mountains and sprawling wilderness that lie between Dawson City and Anchorage, Alaska.

Dawson City Airport

Dawson City Airport serves as the gateway to this historic town, located just a short drive from the center. With a 5,000-foot runway, it accommodates a range of charter flights, offering services like fuel and parking, making it an essential hub for visitors and residents alike.

Other Nearby Airports
Erik Nielsen Whitehorse International Airport

Located in Whitehorse, this airport boasts a 9,500-foot runway. It's a key connector to other parts of Canada and offers comprehensive services including car rentals and dining.

Watson Lake Airport

With a 5,500-foot runway, Watson Lake Airport is a crucial access point for travelers heading to the southern parts of Yukon, providing basic amenities and fuel services.

Mayo Airport

This airport features a 4,000-foot runway, serving the Mayo region and nearby communities. Though small, it offers essential services for those exploring the central Yukon.

Old Crow Airport

As the only airport in the northern Yukon community of Old Crow, this facility has a 4,000-foot runway and is vital for residents and tourists seeking to explore the Arctic wilderness.

Dawson City Hotels / Accommodations
The Aurora Inn

Nestled in the heart of Dawson City, The Aurora Inn provides cozy accommodations and a warm, welcoming atmosphere, ideal for those looking to relax after a day of adventure.

Klondike Kate's Cabins

Offering charming, rustic cabins with modern amenities, Klondike Kate's is a perfect blend of comfort and Yukon history, situated conveniently in Dawson City.

Dawson City Outdoor Adventures
Klondike Gold Rush Tours

Embark on a journey back in time to the days of the Klondike Gold Rush with guided tours that offer a glimpse into the past, combined with the thrill of gold panning.

Yukon River Canoe Trips

Experience the serene beauty of the Yukon River with guided canoe trips that offer a unique perspective of Dawson City's stunning wilderness and wildlife.
