About Lutselk'e Northwest Territories and Location

Lutselk'e, situated on the eastern arm of the Great Slave Lake, is a gateway to the natural wonders of the Northwest Territories. This tranquil community is rich in cultural heritage and offers a plethora of outdoor activities, making it an ideal destination for those seeking an authentic Northern experience. Accessible by charter flights, Lutselk'e serves as a starting point for adventurers looking to explore the vast wilderness, pristine waters, and unique wildlife of the region.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Lutselk'e to Yellowknife

The distance from Lutselk'e to Yellowknife is approximately 120 miles (193 kilometers), highlighting the importance of charter flights for efficient travel and connectivity within the Northwest Territories.

Lutselk'e to Edmonton

Connecting Lutselk'e to Edmonton, the journey spans about 920 miles (1,480 kilometers), underscoring the vital role of charter flights in bridging the gap between remote communities and major urban centers.

Lutselk'e to Fort Resolution

Lutselk'e is roughly 180 miles (290 kilometers) from Fort Resolution, demonstrating how charter flights facilitate access to even the most secluded locations.

Lutselk'e to Fort Simpson

The distance from Lutselk'e to Fort Simpson is about 300 miles (483 kilometers), showcasing charter flights as a key mode of transportation for residents and visitors alike.

Lutselk'e Airport

The community has a public use airport just a mile norheat of town. The single gravel runway is 3,003 feet long and flight operations may be restricted to daylight hours during certain weather conditions.

Other Nearby Airports
Yellowknife Airport

As the main aviation gateway to the Northwest Territories, Yellowknife Airport provides comprehensive services, facilitating connections to remote destinations like Lutselk'e.

Fort Smith Airport

Fort Smith Airport, located near the Alberta-NWT border, offers another critical access point to the Territories, with services catering to both commercial and charter flights.

Hay River Airport

Hay River Airport plays a crucial role in connecting the southern regions of the Northwest Territories, providing vital air services to communities along the Great Slave Lake.

Fort Simpson Airport

This airport is an important hub for accessing the upper reaches of the Northwest Territories, offering flights to various remote locations, including Lutselk'e.

Lutselk'e Hotels / Accommodations
Lakeview Inn

Lakeview Inn in Lutselk'e offers cozy accommodations with stunning views of the Great Slave Lake, providing a peaceful retreat for visitors exploring the natural beauty of the Northwest Territories.

Lutselk'e Outdoor Adventures

The region around Lutselk'e is a paradise for outdoor enthusiasts, offering fishing, kayaking, and wildlife viewing. Winter months bring the spectacular northern lights, while summer offers endless daylight for hiking and canoe trips. Local companies like Great Slave Adventures provide guided tours, ensuring visitors have a safe and unforgettable experience in the wilds of the Northwest Territories.
