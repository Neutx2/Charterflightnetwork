Charter Flights To Trout Lake
About Trout Lake Northwest Territories and Location

Trout Lake, nestled within the pristine landscapes of the Northwest Territories, is a hidden gem waiting to be discovered. Known for its crystal-clear waters and abundant wildlife, Trout Lake offers an escape into nature unlike any other. The community is deeply rooted in tradition, providing visitors with an authentic experience of the Northern lifestyle. From fishing and kayaking to witnessing the mesmerizing northern lights, Trout Lake is the perfect destination for adventurers seeking solitude and beauty.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Trout Lake to Yellowknife

Trout Lake lies approximately 620 miles (998 km) from Yellowknife, offering a serene retreat far from the hustle and bustle of the capital city.

Trout Lake to Edmonton

The distance to Edmonton is around 840 miles (1,352 km), showcasing Trout Lake's remote accessibility and its connection to major urban centers via air.

Trout Lake to Fort Resolution

Spanning about 700 miles (1,127 km), the journey from Trout Lake to Fort Resolution crosses diverse landscapes, highlighting the region's natural beauty.

Trout Lake to Fort Simpson

At a distance of approximately 500 miles (805 km), the route from Trout Lake to Fort Simpson offers a gateway to the South Nahanni River and the spectacular Nahanni National Park Reserve.

Trout Lake Airport

Trout Lake Airport is a vital lifeline for the community, featuring a 1,500-foot runway that caters to small aircraft and essential services. Located just a few miles from the town, it ensures connectivity with the wider region, facilitating the delivery of goods, medical supplies, and enabling the flow of visitors eager to explore the untouched wilderness of Trout Lake.

Other Nearby Airports
Fort Simpson Airport

With a longer runway, Fort Simpson Airport serves as a regional hub, offering scheduled flights and services that connect the outer communities of the Northwest Territories.

Yellowknife Airport

As the main gateway to the North, Yellowknife Airport provides comprehensive services and frequent flights to various destinations, including direct access to Trout Lake.

Fort Smith Airport

Fort Smith Airport, serving the community of Fort Smith, offers another access point to the natural wonders of the Northwest Territories, including the nearby Wood Buffalo National Park.

Norman Wells Airport

Norman Wells Airport, located in the Sahtu Region, facilitates travel and cargo services to the central and northern parts of the Northwest Territories, connecting remote communities like Trout Lake to the rest of Canada.

Trout Lake Hotels / Accommodations
Lakeview Lodge

Lakeview Lodge in Trout Lake offers guests comfortable accommodations with stunning views of the lake and surrounding wilderness, providing a cozy base for all adventures.

Trout Lake Cabins

Trout Lake Cabins feature rustic yet comfortable lodging options, ideal for those looking to immerse themselves in the natural beauty of the Northwest Territories.

Trout Lake Outdoor Adventures

Trout Lake is an adventurer's paradise, offering year-round activities such as fishing, kayaking, hunting, and the chance to see the northern lights. Local outfitters like Wilderness Expeditions & Guides provide expertly led tours, ensuring visitors have safe and memorable experiences in the great outdoors.
