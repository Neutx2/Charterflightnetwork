About Flights To Killarney
Location

Killarney is in the heart of Killarney Provincial Park which is bordered by Hwy 69 and the French River area on the east and Lake Huron and Manitoulin Island to the south and west. Sudbury is about 1 hour drive north. You can arrange a private charter flight and fly key employees or clients direct to the Killarney airport for combination meetings / fishing, team building.

Toronto's Billy Bishop airport is the most popular departure point for charter flights to Killarney Mountain Lodge. The lodge is 200 air miles northwest of Toronto with an average flight time of just under an hour. Both wheel plane and float plane charters are available out of Billy Bishop Airport to Killarney.

Killarney Airport

The Killarney Mountain Lodge is just 5 minutes minutes from the Killarney Airport which features a 3,500 foot asphalt runway which will accommodate a wide variety of single and twin engine charter aircraft.

Cameron Air Services is based in Billy Bishop Airport Toronto and provides wheel plane and float plane charter flights to Killarney Mountain Lodge. If you prefer to fly to Killarney by float plane you can receive competitive float plane charter quotes from airlines based out of Toronto Billy Bishop, Muskoka, Parry Sound, Collingwood, Sudbury, Blind River and Elliot Lake.
