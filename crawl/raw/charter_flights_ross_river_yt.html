About Ross River Yukon and Location

Ross River is an enchanting community nestled in the heart of Yukon, offering an authentic wilderness experience. Known for its stunning landscapes, rich wildlife, and access to the Canol Heritage Trail, Ross River is a prime destination for adventurers. Charter flights to Ross River are the most convenient way to explore this remote haven, providing visitors with an unforgettable journey into Yukon's untouched beauty.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Ross River to Whitehorse

The air miles from Ross River to Whitehorse total approximately 125. The flight showcases the breathtaking vistas of Yukon's wilderness, making it an aerial adventure.

Ross River to Watson Lake

Spanning about 180 air miles, the journey from Ross River to Watson Lake offers passengers stunning views of Yukon's expansive landscapes.

Ross River to Yellowknife, NWT

Ross River to Faro, Yukon

Only 35 air miles away, the flight from Ross River to Faro is a quick trip over Yukon's picturesque settings.

Ross River to Anchorage, Alaska

The journey to Anchorage covers about 580 air miles, offering a scenic flight across the breathtaking landscapes that define Yukon and Alaska.

Ross River Airport

Ross River Airport, with its 5,000-foot runway, serves as a vital link for the community to Yukon and beyond. Located conveniently close to the town, it provides essential services for charter flights, making it an integral part of Ross River's connectivity and accessibility.

Other Nearby Airports
Whitehorse International Airport

Yukon's primary airport features a long runway capable of handling large aircraft and offers a wide range of services, acting as a major hub for flights throughout the territory.

Dawson City Airport

This facility supports the gold rush city of Dawson, providing access to one of Yukon's most historic locations with its well-equipped runway and services.

Watson Lake Airport

As a key point of entry to Yukon from the southeastern border, Watson Lake Airport facilitates regional connectivity with its comprehensive amenities.

Faro Airport

Located in the heart of the Yukon wilderness, Faro Airport is essential for accessing the central regions of the territory, offering direct links to remote communities like Ross River.
