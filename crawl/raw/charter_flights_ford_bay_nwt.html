About Ford Bay Northwest Territories and Location

Ford Bay is an enchanting destination located in the pristine wilderness of the Northwest Territories. Known for its rugged beauty, Ford Bay offers a sanctuary for those looking to escape the ordinary and dive into a world of adventure. With its serene landscapes, abundant wildlife, and rich cultural heritage, Ford Bay provides a unique blend of exploration and relaxation. The area's remote location makes it perfect for charter flights, offering unparalleled access to this untouched part of the world.

Distance From Major Cities
Ford Bay to Yellowknife

The journey from Ford Bay to Yellowknife covers approximately 650 miles (1046 kilometers), a testament to the remote and pristine nature of Ford Bay, accessible primarily by charter flights.

Ford Bay to Edmonton

Traveling from Ford Bay to Edmonton spans about 1200 miles (1931 kilometers), connecting adventurers to the northern wilderness and its unique ecosystems.

Ford Bay to Norman Wells

The distance from Ford Bay to Norman Wells is roughly 350 miles (563 kilometers), showcasing the diverse landscapes of the Northwest Territories.

Ford Bay to Tuktoyaktuk

Ford Bay to Tuktoyaktuk extends over 900 miles (1448 kilometers), providing an epic journey through the Canadian Arctic landscape.

Ford Bay Airport

Ford Bay Airport is a crucial lifeline for the area, with a runway of 4,000 feet (1,219 meters) that welcomes various types of aircraft. Located just a few miles from the heart of Ford Bay, the airport offers easy access to the surrounding wilderness. Services available at Ford Bay Airport include flight charters, making it a hub for visitors seeking to explore the remote beauty of the Northwest Territories.

Other Nearby Airports
Yellowknife Airport

As the main gateway to the Northwest Territories, Yellowknife Airport serves as a critical connection point for travelers and provides a full range of services.

Norman Wells Airport

Norman Wells Airport offers access to the central regions of the Northwest Territories, with facilities to support both commercial and charter flights.

Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport

Inuvik Airport is vital for reaching the northern communities and the Arctic Ocean, providing unique opportunities for adventure and exploration.

Hay River Merlyn Carter Airport

Serving the southern part of the Northwest Territories, Hay River Airport is an important hub for regional flights and cargo services.

Ford Bay Hotels / Accommodations
Arctic Haven Lodge

Arctic Haven Lodge in Ford Bay offers cozy, rustic accommodations amidst the stunning natural backdrop, perfect for those looking to immerse themselves in the wilderness.

Bayview Inn

Bayview Inn provides comfortable lodging with spectacular views of the bay, offering a serene retreat after a day of adventure.

Ford Bay Outdoor Adventures

Ford Bay is a paradise for outdoor enthusiasts, offering a wide array of activities such as fishing in crystal-clear lakes, hunting in the vast wilderness, kayaking through serene waters, and hiking amidst breathtaking landscapes. Winter brings the magic of the northern lights and opportunities for snowshoeing and ice fishing. Local companies like Arctic Outdoor Expeditions provide guided tours and equipment rentals, ensuring visitors have everything they need for an unforgettable experience in Ford Bay.
