About Burwash Landing Yukon and Location

Located in the scenic wilderness of Yukon, Burwash Landing is an ideal destination for adventurers and nature lovers. Known for its breathtaking landscapes, rich wildlife, and cultural heritage, it offers numerous attractions and activities. From fishing and hunting to canoe and kayak trips, the area is perfect for those seeking outdoor adventures. Charter flights to Burwash Landing provide easy access to this remote paradise, making it an unforgettable escape.

Burwash Landing Airport

The Burwash Landing Airport features a 4,000-foot runway, located conveniently close to town. This airport is essential for travelers and adventurers coming via charter flights, offering services such as fueling and maintenance, and acting as a gateway to the vast wilderness of Yukon.

Other Nearby Airports
Erik Nielsen Whitehorse International Airport

As Yukon's primary air hub located in Whitehorse, this airport has a 9,500-foot runway and offers comprehensive services, including international flights, making it a vital connection point for travelers.

Watson Lake Airport

Offering a unique entry point to the southeastern Yukon, Watson Lake Airport has a 5,500-foot runway, with essential services for visitors exploring Yukon's splendors.

Dawson City Airport

This airport serves as a gateway to the Klondike Gold Rush history, featuring a 4,000-foot runway and providing basic services to visitors and history enthusiasts alike.

Mayo Airport

Located in the heart of the Silver Trail, Mayo Airport has a 3,000-foot runway and caters to tourists and locals with services that facilitate exploration of the region's rich mining history and natural beauty.
