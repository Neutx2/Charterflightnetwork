Chicago Airports
Charter flights are available to the following Chicago area airports
O'Hare International Airport (ORD):
  • Location: Chicago, Illinois Description: O'Hare is one of the busiest airports in the world and a major hub for both domestic and international flights. It offers a wide range of services and amenities, and it's located about 17 miles northwest of downtown Chicago.
Midway International Airport (MDW):
  • Location: Chicago, Illinois Description: Midway is another significant airport in Chicago, serving both domestic and limited international flights. It's situated about 8 miles southwest of downtown Chicago and is known for its more convenient size and easy access.
Chicago Rockford International Airport (RFD):
  • Location: Rockford, Illinois (approximately 85 miles northwest of downtown Chicago) Description: While not as large as O'Hare or Midway, Rockford Airport serves as a reliever airport for the Chicago area. It handles cargo and limited passenger flights.
Gary/Chicago International Airport (GYY):
  • Location: Gary, Indiana (approximately 25 miles southeast of downtown Chicago) Description: Although not directly in Chicago, Gary/Chicago Airport is in close proximity and provides an alternative for travelers in the region.
Chicago Executive Airport (PWK):
  • Location: Wheeling, Illinois (approximately 18 miles northwest of downtown Chicago) Description: This general aviation airport serves private and corporate flights and is an important facility for executive travel.
DuPage Airport (DPA):
  • Location: West Chicago, Illinois (approximately 29 miles west of downtown Chicago) Description: DuPage Airport is a general aviation airport that caters to private and business aviation.
Hotels Near DuPage Airport
