About Bonnyville Alberta and Location

Bonnyville, nestled in the heart of Alberta's Lakeland district, offers a picturesque escape with its beautiful lakes, rich history, and vibrant community. This charming town is an ideal destination for those seeking adventure or relaxation in the stunning Canadian landscape. With its convenient access to air charter services, including private jet and helicopter charters, Bonnyville is a gateway to exploring the natural beauty and cultural heritage of Alberta.

Distance From Major Cities
Bonnyville to Calgary

The journey from Bonnyville to Calgary spans approximately 550 kilometers (342 miles), making air charter services a practical and time-saving travel option for visitors and business travelers alike.

Bonnyville to Edmonton

Located about 240 kilometers (149 miles) northeast of Edmonton, Bonnyville is easily accessible via private jet charter, offering a swift connection to Alberta's capital.

Bonnyville to Grande Prairie

Connecting Bonnyville with Grande Prairie involves a distance of roughly 750 kilometers (466 miles), showcasing the efficiency and convenience of helicopter charters for reaching Alberta's northern regions.

Bonnyville Airport

The Bonnyville Airport, with a runway length of 4,000 feet, serves the local community and visiting charter flights. Situated just a few kilometers from downtown, the airport provides accessible travel options for those looking to explore Bonnyville and its surroundings. While on-ground transportation options such as taxis and rental cars can be limited, arrangements can be made in advance to ensure seamless connectivity.

Other Nearby Airports
Cold Lake Regional Airport (YOD)

About 30 kilometers (19 miles) from Bonnyville, Cold Lake Regional Airport offers an alternative entry point to the region, with facilities accommodating a range of charter flights and air services.

St. Paul Municipal Airport (CEW3)

Located approximately 70 kilometers (43 miles) south of Bonnyville, St. Paul Municipal Airport provides another convenient option for air travelers, supporting general aviation and air charter operations.

Bonnyville Hotels
Best Western Bonnyville Inn & Suites

Offering a blend of comfort and convenience, the Best Western Bonnyville Inn & Suites is known for its exceptional service, making it a preferred choice for travelers seeking quality accommodations. With amenities tailored to both business and leisure guests, this hotel ensures a pleasant stay.

Centre Suite Inns

Located in the heart of Bonnyville, Centre Suite Inns provides cozy accommodations with easy access to local attractions and businesses. Its friendly atmosphere and thoughtful amenities cater to guests looking for a home away from home.
