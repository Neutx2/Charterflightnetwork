Charter Flights To Mary River, NU
Location

Mary River is located on Baffin Island about 585 mi northwest of Iqaluit, There are no scheduled flights to Mary River, however you can book direct charter flights to Mary River from departure points across Nunavut and major Canadian cities to the south. Nolinor Aviation operates charter flights to Mary River from Iqaluit, Kitchener/Waterloo and Yellowknife .

Mary River Airport

The area is rich in high-grade iron ore deposits. The ore extracted from the mine is transported to Milne Inlet by a dedicated railway, and from there, it is shipped to markets around the world. The mining activities have brought employment opportunities to the local community and contributed to the economy of Nunavut. Inuit Impact and Benefit Agreement (IIBA):

The mine operates under an Inuit Impact and Benefit Agreement (IIBA), which outlines the benefits and compensation that the local Inuit communities receive from the mining activities. This agreement is aimed at ensuring that the development of natural resources benefits the Inuit people and minimizes environmental impacts.

The Mary River region is known for its Arctic wildlife, including caribou, Arctic foxes, polar bears, and various bird species. The mining operations are conducted with environmental considerations, and measures are taken to minimize the impact on the local ecosystem.

About Mary River, Nunavut

Mary River is located in a remote and challenging Arctic environment. The area experiences harsh weather conditions, including extreme cold temperatures, which can impact transportation and logistics. The Mary River Mine is an open-pit iron ore mine operated by Baffinland Iron Mines Corporation. It is one of the largest iron ore deposits in the world. The mining project has been in operation since 2014 and has played a crucial role in the economic development of the region.
