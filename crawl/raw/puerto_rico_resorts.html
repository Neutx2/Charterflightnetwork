The St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort in Puerto Rico

The St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort in Puerto Rico

The St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort in Puerto Rico is a luxurious beachfront property nestled within a nature reserve on the northeastern coast of the island. Here's an overview of what you can expect:

Location:

The St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort is situated on a stunning stretch of coastline in Rio Grande, Puerto Rico, approximately 30 minutes away from the bustling city of San Juan. The resort is set within the lush surroundings of the Bahia Beach nature reserve, offering guests a serene and secluded retreat.

Accommodations:

The resort offers luxurious accommodations designed to provide guests with comfort and elegance. Rooms and suites feature elegant Caribbean-inspired décor, spacious layouts, and modern amenities. Many accommodations boast private terraces or balconies with views of the ocean, gardens, or golf course.

Golf Course:

The St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort boasts a spectacular 18-hole championship golf course designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr. Surrounded by lush tropical foliage and stunning views, the course offers a challenging yet rewarding experience for golfers of all skill levels.

Dining:

The St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort features several dining options to satisfy guests' culinary desires. This includes:

Fern: A signature restaurant offering contemporary cuisine inspired by the flavors of the Caribbean with a focus on locally sourced ingredients.
Molasses: A beachfront restaurant serving fresh seafood and grilled specialties in a casual, relaxed atmosphere.
Seagrapes: A poolside grill offering light bites, sandwiches, salads, and refreshing beverages.

Activities:

The resort offers a range of activities and amenities to keep guests entertained and relaxed throughout their stay. Some of the activities include:

Beach and Pool: Guests can enjoy lounging on the pristine beach or by the resort's sparkling swimming pool.
Water Sports: The resort offers a variety of water sports activities, including kayaking, paddleboarding, and snorkeling.
Tennis: Tennis enthusiasts can take advantage of the resort's tennis courts for a friendly match or private lesson.
Spa: The Remède Spa offers a range of indulgent treatments and therapies designed to promote relaxation and rejuvenation.

Other Puerto Rico Resorts & Hotels

Dorado Beach, a Ritz-Carlton Reserve:

This luxurious resort offers stunning beachfront accommodations, world-class dining, and access to exclusive amenities such as championship golf courses and a renowned spa

The St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort:

Located on a former coconut plantation, this upscale resort features elegant rooms and suites, a Robert Trent Jones Jr. golf course, a Remède Spa, and a variety of outdoor activities such as kayaking, paddleboarding, and nature trails.

Condado Vanderbilt Hotel:

Situated in the trendy Condado district of San Juan, this historic hotel offers opulent accommodations, five-star dining options, a rooftop infinity pool, and a luxurious spa.

El Conquistador Resort, A Waldorf Astoria Resort:

Nestled atop a cliff overlooking the Caribbean Sea, this sprawling resort in Fajardo offers panoramic ocean views, a water park, a private island, golf courses, and a wide range of dining and entertainment options.

La Concha Renaissance San Juan Resort:

Located in the vibrant Condado district, this stylish resort features modern accommodations, a lively beachfront atmosphere, multiple pools, a casino, and a variety of dining and nightlife options.

Wyndham Grand Rio Mar Puerto Rico Golf & Beach Resort:

Set on 500 acres of lush tropical landscape in Rio Grande, this resort offers spacious rooms and suites, two championship golf courses, a sprawling pool complex, tennis courts, and a variety of restaurants and bars.
