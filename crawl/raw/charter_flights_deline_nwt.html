About Deline Northwest Territories and Location

Deline, located on the shores of the Great Bear Lake in the Northwest Territories, is a community steeped in history and culture. Known as the "where the waters flow," Deline is not only a place of breathtaking natural beauty but also a hub for those seeking to immerse themselves in the traditions of the Sahtu Dene people. From fishing and hunting to exploring ancient trails, Deline offers visitors a unique opportunity to connect with the land and its people.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Deline to Yellowknife

The distance from Deline to Yellowknife is approximately 547 miles (880 kilometers), showcasing the remote yet accessible nature of this community for those exploring the Canadian North.

Deline to Edmonton

Connecting Deline with Edmonton, the journey spans about 1,150 miles (1,850 kilometers), illustrating the vast landscapes and scenic beauty en route to one of Canada's northernmost communities.

Deline to Fort Resolution

From Deline to Fort Resolution, the distance is around 800 miles (1,287 kilometers), offering travelers a glimpse into the diverse and expansive geography of the Northwest Territories.

Deline to Fort Simpson

The trek from Deline to Fort Simpson covers roughly 620 miles (998 kilometers), providing an opportunity to experience the region's rugged beauty.

Deline Airport

Deline Airport is a vital link for accessing the remote wonders of the Sahtu Region. With a runway length of 3,940 feet, it accommodates various sizes of aircraft, facilitating travel and logistics for both residents and visitors. Located just a stone's throw from the town, the airport offers scheduled and charter flights, making Deline an accessible destination for adventurers and those seeking to explore the rich cultural heritage of the Northwest Territories.

Other Nearby Airports
Norman Wells Airport

Located in the town of Norman Wells, this airport offers a longer runway and a range of services, acting as a gateway to the central Northwest Territories and facilitating travel within the region.

Tulita Airport

Serving the community of Tulita, this facility provides essential air service, connecting travelers to the heart of the Sahtu Region with its picturesque landscapes and outdoor adventures.

Fort Good Hope Airport

Fort Good Hope Airport, with facilities to accommodate regional flights, serves as a crucial hub for accessing the northern parts of the Sahtu Region, offering unique opportunities for cultural and wilderness exploration.

Canol Road Airport

Though not a major airport, Canol Road Airport symbolizes the historic and adventurous spirit of the region, providing access to some of the most remote areas along the historic Canol Trail.

Deline Hotels / Accommodations
Great Bear Lake Lodge

Offering comfortable lodgings and breathtaking views of Great Bear Lake, the Great Bear Lake Lodge is the perfect base for those wishing to explore the natural beauty and cultural heritage of Deline and its surroundings.

Deline Guesthouse

With cozy accommodations and warm hospitality, the Deline Guesthouse welcomes visitors to experience the tranquility and beauty of the Arctic, making it an ideal retreat after a day of adventure.

Deline Outdoor Adventures

Embark on unforgettable outdoor adventures in Deline, from fishing in the crystal-clear waters of Great Bear Lake to wildlife viewing and hiking through the untouched wilderness. Local companies, such as Deline Adventures & Outfitters, offer guided tours and excursions, ensuring visitors can safely and authentically experience the best of the Sahtu Region's breathtaking landscapes and rich cultural history.
