About Flights To Webequie

Location

Webequie is a remote and isolated Ojibway community located in Northwestern Ontario on Winisk Lake. There is no road access, other than a seasonal winter road. Webequie is the closest community and year round airport to the Ring Of Fire Mineral Discovery.  There are aproximately 850 residents living in Webequie which is surrounded by the Winisk River Provincial Park and offers sportsmen some of the best fishing and hunting in Northern Ontario against a picturesque and pristine landscape.

Split Charters & Backhauls / Empty Flight Legs

With our high volume of  flights throughout the region each day, we often save clients more money by identifying flights that have empty legs heading north or south that match your travel requirements, or flights with partial loads that are available for split charters. 

Webequie Airport

The Webequie Airport is located just over a mile south west of the community and features a 3,500 foot gravel runway

Popular Charter Flights To Webequie

Flights From Armstrong

There are no wheel plane charters based in Armstrong.  You can however request quotes for float plane charters direct to Webequie which is located 200 air miles south. 

Flights From Pickle Lake

Pickle Lake 160 air miles south west and is one of the closest communities to Webequie with highway access.  We can get you competitive quotes for wheel plane, float plane and helicopter charters out of Pickle.   

Flights From Sioux Lookout

Sioux Lookout is 280 air miles south west of Webequie and is one of the closest communities with a hospital. We can get you up to 3 competitive quotes for wheel plane, float plane and helicopter charter flights.

Flights From Sudbury

It is 530 air miles from Sudbury to Webequie.  If you are in the mining or exploration industry and have a project in the  Ring Of Fire Region, a direct charter flight  from Sudbury might be your best option.  You can arrange helicopter charter to your property.

Flights From Thunder Bay

Thunder Bay is 330 air miles south of Webequie.  From Thuder Bay you can make airline connections direct to Toronto or Winnipeg.  Ask us to source out competitive quotes direct to Webequie with Wheel Plane, Float Plane or Helicopter.

Flights From Toronto

Toronto is 740 air miles south of Webequie, so a direct charter flight would be very expensive.  Webequie has a 3500 foot gravel runway, so you will need a turbo prop aircraft.  You could also charter a jet direct to Pickle Lake and then take a helicopter, float plane or turbo prop from there.  Ask us to source out various options for you.
