Flights to St Theresa Point Airport (YST)

About St Theresa Point:

The St Theresa Point Airport is located just under 1 mile north of the community on St Marys Island. The runway is 3,393 feet in length with a surface of crushed rock. The airport is accessible by a wide variety of charter and commuter aircraft such as the twin engine 18 passenger Metroliner, the 9 passenger King Air and Pilatus PC-12, along with smaller aircraft such as the Piper Navajo, Cessna Caravan, and single-engine Cessna 206.

Flights to St Theresa Point, MB FAQ

What is the airport code for St Theresa Point, Manitoba?

St Theresa Point Airport uses IATA code YST and ICAO code CYST.

Can I arrange wheel plane charter flights to St Theresa Point?

Yes. Wheel plane charter flights can be arranged to St Theresa Point Airport from Winnipeg and other departure points across Manitoba, Ontario, and elsewhere in Canada depending on aircraft type, payload, and scheduling.

Are float plane and helicopter charters available to St Theresa Point?

Yes. Float plane and helicopter charter options may be available for travelers who need more direct access near the community or for routes where a runway arrival is not the best fit.
