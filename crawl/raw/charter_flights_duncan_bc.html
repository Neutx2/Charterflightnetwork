Charter Flights To Duncan

There is an airport located at Duncan British Columbia, however no scheduled flights serve this location. The runway at Duncan Airport is relatively short, less than 1500 feet, it has an asphalt surface, however the types of charter aircraft that can use this airport are very limited due to the runway length.

If you are looking to book a charter flight to the Duncan area and your charter service is unable to use the Duncan airport, the next closest airport is Nanaimo located under 40 km north, with an average drive time of 30 minutes.
