Charter Flights To 108 Mile Ranch
About 108 Mile BC and Location

108 Mile Ranch is a picturesque community located in the South Cariboo region of British Columbia. Known for its stunning landscapes, clear lakes, and outdoor activities, it's a prime destination for those seeking adventure and relaxation. Charter flights to 108 Mile offer an exclusive gateway to exploring this hidden gem, providing access to remote destinations throughout British Columbia. Whether you're into hiking, fishing, or simply enjoying the tranquility of nature, 108 Mile has something for everyone.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
108 Mile to Abbotsford

The air charter flight covers a distance of approximately 195 miles. This quick and scenic journey offers a breathtaking view of British Columbia's diverse landscape.

108 Mile to Calgary

Spanning about 320 miles, the flight from 108 Mile to Calgary is not just a journey; it's an aerial tour of the majestic Rocky Mountains.

108 Mile to Kelowna, BC

At around 150 miles, the charter flight to Kelowna is a gateway to the heart of BC's wine country, offering stunning lake views along the way.

108 Mile to Vancouver, BC

Traveling 200 miles, flights from 108 Mile to Vancouver showcase the lush landscapes of BC, making every trip an unforgettable experience.

108 Mile to Williams Lake, BC

A brief but beautiful 45-mile flight connects 108 Mile with Williams Lake, offering a quick and convenient way to explore more of BC's interior.

108 Mile Airport

108 Mile Airport is a key facilitator for air charter services in the region. With a runway length of 5,000 feet, it comfortably accommodates various sizes of charter aircraft. Located just a few miles from the town center, it provides easy access to 108 Mile and its surrounding attractions. Services available at the airport include fuel, parking, and aircraft maintenance, making it an ideal starting point for your adventure in British Columbia.

Other Nearby Airports
Williams Lake Regional Airport

Located 60 miles from 108 Mile, Williams Lake Airport has a 7,000-foot runway and offers services like car rentals and dining, making it a convenient gateway to the Cariboo region.

Kamloops Airport

About 100 miles away, Kamloops Airport features a 8,000-foot runway. It serves as a critical link for tourists exploring the Thompson region, providing essential services including car rentals and shuttles.

Quesnel Airport

Quesnel Airport, 70 miles from 108 Mile, with a 5,500-foot runway, offers access to the historic gold rush region of BC, along with basic amenities for travelers.

Prince George Airport

Located 160 miles north of 108 Mile, Prince George Airport boasts an 11,450-foot runway, capable of handling larger aircraft and offering a range of services including dining and shopping.

108 Mile Hotels / Accommodations

Spruce Hill Resort & Spa

Nestled in the heart of nature, Spruce Hill offers luxurious accommodations, a full-service spa, and outdoor activities, making it the perfect retreat after a day of adventure.

108 Resort Golf & Outdoor Adventures

Offering cozy rooms with stunning views, the 108 Resort is ideal for those looking to combine relaxation with outdoor activities like golfing and horseback riding.

108 Mile Outdoor Adventures
Fishing at Sepa Lake

Enjoy a serene fishing experience at Sepa Lake, known for its abundant stock of rainbow trout. Ideal for both beginners and seasoned anglers, the lake offers boat rentals and a peaceful environment for a day spent in nature.

Hunting Expeditions

108 Mile Ranch serves as a base for guided hunting expeditions into the wilds of British Columbia. Offering services for both novice and experienced hunters, these expeditions target a variety of game, ensuring a responsible and thrilling hunting experience.

Canoe and Kayak Adventures

Explore the tranquil waters of 108 Mile and its surrounding lakes with a canoe or kayak trip. Rentals and guided tours provide an intimate look at the area's natural beauty, perfect for paddlers of all skill levels.

Horseback Riding Trails

Discover the scenic trails of 108 Mile Ranch on horseback. Local stables offer guided rides, lessons, and horseback tours, allowing visitors to connect with nature from a unique perspective.

Camping at 108 Mile Lake

With several well-equipped campsites, 108 Mile Lake is a favorite among campers seeking the tranquility of the BC wilderness. Whether in a tent or an RV, visitors can enjoy starlit nights and the call of the wild.

Hiking in 108 Mile Heritage Site

For those who love to explore on foot, the 108 Mile Heritage Site offers numerous hiking trails that wind through historic ranch lands and natural forests. It's an excellent way to immerse yourself in the local history and breathtaking landscapes of the area.
