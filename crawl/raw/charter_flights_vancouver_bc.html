Vancouver Wheel Plane Charters Arrive and Depart From Vancouver Airport's South Terminal

When you plan to travel by private air charter out of the main Vancouver airport, you will have the advantage of avoiding the main public passenger terminal. If you are arriving in Vancouver by scheduled airline, you will arrive at the main passenger terminal. Group Charters / Crew Rotations - 3 Competitive Quotes

Group Travel

Contact us if you would like to receive competitive quotes directly from the airlines who are best positioned and equipped for group charter flights in or out of the Vancouver area.

Crew Rotation

Whether you have a 2 or 200 person crew to rotate on an ongoing basis, you can receive quotes and logistics directly from the charter companies best positioned and equipped to provide the service.

If you are considering the option of booking a group charter flight for your sports team, association, corporate group or golf group, you should be prepared with the following information when requesting a quote in order to ensure you have the most accurate pricing.

You will need to know the exact number of adults and children along with any special baggage requirements. If everyone is bringing a golf bag for instance, this makes a big difference in the type of aircraft required.

What is your departure and destination airport? Depending on the size of your group, the aircraft best suited for your charter may not be able to operate in and out of the airports you have chosen. Or, in the case of an international flight, there may be no customs service at the airport you choose, which will then require a landing at a designated port of entry airport for customs clearance – which will increase your costs. So have alternate airports in mind. Some airports may offer customs / immigration services with restrictions on the number of passengers they will process. Picking the right airport is important on such flights.

To keep it cost effective, it is important that your entire group departs from the same airport. If a stop is required en route to pick up additional passengers, this will add considerably to the costs.

If you can be prepared with the above information when requesting a quote for a group charter flight, it will ensure you receive accurate pricing quickly.
