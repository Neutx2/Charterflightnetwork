About Flights To Nakina
Location

Nakina is a small community located north of Geraldton at the end of highway 584 and is on the main line of the CNR Railroad. You can book wheel plane charter flights to or from the Nakina Airport, or you can arrange float plane charter flights to the dozens of remote outpost and wilderness fishing lodges north of Nakina. If you have bulk cargo to ship to any remote destination across Northern Ontario - Nakina may be your most cost efficient staging area for cargo charter flights. For shipping any cargo north request a no obligation using our Charter Quote Request Service.

Nakina Airport

The Nakina Airport has a 3,500 foot asphalt runway and is located adjacent to the community.  We can arrange direct charter flights to the Nakina Airport and upon arrival, you connect with a float plane charter for a direct flight to a remote fly-in fishing lodge or outpost camp.
