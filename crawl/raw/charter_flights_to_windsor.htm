Location

Windsor is a highly industrialized region located just across the river from Detroit Michigan. Much of the industry is closely tied to the auto industry which creates a considerable amount of business travel from major cities across southern Ontario, Quebec and USA. Business travelers whose time is limited may find that taking a private charter flight direct to the Windsor Airport will allow them to conduct their business and return home the same day.

Windsor is directly across the Detroit River from the city of Detroit, Michigan. The Ambassador Bridge and the Detroit-Windsor Tunnel are the two main crossings between the two cities. This proximity has led to strong economic and cultural ties between Windsor and Detroit, making them essentially sister cities with a history of shared industries and cross-border activities.

Windsor offers a variety of recreational facilities and golf courses for residents and visitors. The city has parks, sports complexes, community centers, and outdoor spaces for various recreational activities, including sports, picnicking, and leisurely walks. Some of the golf courses in and around Windsor include:

  • Ambassador Golf Club
  • Roseland Golf and Curling Club
  • Seven Lakes Golf Course
  • Pointe West Golf Club
  • Little River Golf Course
The Windsor Airport

Windsor Airport, officially known as Windsor International Airport (IATA: YQG, ICAO: CYQG), is a public airport located in Windsor, Ontario, Canada. Here are some key details about Windsor Airport:

The longest runway at Windsor Airport is approximately 7,500 feet (2,286 meters) in length. This runway is equipped to handle a variety of aircraft, including commercial airliners.Windsor Airport is situated approximately 8 to 10 kilometers (about 5 to 6 miles) southwest of downtown Windsor. Porter Airlines operate flights between Toronto's Billy Bishop Airport and Windsor Airport.
