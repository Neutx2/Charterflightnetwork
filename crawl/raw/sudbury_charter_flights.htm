Sudbury, Ontario, is a vibrant city located in the northeastern part of the province. Nestled among the stunning landscapes of the Canadian Shield, the city is known for its rocky terrain, pristine lakes, and abundant natural beauty. With a population of over 160,000, The city serves as a regional economic and cultural hub for Northern Ontario.

The Local Airport (YSB) is located about 13 miles northeast of downtown and is the primary air transportation hub for passengers, cargo and medevac flights throughout northern Ontario. The airport features two asphalt runways 6,600 ft and 5,000 ft in length making this airpor accessible to all charter aircraft operating in the region. The airport is classified as an airport of entry but limited to general aviation with flights with no more than 15 passenger.

About Flights To Sudbury
Bearskin Airlines Flights From Sudbury to Thunder Bay

Bearskin Airlines operates regular scheduled flights between Sudbury and Thunder Bay. The airline offers both non stop flights direct to Thunder Bay as well as a flight with a stop in Sault Ste Marie.

Flights From Sudbury to Red Lake Ontario

You can book a direct non stop charter flight from Sudbury to Red Lake with Air Bravo or you can fly Bearskin Airlines scheduled flight with stops in either Sault Ste Marie, Thunder Bay, Dryden or Sioux Lookout before arrival in Red Lake . Red Lake is located 670 air miles northwest of Sudbury. Depending on the type of aircraft, the average flight time between the two communities will average between 2:30 to 3:20. There is a wide choice of accommodations available in the Red Lake area, along with dozens of remote fishing lodges and outpost camps which are accessible by float plane charters from Red Lake.

Flights From Sudbury to USA

Hundreds of USA destinations are only a few hours away from Sudbury with a direct charter flight. If you have business in New York, you can fly direct to the Teterboro airport in 2.5 hours. Chicago's Executive Airport is just 2 hours south west and Cleveland can be reached in 1:30. Flights From Sudbury to Toronto Ontario

Business travellers prefer the speed and convenience of taking a charter flight between Toronto and Sudbury. A charter flight from Toronto's Billy Bishop Airport to Sudbury averages 1 hour. Clients can fly from Sudbury to Toronto in the morning, conduct a few hours of business and return home early that same afternoon.

Flights From Sudbury to Montreal Quebec

Montreal is 350 air miles south east of Sudbury. The average flying time is between 2 hours and 1:30 depending on the type of aircraft. The two main air charter airports in Montreal include the Trudeau Interantional Airport which can be used for clearing Canadian Customs for flights from the USA and the St Hubert Longueuil Airport located 10 miles east of downtown Montreal
