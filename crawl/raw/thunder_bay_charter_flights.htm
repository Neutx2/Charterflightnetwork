Thunder Bay Charter Flights: Wheel, Float & Helicopter

Thunder Bay, on the northwestern shore of Lake Superior, is a true northern gateway—mixing rugged boreal wilderness with a lively waterfront, Indigenous heritage, and year-round outdoor adventure. Thunder Bay International Airport (YQT) connects travelers to communities across Northwestern Ontario and beyond, with fast charter links to remote lodges, mining and industrial sites, corporate destinations, and major Canadian and U.S. cities.

  • Business: direct access to mines, exploration camps and regional hubs.
  • Adventure: floatplane links to fly-in fishing lodges, remote outpost camps and canoe routes.
  • Logistics: same-day wheel, float, and helicopter connections.

Wheel Plane Charters
Air Bravo - High Performance Turbo Prop Aircraft

Operating one of the largest fleets of the modern Pilatus PC12 turboprop aircraft in Canada, Air Bravo is a reliable choice for passenger, cargo and medevac flights across Northern Ontario and throughout Canada.  Charters into the USA as well.

North Star Air - Passenger and Cargo Charters

North Star Air provides scheduled and charter passenger flights as well as bulk cargo  services throughout Northern Ontario, Northern Manitoba and Nunavut.   They also are licenced to provide passenger charters into the USA 

Wasaya Airways

Owned by 12 First Nations, Wasaya Airways delivers safe and efficient transportation solutions, supporting remote communities without year-round road access.

Float Plane Charters
Lakehead Airways - Float Plane Charters Throughout Northwestern Ontario

Lakehead Airways is based in Thunder Bay and operates a fleet of float planes which include the legendary De Havilland Beaver, as well as the modern Cessna Caravan on amphibious floats.

Helicopter Charters
Zimmer Air

Thunder Bay is home to Zimmer Air helicopter charter services, making it easy to reach remote camps, survey sites, or wilderness lodges.

Wisk Air - Helicopter Charters

Headquartered in Thunder Bay, Wisk Air operates multiple bases across Canada, offering flexible helicopter services to industry, government, and First Nation communities.

Thunder Bay — Gateway To Remote Fly-In Fishing Adventures
Reduce Your Drive Time With A Charter Flight From Thunder Bay - Direct To Your Fishing Lodge or Outpost Camp

Thunder Bay is widely recognized as the starting point for Ontario’s legendary fly-in fishing experiences. Guests often fly into Thunder Bay by private jet or turboprop before boarding a float plane or helicopter that connects them to remote wilderness camps. Charter Flight Network can put you directly in touch with dozens of lodge operators and outfitters offering full-service fishing packages across Northwestern Ontario.
