#2 - Our Unique Island Location

Guests enjoy exceptional fishing for Walleyes and Northerns while staying at our fully modern outpost on this island location.  We have traditionally not booked more than 60 guests at our outpost each year, in order to sustain a high quality fishing experience for all our guests.
