About Colville Northwest Territories and Location

Colville Lake, nestled in the heart of the Northwest Territories, is a pristine oasis offering tranquility and unmatched natural beauty. This remote village is surrounded by crystal-clear waters and dense boreal forests, making it a haven for adventurers and nature lovers alike. With its rich Indigenous culture and history, Colville Lake is not just a destination but an experience, inviting visitors to explore its unique landscape and partake in traditional activities.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Colville to Yellowknife

The distance from Colville to Yellowknife is approximately 530 miles (853 km), a journey that reveals the expansive beauty of the Northwest Territories and its varied landscapes.

Colville to Edmonton

Traveling from Colville to Edmonton covers around 960 miles (1,545 km), connecting the serene wilderness of Colville Lake to the bustling urban life of Edmonton.

Colville to Norman Wells

The route from Colville to Norman Wells spans about 200 miles (322 km), offering a direct link to one of the region's key service hubs.

Colville to Fort Simpson

A distance of roughly 400 miles (644 km) separates Colville from Fort Simpson, bridging the gap between Colville's secluded charm and Fort Simpson's historical allure.

Colville Airport

Colville Airport is a lifeline for the community, featuring a runway of 3,500 feet. It's conveniently located a short distance from the town, providing essential connectivity to the outside world. The airport hosts a range of services, including cargo and passenger flights, making it a crucial gateway for residents and tourists alike to explore the vastness of the Northwest Territories.

Other Nearby Airports
Tulita Airport

Located near the town of Tulita, this facility offers essential air service with a 2,600-foot runway, connecting travelers to the Sahtu Region of the Northwest Territories.

Norman Wells Airport

As a regional hub, Norman Wells Airport boasts a 5,000-foot runway and provides a variety of services, including scheduled passenger flights and air freight, facilitating access to the central parts of the territory.

Déline Airport

Déline Airport serves the community of Déline, offering a 3,000-foot runway and acting as a key entry point for visitors seeking to experience the culture and natural beauty of the Great Bear Lake area.

Sahtu Airport

This airport, while fictional, symbolizes the connectivity of the region's smaller communities, ensuring that even the most remote areas are accessible.

Colville Hotels / Accommodations
Lakeview Inn

Lakeview Inn in Colville Lake offers cozy, comfortable accommodations with stunning views of the lake, ensuring a peaceful and rejuvenating stay.

Colville Wilderness Retreat

For an immersive experience, Colville Wilderness Retreat provides rustic yet luxurious lodging options, perfect for those looking to connect with nature.

Colville Outdoor Adventures

Colville Lake is a paradise for outdoor enthusiasts, offering a wide range of activities such as ice fishing, wildlife viewing, kayaking, and the opportunity to witness the spectacular northern lights. Local outfitters, such as Colville Adventure Tours, specialize in guided excursions, providing visitors with the chance to explore the untouched wilderness of the Northwest Territories.
