About Teslin Yukon and Location

Teslin, Yukon, is a picturesque locale known for its stunning landscapes, vibrant wildlife, and rich cultural heritage. Nestled in the heart of Yukon, it offers an array of attractions and activities such as fishing, kayaking, and northern lights viewing, making it a must-visit for those seeking adventure and relaxation. Charter flights to Teslin provide an exclusive gateway to exploring this remote paradise.

Distance From Regional Cities and Towns
Teslin to Whitehorse

It is 90 air miles from Teslin to Whitehorse and the flight provides  a window to breathtaking views of Yukon's wilderness, a testament to the allure of charter flights in accessing remote destinations.

Teslin to Watson Lake

Flying the 140 air miles from Teslin to Watson Lake offers not just convenience but an aerial perspective of Yukon's untouched landscapes, accessible uniquely through charter flights.

Teslin to Yellowknife, NWT

Charter flights from Teslin, 630 air miles west to Yellowknife will allow you to witness the beauty of the northern frontier, offering passengers a journey as memorable as the destination itself.

Teslin to Faro, Yukon

It is 145 air miles from Teslin to Faro.  Flight time will be 45 minutes to 1 hour depending on aircraft type, wind and weather.    

Teslin to Anchorage, Alaska

The distance in from Teslin to Anchorage is 590 air miles and is a unique and exceiting adventure when you travel by private charter flight allowing you to experience unparalleled access to the vast wilderness of Alaska and Yukon alike.

Teslin Airport

Teslin Airport, with its expansive runway, is the linchpin for air travel and charter flights in the region. Located conveniently close to the town, it provides essential services, making it the perfect starting point for your Yukon adventures.

Other Nearby Airports
Whitehorse International Airport

Located in the capital, this airport is a hub for travelers seeking to explore Yukon. Its long runway and comprehensive services cater to all needs.

Watson Lake Airport

This gateway to the south of Yukon is equipped with essential services and a runway that welcomes visitors to the region's splendors.

Dawson City Airport

Step back in time with a visit to Dawson City, facilitated by this airport's runway and services, connecting you to the Klondike's history.

Old Crow Airport

Access the remote beauty of the Arctic Circle through Old Crow Airport, where the runway and services ensure a seamless journey to the north.

Teslin Hotels / Accommodations
