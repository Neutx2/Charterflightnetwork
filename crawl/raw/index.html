#1. Wheel Plane / Float Plane / Helicopter

Use our Free, No Obligation Charter Quote Request Service for wheel plane, float plane or helicopter charter flights in any province or territory of Canada, into the USA or even the Bahamas and Caribbean.

#2. Your Request Is Matched To The Charter Airlines Best Equipped And Positioned To Provide The Service Requested

When we receive your charter quote request, our database searches through our network of over 400 charter airlines across Canada and sends your request to up to 3 charter airlines who are best positioned and equipped to provide the service requested.

#3. Receive Up To 3 Competitive Quotes Directly From The Air Charter Companies

Each charter company prepares a competitive quote and sends it directly to your email. You may receive as many as 3 competitive quotes. Review the quotes, check out the company websites and feel free to give any of the companies a call to review any comments or concerns.

#4. You Confirm Scheduling & Payment Directly With The Charter Company Of Your Choice

There is never an obligation to accept any quote. However, if you decide to confirm your flight with one of the submitted quotes, you deal directly with the charter company to confirm all details, including scheduling, passenger names and payment options. Payments are made directly to the charter company.

#5. Or Use The Canadian Air Charter Directory

If you prefer not to submit our Charter Quote Request Form, you can locate the charter company best positioned and equipped to provide the service you require by linking to our Canadian Air Charter Directory. Our directory, along with our 3 specialty directories, lists every wheel plane, float plane and helicopter charter service operating in every province and territory of Canada. You can contact any of the charter companies listed directly and request a quote.
