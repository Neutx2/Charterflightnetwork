Here Are Some Of The Features You Will Find

  • 1. Unlimited Use Of Our Network Search Tool

    Search our network data base for upcoming charter flights to any destination throughout Canada, USA, Bahamas, Caribbean. Access list of Empty Flight Legs to your destination, available at exceptional savings.


  • 2. Access Our Network Air Charter Directory
    Using our search tool you can locate Air Charter Services in every region of Canada, USA, Bahamas and Caribbean. View their profile of service offered and images of aircraft operated. Direct links to Air Charter websites

  • 3. Request Competitive Charter Quotes Direct From The Airlines
    Using only your screen name, to maintain your privacy, you may post your charter flight requirements online. The Air Charter Services that are best positioned and equipped to provide your service and who have availability for the travel dates you provided will prepare and submit competitive quotes - which will forward to your Network Screen Name.

  • 4. Save 50% On Flight Costs With Flight Share
    If you want to save 50% or more on a charter flight you have the option of selecting "Flight Share". There is no guarantee, but if one of our Associate Air Charter Members has an upcoming flight that matches your destination and travel dates and is available for Flight Share, you have the option of booking in on the same flight at an exceptional cost savings.

  • 5. Monitor And Submit Posts To Multiple Network Air Charter Forum Categories

    We have created multiple forum travel categories designed to feature charter flights that match specific travel interests. For Example we offer a forum that promotes Charter Flights To 1. Golf Destinations 2. Canadian Fly-In Fishing Destinations 3. Charter Flights To Polar Bear Adventures in Churchill 4. Helicopter Charter Flights 5. Float Plane Charter Flights

    If you have a special travel interest that matches one or more of these forums, you can monitor or submit posts to one or more forums. This is an excellent platform for locating other groups who share the same travel interests and presents Multiple opportunities for sharing the cost of a charter flight when travel dates and destinations coincide.
