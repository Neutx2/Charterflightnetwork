About Flights To Blind River
Location

Blind River is located east of Sault Ste Marie on Highway 17. There is no airport in Blind River, howevver float plane charter flights are available from this location. Blind River is a popular starting point for fly-in fishing and hunting adventures. If you would like to view all available outpost camps, lodges and outfitters along with posts from all area air charter services, feel free to join our members network.

Blind River Airport

The closest airport is located in Elliot Lake which is just over a 1/2 drive from Blind River.  The closest airport with scheduled airline service is Sault Ste Marie which is 1.5 drive west via hwy 17
