Charter Flights To Penticton
Penticton Wheel Plane Charters

Wheel plane charter flights will arrive and depart from the Penticton Airport located 2 miles southwest of town. The airport has one 6,000 ft asphalt runway making it acessible by jet, turbine and piston powered aircraft

Penticton Float Plane Tours and Charters

Visitors can also book float plane charter flights to Penticton from departure points across BC. You will arrive at Skaha Lake 3.5 miles south of town. Float plane charter flights are available out of Vancouver and Kelowna.

Penticton Helicopter Tours and Charters

There are a number of Okanagan Helicopter sightseeing and wine tours available out of Pentiction. Explore Ellis Creek Canyon, Enjoy a mountain top champagne picnic or take in the Skaha Winery Tour.

Location Of Penticton

You can book direct Charter Flights to Penticton which is located on the far southern shore of Lake Okanagan in south central region of British Columbia. The city is 30 air miles south of Kelowna, 160 air miles east of Vancouver and 270 air miles south west of Calgary

Charter Flights From Vancouver To Penticton

Penticton is located 160 air miles east of Vancouver. Visitors to the region can arrange wheel plane as well as float plane charters from Vancouver direct to Penticton. If you are wheel plane charters you will have your choice of jet aircraft, turbine powered aircraft or piston powered aircraft. If you are flying float plane, you have a choice of Turbine Powered or Piston Powered aircraft.

Charter Flights To Penticton From Calgary

Penticton is located 270 air miles south west of Calgary. You can receive competitive quotes for both jet aircraft as well as turbine powered aircraft. The 6000 ft asphalt runway at the Penticton Airport can easily accommodate jets in the light to medium size category.

Penticton Charter Flights To USA

If you want to book a charter flight from Penticton to the USA, we will need to bring an aircraft in from Cranbrook or Vancouver, which will add to the cost of the charter flight. If you require a return trip from the US back to Penticton, your flight will first need to land in Kelowna to clear Canadian Customs, before landing in Penticton.
