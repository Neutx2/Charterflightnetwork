About Flights To Red Lake

Location

Red Lake is one of the best known departure points for float plane charter flights to remote Ontario fishing lodges and Outpost Camps in North Western Ontario. Red Lake Is Famous As The Norseman Capital Of The World. The Norseman Is A 1930's Vintage Float and Ski Plane And Several Still Operate Today Out Of Red Lake.

You can arrange a private charter flight to Red Lake Airport from departure points across North America, or you can drive to Red Lake and book charter flights to remote northern communities, lakes or rivers.

You can book a scheduled flight to Red Lake with one of the regional airlines such as Wasaya Airways or Northstar Airlines. If however, you are looking for an alternative to commercial airline travel you may find that a private charter flight gives you the safety and peace of mind you are seeking. You can book a private charter flight direct to Red Lake from virtually any city across Canada. The more popular departure cities for direct charter flights include, Thunder Bay, Winnipeg, Sudbury, Timmins and even Toronto

Red Lake Airport

The Red Lake Airport is located just under 4 miles north of the town and features a 5,000 foot asphalt runway making it accessible to a wide range of aircraft operating in the region.
