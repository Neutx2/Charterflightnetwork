About Flights To Bearskin Lake

Location

Bearskin Lake is a remote First Nations community located about 425 km north of Sioux Lookout. There are no charter aircraft based here; however, you can charter flights to Bearskin Lake from almost any airport in North America. Most charters originate from Pickle Lake, Sioux Lookout, and Red Lake. Since essentials such as groceries and fuel are flown in, local prices are higher than down south.

Pickle Lake to Bearskin Lake

Wheel Plane / Float Plane / Helicopter Charters

Bearskin Lake is a remote fly-in community accessible primarily by air for much of the year and is 180 air miles north of Pickle Lake. Winter roads provide seasonal ground links for transporting fuel, building supplies and other bulky goods.
