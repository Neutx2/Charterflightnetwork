Location

Chatham - Kent is a ideally located about half way between London and Windsor along Hwy 401 with Lake St Clair to the west and Lake Erie to the south with Lake St Clair to the west and Lake Erie to the south. . It is a popular jumping off point for charter flights to world famous Pelee Island. The region is a combination of agriculture, industrial and high tech which means there is a lot of business travel in and out of the area.

The Chatham-Kent Airport

The Chatham-Kent Airport, located about 7 miles south east of Chatham, Ontario and features one runway which is approximately 5,500 feet long making it accessible to a wide range of charter aircraft operating in the region. Facilities at the Chatham-Kent Airport include a terminal building, fuel services, aircraft parking, and maintenance services.
