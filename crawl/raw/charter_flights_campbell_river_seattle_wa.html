Seattle to Campbell River — Frequently Asked Questions

Scheduled airline flights arrive at Seattle–Tacoma International Airport (SEA / SeaTac). From SEA, a pre-arranged car or van service takes you to the correct charter departure terminal.
Wheel-plane charters typically depart from Boeing Field / King County International Airport (BFI). The SEA to BFI ground transfer is commonly around 15 to 30 minutes depending on traffic and pickup point.
Floatplane charters may depart from Renton or the Lake Union seaplane terminal. Your quote will confirm the operator, departure point, baggage limits, and check-in timing.
Most turboprop and light twin options average around 1 hour, depending on winds and routing. Floatplane routings can take longer depending on aircraft type and departure point.
Customs handling for cross-border charter flights depends on the operator and the planned port of entry. We confirm the exact process during quoting so your group knows where and when formalities are completed.
