About Berens River:

Berens River is a northern Manitoba community on the east side of Lake Winnipeg at the mouth of the Berens River. It serves as an important regional access point and offers strong ties to the area’s waterways, Indigenous culture, and outdoor setting.

Berens River Services / Accommodations / Contacts:
Airport and Flight Access
  • Charter flights to Berens River use Berens River Airport, YBV / CYBV, which has a certified crushed-rock runway measuring about 2,888 feet. The airport is an important regional access point for community travel, essential services, and northern Manitoba charter operations.
Accommodations and Local Services
  • Berens River offers practical visitor services, including local lodging options, groceries, freight support, and community facilities. Travelers planning short business, service, or family visits can generally find the basic services needed for a practical stay in a remote northern community.
Things to Do and Local Attractions
  • Berens River’s appeal is closely tied to its lakeside setting, river access, and surrounding wilderness. The area is relevant for boating, fishing, seasonal outdoor travel, and visits connected to the region’s cultural and community life along the east side of Lake Winnipeg.
