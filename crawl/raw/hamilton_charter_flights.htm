Location

Hamilton, Ontario is located in the southern part of the province of Ontario, Canada. It is situated on the western tip of Lake Ontario, approximately 68 kilometers (42 miles) southwest of downtown Toronto.

If you are traveling to any region of Ontario, Canada or North America from the Hamilton area for business or vacation and scheduled airlines do not meet your travel requirements, you may find that booking private charter flights out of Hamilton is your best travel option. The Hamilton area offers a wide selection of charter flight options that are sure to meet your demands. If your flight originates in the USA you will be pleased to learn that the Hamilton Airport is designated as an airport of entry, which means you can clear Canadian Customs and Immiggration upon landing in Hamilton.

The main airport serving Hamilton, Ontario is called John C. Munro Hamilton International Airport (IATA: YHM, ICAO: CYHM). It is located in Mount Hope, about 11 kilometers (7 miles) southwest of downtown Hamilton.

  • Runway 06/24: This runway is 10,006 feet (3,050 meters) in length and is primarily used for larger aircraft. It has precision instrument approach capabilities.
  • Runway 12/30: This runway is 6,010 feet (1,832 meters) in length and is suitable for smaller aircraft. It also has precision instrument approach capabilities.
  • Terminal Building: The airport has a modern terminal building with various amenities, including check-in counters, baggage claim, security screening, and a variety of dining and shopping options. However, when you fly private charter flights, you will arrive and depart from a private passenger charter terminal.
  • Parking: The airport offers both short-term and long-term parking facilities for travelers. There are multiple parking lots available, including covered and uncovered options.
  • Rental Car Services: Several car rental companies have counters located in the terminal building, providing convenient options for travelers to rent a vehicle upon arrival.
  • Ground Transportation: Various ground transportation options are available, including taxis, limousines, shuttles, and public transportation, allowing passengers to travel to and from the airport easily.
