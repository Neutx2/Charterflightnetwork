Corporate Charter Flights Kitchener to Raleigh–Durham, North Carolina

When you need to be in the right place at the right time, a direct charter flight keeps you on schedule

With a dedicated flight from Kitchener–Waterloo to Raleigh–Durham (RDU), you set the schedule. Same-day out-and-back is easy—and if meetings run long, hold the aircraft and return later.

  • RDU is ~530 air miles south of Kitchener–Waterloo.
  • Typical flight time: ~1h30–1h50 in a light/mid jet; just over 2h in a high-performance turboprop.
  • U.S. Customs clearance available; the charter airline coordinates all arrangements in advance.
  • Major car rentals: Enterprise, Avis, Alamo, Hertz, and more.
  • Close to the Research Triangle; ~10 minutes to downtown Raleigh and downtown Durham.
  • Corporate FBOs include Signature Flight Support and Atlantic Aviation.

Private Jet Charters to Raleigh–Durham

When time matters most, a jet is often the best fit. Options range from 4-seat light jets (e.g., Phenom 100) to 8-seat mids (Citation Excel) and 10-seat large-cabin (Challenger 604). Charter costs can range from $3,000 per hour to over $5,000 per flight hour. On shorter flights, the charter company may charge a minimum of 2 hours or more.

Turboprop Aircraft Charters

Popular turboprops include King Air and Pilatus PC-12. Configurations vary from commuter 9 seat layouts to 5 seat executive cabins with work tables and lavatory, msking them ideal for business productivity while enroute.Airlines may charge by the hour or by the mile. The charter rate for these types of turboprops may average in the $12 - $14 per mile range.

Twin-Engine Piston Aircraft

Piper Navajo and similar twins suit 4–6 passengers for budget-sensitive missions. The average flight time for a flight from YKF to RDU will be around 2.5 + hours and may require a fuel stop depending on weather and number of passengers.These aircraft may charter out in the $10 - $12 per mile range.
