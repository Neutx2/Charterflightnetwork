The Kitchener Waterloo  Airport

Kitchener is conveniently located just 45 minutes west of Pearson International Airport and is the heart of what is referred to as the "Golden Triangle". The Kitchener Waterloo Guelf and Cambridge area is home to an extensive manufacturing and high tech sector. The Kitchener Waterloo airport is an extremely busy airport for corporate and charter flights. It is staffed by CBSA officers making it convenient to fly private charter flights from any point in the USA.

In addition to several air charter airlines based at the Kitchener / Waterloo Airport, the airport is also served by Flair Airlines, Sunwing Airlines and WestJet.

Kitchener Waterloo Golf Courses

If you are flying to Kitchener / Waterloo region to meet with clients or to make sales calls, you may want to consider entertaining priority clients or key employees with a few rounds of golf. There are numerous courses in the region, some of which include

  • Grey Silo Golf Course: Located in Waterloo, this golf course offers a challenging layout with beautiful scenery and is often considered one of the top courses in the region.
  • Deer Ridge Golf Club: Situated in Kitchener, this course features a mix of open and wooded holes, providing an enjoyable golfing experience.
  • Westmount Golf and Country Club: Found in Kitchener, Westmount offers a well-maintained course with mature trees and water features.
  • Whistle Bear Golf Club: Situated in nearby Cambridge, this championship course is known for its stunning landscape and challenging design.
  • Guelph Lakes Golf and Country Club: As the name suggests, this course is located in Guelph and offers a scenic and relaxing golfing experience.
  • Ariss Valley Golf and Country Club: Located near Guelph, this course features rolling terrain and picturesque views.
  • Victoria Park East Golf Club: Situated in Guelph, this course offers a mix of open and tree-lined fairways.

These are just a few examples of the golf courses in the Kitchener-Waterloo, Guelph, and surrounding areas. Each course has its own unique features and challenges, catering to golfers of various skill levels. It's recommended to contact the individual golf courses for more information on their amenities, tee times, and course conditions.
