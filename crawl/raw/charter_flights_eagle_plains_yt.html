About Eagle Plains, Yukon and Location

Eagle Plains, Yukon, offers a secluded retreat for those looking to explore the vast wilderness of the Yukon Territory. With its stunning landscapes, abundant wildlife, and access to the historic Dempster Highway, it's a prime destination for adventurers and nature enthusiasts. Charter flights to Eagle Plains provide an essential link to this remote area, making it accessible for tourists seeking the ultimate Yukon experience.

Eagle Plains Airport

Eagle Plains Airport features a 2,500-foot runway, which is actually part of the Dempster Highway and provides vital access to this remote community. Situated just steps away from the town center, it provides essential services for residents and visitors alike, including charter flights that connect Eagle Plains with the rest of Yukon and beyond.

Other Nearby Airports
Dawson City Airport

Located in the heart of the Klondike Gold Rush country, Dawson City Airport provides access to one of Yukon's most iconic historical sites, with a runway accommodating a wide range of aircraft for both commercial and charter flights.

Old Crow Airport

As the only connection to the outside world for the village of Old Crow, this airport is crucial for supplies and visitors. It supports small to medium-sized aircraft, including charter flights that cater to tourists looking to explore the remote wilderness.

Mayo Airport

Serving the beautiful Silver Trail region, Mayo Airport facilitates access to stunning natural landscapes and historic sites, with charter flights enhancing the travel experience for adventure seekers.

Teslin Airport

Providing critical access to the scenic area surrounding Teslin Lake, Teslin Airport supports regional travel and tourism through both scheduled and charter flights, with a focus on outdoor adventure and exploration.

Eagle Plains Hotels / Accommodations
Eagle Plains Hotel

The Eagle Plains Hotel offers comfortable lodging, dining, and support services for travelers embarking on the Dempster Highway adventure, making it an essential stop in the journey through Yukon's wilderness.
