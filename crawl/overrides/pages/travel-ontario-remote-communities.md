## Communities without year-round road access

Across Northern Ontario, dozens of communities are reachable only by air for
most of the year. Dozens of remote First Nations communities rely on Charter
Flight Network to ensure they find the most competitive air charter options —
for passengers, for freight, and for the medical, band and program travel that
keeps a community running.

## The operators who fly these routes

Network members serving remote Northern Ontario include carriers owned by the
communities themselves: Wasaya Airways, owned by 12 First Nations, delivers
transportation solutions supporting remote communities without year-round road
access. Others provide scheduled and charter passenger flights as well as bulk
cargo services throughout Northern Ontario, Northern Manitoba and Nunavut.
Float plane and helicopter operators cover the lakes and sites that no strip
serves at all.
