## Your schedule, not the airline's

Airlines that offer charter flights have no set departure or arrival times and
no specific destinations: you tell the airline when and where you want to fly,
and if the destination suits their aircraft, they schedule around you. For
business travellers that is the whole point — reaching a regional plant, a
project site or a client meeting the same day, and getting home the same
night.

## Where business charters fit

- **Moving a team or delegates** — Charter Flight Network is a resource when
  companies need to determine how to move delegates in from various regions.
- **Regional and remote sites** — turboprop and piston charters cover regional
  and remote destinations; [helicopters](/aircraft/helicopters) reach
  locations with no airstrip at all.
- **Cross-border trips** — Canadian operators in the network are licensed to
  provide passenger charters into the USA. See
  [USA charter flights](/usa).
- **Longer legs and international travel** — [jet charters](/aircraft/jets)
  serve corporate, private and international travel, including the
  [Bahamas](/bahamas) and [Caribbean](/caribbean).
- **An alternative to scheduled seating** — any business, professional or
  individual traveller searching for an alternative to sitting shoulder to
  shoulder on scheduled airlines.
