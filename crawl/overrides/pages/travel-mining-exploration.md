## Moving crews and cargo to sites the road doesn't reach

When construction and engineering companies bid on a remote project, they turn
to Charter Flight Network to request competitive charter quotes. With new
mining operations opening up throughout Canada, determining logistics for
staff and crew rotations is a major consideration — and the aircraft that can
do it are spread across a network of operators in every province and
territory.

## What the network flies for resource projects

- **Crew rotations** — turboprop operators in the network fly passenger,
  cargo and medevac work across Northern Ontario and throughout Canada,
  including one of the largest fleets of the modern Pilatus PC-12 in the
  country.
- **Bulk and priority cargo** — network members provide bulk cargo services
  throughout Northern Ontario, Northern Manitoba and Nunavut. Clients
  consistently turn to Charter Flight Network when they need to ship priority
  cargo or hot shot services to remote regions of Canada.
- **Helicopter access** — for survey sites, drill camps and locations with no
  airstrip at all, helicopter operators in the network fly from multiple bases
  across Canada, serving industry, government and First Nation communities.
- **Environmental and consulting teams** — consultants frequently need to send
  people to regions across Canada accessible only by fixed wing or helicopter
  charter.

## Common departure points for project work

Northern gateways used as staging points for resource projects include
[Thunder Bay (YQT)](/canada/northern-ontario/thunder-bay),
[Sioux Lookout](/canada/northern-ontario/sioux-lookout),
[Pickle Lake](/canada/northern-ontario/pickle-lake) — the closest all-weather
strip to much of the [Ring of Fire](/canada/northern-ontario/ring-of-fire)
region — plus hubs across
[the Northwest Territories](/canada/northwest-territories),
[Nunavut](/canada/nunavut) and [Saskatchewan](/canada/saskatchewan).
