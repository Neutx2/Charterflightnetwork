## The trip of a lifetime starts with the flight in

Canada's best fishing is on water no road reaches. Guests typically fly into a
northern gateway like Thunder Bay by private jet or turboprop, then board a
float plane or helicopter that connects them to remote wilderness camps.
Charter Flight Network can put you directly in touch with dozens of lodge
operators and outfitters offering full-service fishing packages across
Northwestern Ontario — and with the charter airlines that get you there.

## How the connection works

- **Get to the gateway** — scheduled or charter flights bring your group to a
  northern hub such as [Thunder Bay (YQT)](/canada/northern-ontario/thunder-bay),
  [Sioux Lookout](/canada/northern-ontario/sioux-lookout) or
  [Red Lake](/canada/northern-ontario/red-lake).
- **Board the float plane** — operators in our network fly legendary
  De Havilland Beavers and modern Cessna Caravans on amphibious floats,
  direct to your lodge dock or outpost camp.
- **Cross-border pickups** — network members based in the region are licensed
  for USA charters; one Red Lake operator's pressurized twin-engine King Air
  can pick you up in the US and provide a seamless float plane connection
  direct to your fishing lodge or outpost camp.
- **Flexible dates save money** — flights with empty legs heading north or
  south, or partial loads available for split charters, often cut the cost.
  See [empty leg flights](/empty-legs).

## Book early for open-water season

Lodges, outfitters and the charter airlines that serve them book up ahead of
open-water season. Submitting your quote request early gives the operators
best positioned for your route time to hold space for your group — and there
is never a cost or obligation to request quotes.
