### Charter Flight Network — 100% Canadian owned & operated. Finding clients the most cost efficient air charter options since 2008.

## Our Mission

At Charter Flight Network, our mission is simple yet powerful: to connect clients with the most cost-efficient and reliable air charter solutions across Canada and beyond. We achieve this by streamlining the process of obtaining quotes from multiple professional charter airlines, allowing clients to quickly compare options and book with confidence. Our focus is on saving time, reducing costs, and ensuring every traveler finds the charter service that perfectly matches their needs.

## What We Offer

Our service extends to all forms of charter aircraft, including:

* Jet charters for corporate, private, and international travel
* Turboprop and piston aircraft charters for regional and remote destinations
* Float plane charters for access to wilderness lodges, northern communities, and fishing or hunting camps
* Helicopter charters for business, sightseeing, and specialized missions

Our system allows clients to book charter flights not only anywhere in Canada, but also into the United States, the Bahamas, and the Caribbean.

## Why Choose Us?

Unlike traditional charter brokers, we do not simply present a single option. Instead, we source each quote request to multiple charter companies that are best positioned and equipped to provide the requested service. Each operator submits a competitive price quote, ensuring clients have the freedom to choose from the best available offers. This process puts control back in the hands of the customer, with the assurance that all quotes come from trusted, professional operators.

## Our History

Charter Flight Network has been serving Canadian travelers since 2008, growing a network of professional charter airlines with partners operating in every province and territory.

The company was founded by Phil Robinson, who has been directly involved in the Canadian Air Charter Industry for more than 50 years. Phil's experience and lifelong dedication to aviation continue to shape the way Charter Flight Network operates today — professionalism, transparency, and customer service in the air charter market.

## Who Uses Our Charter Quote Request Service

Since 2008 we have built a network of wheel plane, float plane and helicopter charter services in every province and territory of Canada who participate in our charter quote request service. The clients who rely on it include:

* **Remote and First Nations communities** — dozens of remote communities use Charter Flight Network to find the most competitive air charter options for passenger and priority-cargo service.
* **Fly-in fishing and hunting travelers** — sportsmen across Canada and the USA use our network to reach remote lodges and outpost camps.
* **Mining, construction and engineering companies** — when firms bid on remote projects or plan staff and crew rotations, they request competitive charter quotes through our network.
* **Corporate and group travel planners** — companies use Charter Flight Network to work out how to move delegates and teams in from different regions.
* **Environmental consultants and researchers** — teams that need to reach regions accessible only by fixed wing or helicopter charter.
* **Priority cargo shippers** — clients turn to the network for hot shot services to remote regions of Canada.
* **Anyone avoiding the scheduled-airline squeeze** — business or personal travelers looking for an alternative to shoulder-to-shoulder seating on scheduled airlines.
