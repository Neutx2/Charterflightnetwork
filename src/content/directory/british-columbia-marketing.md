(1b) Your Listings Will Be Featured On All Pages Within Your Target Markets

You will see in (2a) below how each charter company who subscribes to our program, defines their geographic charter market by completing our Charter Market Profile. ## #2. Determine The Charter Markets You Want To Target

### Charter Flight Network Promotes Charter Flights and Generates Charter Quote Requests From Every Province and Territory of Canada

Charter Flight Network website is made up over 800 pages. Each page promotes wheel plane / float plane and helicopter charter flights to a specific destination within a Canadian province or territory.

(2a) Charter Market Profile

In order to ensure we place your listings on the pages that best represent the markets you want to target, we ask all new subscribers to complete and submit the Charter Market Profile form. Here are some of the required categories on our form.

* **List Aircraft In Fleet** (Provide a list of all aircraft in your fleet)
* **List Geographic Markets** (list current markets served as well as any new markets you want to target with this program)
* **Licences & Services** (Are you licenced under CARS 703 as well as others / Is your operation IFR or only VFR )
* **Helicopter Charters** (List all helicopters in your fleet)
* **Operations Base** (List main base of operations, plus any satellite bases)
* **USA** (Are you licenced to provide charters into the USA )
* **Miscellaneous** (Minimum runway length / Do you operate into Gravel Runways/ Are you A Seaonal or year round service )

(2b) We Place Your Listings On Multiple Pages Throughout Our Website, Based On Your Charter Market Profile

Once we receive your completed Charter Market Profile, we start placing your listings on muiltiple pages, based on the information you provide in your Charter Market Profile.

We will place your listings on as many as ten pages for every province or territory you serve. If for example you provide service to destinations in Quebec, Ontario and Manitoba, you listings will be featured on 30 pages at any one time. **All at no charge.**

(2c) Every Charter Destination Page Features Available Aircraft Categories

To allow clients to easily request a quote for the exact type of aircraft required, each page features the following Charter Aircraft Categories:

* **Jet Charter Aircraft** (listed only when the local airport supports Jet operations)
* **Turbo Prop and Piston Aircraft** (listed only when the destination has an airport)
* **Float Plane Charters** (listed only when the destination is accessible by float plane)
* **Helicopter Charters** (listed pretty much for all destinations)

Based on your Charter Market Profile, your listing will appear in the category that matches your profile. If for example a charter airline operates both jet and turbo prop aircraft, their listings will appear in both categories, but on different pages .

## #3. Generating Charter Quote Requests

### Your Listings Are Designed To Generate Quality Charter Quote Requests

(3a ) Every Page Provides Clients With Two Options For Requesting A Charter Quote

Every page is designed to give clients two charter quote request options. Clients can scroll down the page and choose from the Jet / Turbo Prop / Float Plane / Helicopter category and then request a charter quote directly from any of the charter companies listed in each category. Or, if clients would like to receive up to 3 competitive quotes, they can complete and submit our generic charter quote request form.

Here is how each charter quote request option works:

(3b) Requesting A Quote Through Your Company Listing

When a client clicks the "Quote Request" Button on any of your listings, he does not link to your website. He links to a charter quote request form that has been set up and designed exclusively for your company.

When the client submits the completed form, it is routed through the Charter Flight Network email server. We confirm that is not spam and that it is complete with the client's contact information and it matches your Charter Market Profile. We assign it a quality score and within 60 - 90 seconds it forwards to your email.

Your team prepares a quote and sends it directly to the client. If the client accepts your quote, he or she deals directly with your charter service to confirm all details, scheduling and payment.

(3c) Generic Charter Quote Requests

When clients would like to receive up to 3 competitive quotes, they can complete and submit our generic charter quote request form, located on all 800 pages of our website.

After reviewing the quote for completeness, we assign it a quality score and forward the request to the charter companies whose Charter Market Profile best matches the request.

This means that when you subscribe to our Pay For Response Air Charter Marketing Program, **you can receive charter quote requests that match your Charter Market Profile, from any page,** even if you do not have a featured listing on that page.

(3d) Quality Score

In order to ensure that we are sending you high quality and genuine charter quote requests that have an above average chance of converting to a revenue charter, we have developed a method of assigning a quality score to every charter quote request you receive. Every Charter Quote Request generated through your featured listing or through our generic charter quote request form, is assigned a quality score that can rank from #0 to #10
