## Thunder Bay Charter Flights: Wheel, Float & Helicopter

Thunder Bay, on the northwestern shore of Lake Superior, is a true northern gateway—mixing rugged boreal wilderness with a lively waterfront, Indigenous heritage, and year-round outdoor adventure. **Thunder Bay International Airport (YQT)** connects travelers to communities across Northwestern Ontario and beyond, with fast charter links to remote lodges, mining and industrial sites, corporate destinations, and major Canadian and U.S. cities.

* Business: direct access to mines, exploration camps and regional hubs.
* Adventure: floatplane links to fly-in fishing lodges, remote outpost camps and canoe routes.
* Logistics: same-day wheel, float, and helicopter connections.

## Thunder Bay — Frequently Asked Questions

### How far is Thunder Bay Airport (YQT) from downtown?

About **15 minutes** by car in normal traffic.

### Can I clear Canadian customs at YQT on a private charter?

Yes—private arrivals from the U.S. can arrange CBSA clearance at YQT with advance notice.

### Can I connect to a float plane the same day?

Often, yes. Many guests land at YQT, then take a pre-booked shuttle (~20 min) to the Harbour float base for same-day departures.

### What aircraft are common for Thunder Bay charters?

Wheel planes (jets and turboprops) for regional and long-range trips; **De Havilland Beaver** and **Cessna Caravan (amphib)** for remote water access; helicopters for survey and lodge support.

### How far in advance should I request quotes for peak season?

For June–September, submit details several weeks ahead to secure aircraft and ideal times.
