### [Charter Flights To Charter Flights To Port Elgin](#collapseOne1)

Port Elgin is located along the eastern shore of Lake Huron, north of Kincardine and south west of Owen Sound. The area is a popular destination for outdoor recreation, sailing and other water sports, plus has a good selection of golf courses. It makes a popular destination for business and vacation destinations. Port Elgin has an airport however it is turf surface which would restrict the types and size of aircraft. Alternative airports would be Kincardine to the south or Owen Sound north east.
