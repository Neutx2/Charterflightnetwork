### About Charter Flights To Peterborough

### Location

Fly private charter flights direct to Peterborough which is located on Hwy 115 north east of Oshawa and offers the business traveler a great selection of accommodations, restaurants along with recreational activities such as golf and water sports.

There is one air charter service based at the Peterborough Airport. They operate a Beech King Air which is a twin engine, tubro prop aircraft. Passengers fly in pressurized comfort, well above most weather systems. Charter flights are available to destinations across Ontario, throughout Canada and into the USA

### Peterborough Airport

Business travelers can fly private charter flights direct to the Peterborough Airport locted 4 miles south of the city. The airport is widely used by medium to large corporate and charter aircraft and features a 7000 foot asphalt runway which will handle aircraft as large as Boeing 737.

The airport has a modest terminal building that provides basic amenities for passengers and pilots. Inside, you'll find a waiting area, washrooms, and limited seating. There is ample parking available for private vehicles near the terminal building. Parking facilities are typically free of charge.

The airport offers aviation fuel services, including Jet A and 100LL avgas. Fuel can be accessed at the self-serve fueling station or through arrangements with the airport staff.
