About Woodstock New Brunswick

Woodstock is located in west central New Brunswick, just south of Florenceville and is just east of the US border with Maine. The public use airport is actually in the nearby town of Grafton which is 1 miles east of Woodstock. The runway is 3,000 feet long with a chipseal/turf surface. The airport may not be accessible to all charter aircraft - depending on weather and runway conditions.
