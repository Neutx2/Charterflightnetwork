### About Flights To Cochrane

### Location

There is an airport in Cochrane, however there are no scheduled flights. You can however book charter flights to the Cochrane Airport from virtually any departure point across the north. Plus you can book Float plane and Helicopter charter flights in the Cochrane area. For exceptional outdoor adventure book a fly-in fishing trip to a remote outpost camp or wilderness fishing lodge. Once you decide to book your flight, you contact and deal directly with the charter operator of your choice. There is no cost nor obligation when you use our Free Charter Quote Services.

### Cochrane Airport

The Cochrane Airport is located 2.5 miles north of town and features a 4,484 foot asphalt runway.

Cochrane Air Service operates float plane charter services from their base in Cochrane, Ontario on Lillabelle Lake. They provide service to fishermen, hunters, mining and exploration companies, contractors, government agencies, Remote First Nations Communities and many more. Use the link below to request a quote.
