South Indian Lake Airport is the main fixed-wing gateway for this remote northern community and sits about two nautical miles east of town. ## **What airport serves South Indian Lake, Manitoba?**

South Indian Lake is served by South Indian Lake Airport, identified by the codes XSI and CZSN. It is the main fixed-wing airport for the community and surrounding area.

## **How far is the airport from the community?**

South Indian Lake Airport is located about two nautical miles east of the community, making it a practical arrival point for passenger service, charter flights, cargo, and essential travel.

## **What type of runway does South Indian Lake Airport have?**

The airport has a crushed-rock runway measuring about 3,510 feet. That runway length is suitable for many northern turboprop and utility aircraft commonly used in remote Manitoba operations.

## **Are float plane charter flights useful for this area?**

Yes. During open-water months, float plane charters can be a useful option for shoreline access, remote camps, fishing areas, and destinations around the broader Southern Indian Lake region.
