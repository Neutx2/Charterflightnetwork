### Missinipe Airport

Missinipe has a public use airport called Otter Lake Airport, which is located 2 miles south of town. The runway is 2,500 feet in length with a gravel/clay surface making it accessible to only a small selection of charter aircraft operating in the region. Otter Lake also is a popular float plane base making it a key gateway for accessing other remote northern Saskatchewan's destinations.
