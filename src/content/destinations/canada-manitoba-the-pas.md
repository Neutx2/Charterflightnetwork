## **About The Pas:**

The Pas is one of northern Manitoba's main service and transportation centres, located on the Saskatchewan River beside Opaskwayak Cree Nation. It is known for regional commerce, historic character, and easy access to nearby lakes, forests, and outdoor recreation.

### The Pas Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Charter flights to The Pas use The Pas Airport, YQD / CYQD, a public airport northeast of town. Aviation data lists a longest runway of about 5,901 feet, supporting practical northern charter operations.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* The Pas has a stronger visitor-services base than many northern Manitoba communities. Travel Manitoba currently lists Andersen Inn and Suites in The Pas, and current traveler listings also show Wescana Inn among the town's hotel options.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Travel Manitoba highlights The Pas / OCN as a base for northern lakes, wildlife, and broader regional exploration, making it attractive for both business travelers extending a stay and leisure visitors heading north.

## What airport serves The Pas, Manitoba?

The Pas is served by The Pas Airport, with airport codes YQD and CYQD. Public airport data lists a longest runway of about 5,901 feet, suitable for a broad range of charter operations.

## Can private charter flights be arranged directly to The Pas?

Yes. Private charter flights can be arranged directly to The Pas from Winnipeg, St. Andrews, Regina, and other western Canadian departure points, depending on aircraft size, range, and availability.

## Are float plane charters available from The Pas?

Yes. The Pas is a practical starting point for remote fishing and wilderness trips, and float plane charter options may be useful for travelers continuing on to lodges, lakes, and outpost destinations.

## Are there hotel options in The Pas for overnight stays?
