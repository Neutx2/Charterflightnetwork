### Location

Hudson Ontario is located just south of Sioux Lookout. There is no airport located in Hudson, however there is a float plane base and float plane charter flights are available from this location to hundreds of lakes and rivers throughout the region.

### Hudson Airport

There is no airport in Hudson. The closest airport is in Sioux Lookout located about 20 minutes north of Hudson.

The primary float plane / ski plane base location for Slate Falls Airways is located at 29 Wellington St in Sioux Lookout located about 20 minutes north of Hudson and is easily recognized when you see the big blue hangar on your way in to town.
