About Akulivik Quebec

Discover The Convenience and Affordability of Flying Direct to or From Akulivik Quebec In The Speed Comfort and Security of A Private Charter Flight
Akulivik is a remote community located in the far northwest region of Quebec along the eastern shores of Hudson Bay. The town is served by a local airport located 1.5 miles southwest of the community. There is a single gravel runway which is 3,500 feet long which makes it accessible by many of the charter aircraft available throughout the region.
