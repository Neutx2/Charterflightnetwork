### About Aklavik Northwest Territories and Location

Aklavik, nestled in the vast wilderness of the Northwest Territories, offers a unique blend of cultural heritage and natural beauty. This remote community, accessible only by "charter flights" and waterways during the summer, is a gateway to exploring the untouched landscapes of the North. Visitors are drawn to Aklavik for its rich history, traditional lifestyles, and the opportunity to witness the spectacular Northern Lights.

### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Aklavik to Yellowknife**

The distance from Aklavik to Yellowknife is about 700 miles (1126 kilometers), showcasing the remoteness of Aklavik and the vital role of charter flights in connecting it with the territorial capital.

**Aklavik to Edmonton**

Traveling from Aklavik to Edmonton covers a distance of approximately 1,500 miles (2414 kilometers), highlighting the importance of air travel in bridging the vast distances to major urban centers.

**Aklavik to Fort Resolution**

Aklavik is roughly 800 miles (1287 kilometers) from Fort Resolution, emphasizing the isolated nature of the community and the necessity of charter flights for transportation and supplies.

**Aklavik to Fort Simpson**

The journey from Aklavik to Fort Simpson spans about 600 miles (965 kilometers), further underlining the critical role of air travel in connecting the diverse regions of the Northwest Territories.

### Aklavik Airport

The community has a public use airport adjacent to the town. The single runway is 3,002 feet long with a gravel surface. Many of the air charter operators listed on this page operate aircraft designed for operations in and out of 3,000 gravel runways.

### Other Nearby Airports

**Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport**

As a significant northern hub, Inuvik Mike Zubko Airport offers more extensive services and connects to various destinations, serving as an alternative entry point for visitors to the region.

**Tuktoyaktuk Airport**

Located on the coast of the Arctic Ocean, Tuktoyaktuk Airport provides unique access to northern coastal communities and the polar landscape, enhancing the region's tourism potential.

**Norman Wells Airport**

This airport serves as a key connection for communities and oil fields along the Mackenzie River, facilitating travel and logistics in the central Northwest Territories.

**Fort McPherson Airport**

Fort McPherson Airport offers access to the eastern regions of the Northwest Territories, supporting local communities with transport and supply services.

### Aklavik Hotels / Accommodations

**River's Edge Inn**

The River's Edge Inn in Aklavik provides cozy accommodations for travelers looking to explore the area. Offering warm, comfortable rooms and local cuisine, it's the perfect base for your northern adventure.

### Aklavik Outdoor Adventures

The area around Aklavik is a paradise for outdoor enthusiasts. With activities ranging from fishing in the pristine waters of the Taltson River to hunting, kayaking, and snowmobiling, there's no shortage of adventure. During the winter months, the Northern Lights provide a breathtaking natural display. Local companies like Arctic Adventure Tours offer guided experiences, ensuring visitors capture the full beauty and thrill of the Northwest Territories.
