About Mont Joli Quebec

Mont Joli is located in south eastern Quebec and can be found on the southern shores of the St Lawrence River, east of Rimouski and across the river from Forestville on the north shore. The local public use airport is 2 miles northwest of Mont Joli and has two asphalt runways - the longest of which is 5,000 feet long making it well suited to all charter aircraft in the region.
