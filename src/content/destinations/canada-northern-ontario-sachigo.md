### About Flights To Sachigo

### Location

Sachigo is a remote and isolated settlement located in the north west corner of northwestern Ontario. There is no road access so residents and visitors need to travel by air. You can book scheduled or charter flights direct to the Sachigo Airport or you can arrange float plane charter flights to Sachigo or to the surrounding remote lakes and rivers. Travelers should be aware not to expect hotel nor restaurant facilities in this community.

### Sachigo Airport

The Sachigo Airport is located just under 2 miles north of the community and features a 3,506 foot gravel runway. The community is served by 3 scheduled airlines: Bearskin Airlines, North Star Air each offering flights between Sioux Lookout and Thunder Bay and Perimeter Aviation offering flights between Sandy Lake and Winnipeg.
