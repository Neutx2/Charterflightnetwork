### Float Plane Charter Flights From Natashquan Quebec

Natashquan is a popular starting point for exceptional fly-in fishing and hunting adventures in eastern Quebec and Labrador. Air Tunilik operates a float plane base in Natashquan and provides transportation to dozens of remote lodges and outfitters fishing for Northern Pike, Walleyes, Speckled Trout, and Atlantic Salmon.

Guests who are looking for a truly unique and world class fishing experience can fly schedule flights from Toronto or USA to Halifax Nova Scotia. Upon arrival you will connect with a pre arranged, twin engine wheel plane charter that will fly you 375 air miles north to the Natashquan airport where you will connect with the waiting float plane for your final leg of the journey direct to your lodge.

With these seamless connections and good flying weather, you can actually leave your home in the morning and be on the lake fishing deep in the Quebec wilderness that same afternoon.
