### About Saskatoon, Saskatchewan and Location

Saskatoon, the vibrant heart of Saskatchewan, offers a gateway to the pristine wilderness of the province with its strategic location. As a hub for charter flights, Saskatoon connects adventure seekers to remote destinations, highlighting its rich cultural heritage, diverse attractions, and numerous outdoor activities that appeal to visitors year-round.

**Saskatoon to Stony Rapids**

**Saskatoon to Yorkton**

### Saskatoon Airport

The Saskatoon John G Diefenbaker International Airport is the busiest airport in the province and is served by numerous regional, domestic and international airlines. The airport is an airport of entry and is staffed by the Canada Border Services Agency. Two runways - 8,300 feet and 6,200 feet ensure that the airport is accessible by virtually all types of charter aircraft. Contact the charter operators listed on this page for details on the types of aircraft available for charter flights to the Saskatoon Airport.

### Other Nearby Airports

**Regina International Airport**

Located in the capital city of Regina, this airport features a 7900-foot runway and provides comprehensive services for travelers across the province.

**Prince Albert (Glass Field) Airport**

With a 5000-foot runway, it serves as a northern gateway, offering flights to and from Saskatoon and other destinations.

**Moose Jaw Municipal Airport**

This airport boasts a 4200-foot runway and supports regional flights, playing a crucial role in southern Saskatchewan's air transport.

**Swift Current Airport**

Facilitating access to southwestern Saskatchewan, Swift Current Airport has a 3900-foot runway and hosts a variety of aviation services.
