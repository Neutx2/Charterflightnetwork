### Brandon Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Brandon Municipal Airport is located roughly 1.6 kilometres north of the city. The main asphalt runway is long enough for many turboprops and light business aircraft, making Brandon one of Manitoba’s more flexible regional charter destinations.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Brandon offers a full range of visitor services, including hotels, restaurants, vehicle rentals, event facilities, and regional business support. Travelers arriving by charter can expect far more ground transportation and accommodation choices than at most remote Manitoba destinations.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Visitors come to Brandon for business, university events, agriculture, sports tournaments, and regional tourism. Attractions include the Commonwealth Air Training Plan Museum on airport grounds, Keystone Centre events, and access to western Manitoba recreation and parkland areas.

## Brandon Charter Flight FAQ

## **What airport serves Brandon, Manitoba?**

Brandon is served by Brandon Municipal Airport, using the codes YBR and CYBR. It is the main airport for Brandon and a key aviation hub for western Manitoba.

## **How far is Brandon Municipal Airport from the city?**

Brandon Municipal Airport is just north of Brandon, approximately 1.6 kilometres from the city. That close proximity makes transfers simple for business travelers, event guests, and regional visitors.

## **What types of aircraft can use Brandon Municipal Airport?**

With a main asphalt runway of about 6,510 feet, Brandon Municipal Airport can accommodate many charter turboprops and light business aircraft used for private, corporate, and regional air travel.

## **Are there services for visitors arriving in Brandon?**

Yes. Brandon offers hotels, restaurants, vehicle rentals, event venues, and business services, making it much easier to support overnight stays and scheduled meetings than at most remote Manitoba charter destinations.
