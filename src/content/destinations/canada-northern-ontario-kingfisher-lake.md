## About Flights To Kingfisher Lake

### Location

Both Wasaya Airways and North Star Airways provide direct flights to the community of Kingfisher Lake which is a remote and isolated First Nation's community located in Northwestern Ontario and is accessible only by air. Wasaya Airways provides daily schedules airline service from Thunder Bay and Sioux Lookout. However if scheduled airlines do not meet your requirements, you can book charter flights to the Kingfisher Lake Airport from Pickle Lake, Sioux Lookout, Red Lake or numerous other departure points

### Kingfisher Lake Airport

The Kingfisher Lake Airport is located just under 2 miles southwest of the community and features a 3,500 foot gravel runway
