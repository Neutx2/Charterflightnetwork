Cree Lake is a gem nestled in the vast wilderness of Saskatchewan, known for its pristine beauty and untouched landscapes. The area is a haven for adventurers and nature lovers, offering a plethora of activities such as fishing, canoeing, and wildlife viewing. Charter flights to Cree Lake provide an exclusive gateway to exploring this remote destination, making it accessible for those seeking solitude and a unique outdoor experience away from the hustle and bustle of crowded tourist spots.

### Cree Lake Airport

The Cree Lake Airport is a private air strip owned and operated by the Crystal Lodge - which is a fly-in fishing lodge located on Cree Lake. The runway is 3,185 feet in length with a clay surface. Check with the charter companies listed on this page for the types of aircraft approved for operation in and out of this type of airport.

**La Ronge (Barber Field) Airport**

Located in the heart of northern Saskatchewan, Barber Field Airport features a 1,200-meter runway and offers essential services, including refueling and maintenance.

As a key hub in southern Saskatchewan, Regina International boasts a 2,400-meter runway, with comprehensive services catering to both commercial and private flights.

**Stony Rapids Airport**
