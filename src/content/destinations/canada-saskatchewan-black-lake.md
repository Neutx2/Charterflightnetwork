### About Black Lake, Saskatchewan and Location

Black Lake, Saskatchewan, is a hidden jewel in the northern part of the province, offering pristine wilderness, rich cultural heritage, and unparalleled tranquility. Ideal for adventurers and those seeking solitude, Black Lake's remote location makes charter flights a preferred method of travel, ensuring a unique and personal journey to this untouched destination.

### Black Lake Airport

Black Lake is located in far north central Saskatchewan. There is no land airport, however the community is has access to the Stony Rapids Airport locted just a few miles west. Passengers can also by float plane and helicopter direct to Black Lake. See the charter companies listed on this page for details.

### Other Nearby Airports

**Fond-du-Lac Airport**

Located 150 miles south of Black Lake, it serves as another key point for accessing the northern wilderness, with a 3,500-foot runway.

**Stony Rapids Airport**

About 80 miles away, Stony Rapids Airport is a crucial hub for northern Saskatchewan, offering scheduled and charter flight services.

**Points North Landing Airport**

Another vital northern airport, approximately 200 miles southeast of Black Lake, facilitating access to the far north's unique destinations.

**Wollaston Lake Airport**

Located 300 miles southwest, it provides additional connectivity for travelers exploring northern Saskatchewan's natural wonders.
