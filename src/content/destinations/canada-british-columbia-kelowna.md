### The Kelowna International Airport

The Kelowna International Airport is located 7 miles northeast of the city and features a single runway that is 8,900 feet long, making it accessible to all charter aircraft and scheduled commercial airlines across Canada and throughout the USA. Numerous airlines provide scheduled service in and out of the airport with scheduled year round flights to Vancouver, Victoria, Edmonton, Calgary, Toronto, Montreal as well as seasonal flights to Seattle, Mexico, and Phoenix. This website however, is designed to provide charter flight options to anyone who needs to travel but wants to avoid the crowds, congestion and security lines associated with public airport terminals and scheduled airline flights.

### The City Of Kelowna BC

The city of Kelowan is located on Okanagan Lake which, along with mild winters and moderate summer temperatures, makes it an extremely popular all season tourism destination. In the summer months, visitors come from all around the world to experience the region's unlimited boating and water sporting activities, exceptional and challenging golf courses, wine tours and resorts, biking and hiking. In the winter months the region sees a huge influx of visitors who come to enjoy the area ski hills and snow boarding. Popular resorts that cater to skiers include the Big White And Silver Star Ski Resorts.
