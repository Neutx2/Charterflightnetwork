### About Regina, Saskatchewan and Location

Regina, the heart of Saskatchewan, is a vibrant city rich in history and natural beauty. Known for its lush parks, cultural attractions, and exciting activities, Regina offers a unique experience for visitors. With its convenient location, Regina serves as a gateway to exploring remote destinations in Saskatchewan through charter flights.

### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Regina to Saskatoon**

Traveling from Regina to Saskatoon covers a distance of about 150 air miles (240 km), connecting two major cities in Saskatchewan seamlessly.

**Regina to Stony Rapids**

The air journey from Regina to Stony Rapids spans approximately 615 air miles (985 km), offering a gateway to the pristine northern wilderness.

**Regina to La Ronge, SK**

Charter flights from Regina to La Ronge cover around 325 air miles (520 km), a journey into the heart of Northern Saskatchewan's beauty.

**Regina to Yorkton**

The distance from Regina to Yorkton is about 120 air miles (193 km), a short trip to explore the eastern charm of Saskatchewan.

### Regina Airport

The Regina International Airport is located 2 miles south west of the city. The longest runway is 7,900 feet with asphalt surface - making it accessible by virtually all charter aircraft operating across Canada or USA. The airport offers various services, making it a perfect starting point for charter flights to remote destinations.

### Other Nearby Airports

**Moose Jaw Municipal Airport**

Located 40 miles west of Regina, this airport has a 1,500-meter runway. It offers basic services and serves as an alternative departure point.

**Saskatoon John G. Diefenbaker International Airport**

With a 2,700-meter runway, it's located in Saskatoon and provides comprehensive services for travelers.

**Prince Albert (Glass Field) Airport**

This airport, 140 miles north of Regina, has a 1,500-meter runway and offers limited services.

**Swift Current Airport**

About 170 miles west of Regina, Swift Current Airport has a 1,400-meter runway with basic aviation services available.

**Delta Hotels by Marriott Regina**
