### Location

Armstrong Ontario is just under a 3 hour drive north of Thunder Bay and is a popular jumping off spot for fly-in hunting and fishing trips. There is a vast wilderness area north of Armstrong and each year hundreds of sportsmen from all across North America come here to enjoy a truly memorable wilderness experience. You can arrange float plane charter flights from Armstrong to a huge variety of remote lakes, fishing lodges and outpost camps throughout the northern region. There is also an airport located just south east of the town which is suitable for most wheel plane charter aircraft in the region. Once you decide to book your flight, you contact and deal directly with the charter operator of your choice. There is no cost nor obligation when you use our Free Charter Quote Services

### Armstrong Airport

The Armstrong Airport is located 5 miles south east of the community and features a 4,000 foot ashphalt runway.

It is 135 air miles from Thunder Bay to Armstrong. You can save 3 hours of driving with a direct flight from Thunder Bay to Armstrong
