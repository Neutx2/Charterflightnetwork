### **Pitt Meadows Airport**

The airport is located along the Fraser River and also offers a float plane base adjacent to the airport, meaning that you can book either wheel plane or float plane charters directly to Pitt Meadows. The airport is located about 20 miles east of downtown Vancouver and is served by Harbour Air with float plane flights to Victoria harbour.

If you are searching for a wheel plane charter out of Pitt Meadows, you will save money by choosing to fly out of Abbotsford, Boundary Bay or Vancouver South Terminal. Otherwise, the aircraft would need to re position over to Pitt Meadows to pick you up, which will cost you additional.

### **[Charter Flights To / From USA](/directory/wheel-plane-charters-usa-bc)**

Pitt Meadows is not classified as an Airport of Entry. If your flight originates in the US, you will need to first land at an AOE such as Vancouver International.

### **[Float Plane Charter Flights](/directory/float-plane-charters-southern-bc)**

Float Plane charter services are available out of Pitt Meadows with Fort Langley Air. Fort Langley Air offers sight seeing tours as well as float plane charters to dozens of distant lakes and rivers throughout southern BC. More float plane charter options are available out of the Vancouver Harbour or South Terminal, such as Harbour Air, Seair Seaplanes and Van City Seaplanes.

Helicopter charter services are available through Sierra Helicopters based in Pitt Meadows. Their fleet includes EUROCOPTER EC120, AS350, AS355 / BELL 206B
