### Here Are Some Of The More Popular Beaches That You Will Want to Visit While In Anguilla

**Shoal Bay East:** Known for its powdery white sand and clear turquoise waters, Shoal Bay East is often considered one of the most beautiful beaches in the Caribbean.
**Meads Bay:** Meads Bay is another stunning beach with soft white sand and calm waters, perfect for swimming and sunbathing. It's also home to several luxury resorts and beachfront restaurants and beach bars perfect for enjoying a refreshing drink or delicious meal with a view.
**Rendezvous Bay:** Rendezvous Bay stretches for two miles along Anguilla's southern coast, offering picturesque views of the surrounding cliffs and crystal-clear waters.
