### You can take a private charter flight direct to the Hudsons Hope Airport which is located just under 4 miles northwest of town. The airport has a 5,200 foot asphalt runway making it accessible to a wide range of charter aircraft from small single engine, high performance turbine aircraft as well as large pasenger jets.

There are no scheduled commercial flights to Hudson's Hope BC, however if you wish to take a scheduled flight to the region, you have several choices of airline destinations within the region, including Fort St John located north east of town, Dawson Creek located south east of town, Grande Prairie Alberta located south east of town and Prince George BC located south of Hudson's Hope. Upon arrival at any of these airports, you would need to arrange rental car for the drive to Hudson's Hope.
