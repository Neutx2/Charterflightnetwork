## Your Smithers Air Charter Options

### Location Of Smithers

Direct charter flights are available to Smithers which is located in northwestern British Columbia on the Yellowhead Highway. Passengers will arrive at the Smithers Airport which is located two and a half miles north of town. The airport has a single asphalt runway which is 7,500 feet long, making it accessible to virtually all charter aircraft types that are available throughout the region.

### Charter Flights From Abbotsford

It is 450 air miles from Abbotsford to Smithers, BC. When flying this distance across British Columbia's rugged landscape you will want to fly in a presuurized, high performance aircraft certified for all weather operations. Depending on the type of aircraft, your flight time will average just over 2 hours.
