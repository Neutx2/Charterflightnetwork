### Location

Bonaventure is located in south eastern Quebec on the Gaswpe Peninsula near the Bonaventure River and the border with New Brunswick. It can be found on the Baie es Chaleurs which is just off the Gulf of St Lawrence.

### Bonaventure Airport

The town has a public use airport 2 miles northeast with one asphalt runway which is 5,985 feet long.
