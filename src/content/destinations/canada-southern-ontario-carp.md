### Location

The Carp Airport is located about 40 km west of downtown Ottawa whic is about a 40 minute drive.

### Carp Airport

If you want a convenient arrival airport without the crowds and security delays of the International Airport, you can fly private charter flights direct to the Carp Airport located just over a mile south of town. With a 4000 foot asphalt runway the airport can accommodate a wide range of charter and corporate aircraft.
