Charter flights to Old Crow offer an exclusive gateway to exploring pristine rivers, majestic mountain ranges, and the unparalleled beauty of the Arctic tundra.

### Old Crow Airport

Old Crow Airport is a vital lifeline for the village, featuring a runway 4000 feet in length. Located merely a short drive from the town center, it facilitates access to this remote community. The airport provides basic amenities and services, including charter flight operations to various destinations, making it a hub for travelers seeking to explore the Yukon's untouched beauty.

### Other Nearby Airports

**Erik Nielsen Whitehorse International Airport**

Located in Whitehorse, this airport features a runway of 9,500 feet and offers a range of services, including flights to major Canadian cities and seasonal international destinations.

**Dawson City Airport**

This airport, with a runway length of 5,000 feet, serves as a gateway to the historic town of Dawson City. It provides essential services for tourists and residents alike.

**Mayo Airport**

With a 5,400-foot runway, Watson Lake Airport is a key entry point for exploring the southern Yukon and the scenic Liard River basin.
