### Taltson River Airport

The Northwest Territories Power Corporation owns and operates a private airstrip at Taltson River which is located in the south central region of NWT just north of the border with Alberta. The single runway is 3,792 feet long with a sand/gravel surface.

As a gateway to the South Slave Region, Fort Smith Airport plays a pivotal role in connecting the southern parts of the Northwest Territories with the broader northern regions.
