### Location

Sept-Iles is located in south eastern region of Quebec along the north shore of the St Lawrence River. Sept-Iles is a busy travel destination and the local public use airport which is located 5 miles east of town is served by several regional airlines.

### Sept-Iles Airport

The airport has two asphalt runways one of which is 6,550 feet and the other is 5,770 feet - making the airport accessible by virtually all the charter aircraft available throughout the region. Charter flights are regularly arranged from Quebec, Labrador, Newfoundland and New Brunswick
