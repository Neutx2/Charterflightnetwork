Mining remains an important part of the local economy, but the town also attracts visitors looking for fishing, boating, beaches, hiking, golf, winter recreation, and access to the surrounding boreal landscape.

### Snow Lake Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Charter flights to Snow Lake use Snow Lake Airport, CJE4, located northeast of town. The airport has a gravel runway and supports northern fixed-wing access for mining, tourism, business travel, and general regional transportation.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Snow Lake offers more visitor services than many smaller northern communities. Local accommodations may include the Snow Lake Motor Inn, Gold Rush Motel, and other town-based lodging options, while nearby Wekusko-area lodging can also be useful for visitors exploring the region.

**Things to Do and Local Attractions**

* Popular activities in and around Snow Lake include fishing, boating, beaches, golf, snowmobiling, cross-country skiing, and museum visits. Nearby Wekusko Falls Provincial Park is a major regional attraction with waterfalls, trails, scenic viewpoints, and campground access.
