### Location

Fort Severn is the most northern community in Ontario and is accessible only by air. You can book charter flights direct to the Fort Severn Airport from almost any airport across Ontario or Canada. You can also arrange float plane charters to Fort Severn, however they are not as cost efficient as wheel plane charter flights. Because of the distance to Fort Severn, a charter flight from any of the main departure points in Ontario will be fairly costly.

You can fly Wasaya Airways 535 air miles from Thunder Bay to Fort Severn with stops in Sioux Lookout and Big Trout Lake before arrival in Fort Severn. ### Fort Severn Airport

The Fort Severn Airport is 3.5 air miles northwest of the community and has a 3,500 foot gravel runway.
