There is plenty of great hunting and fishing in the regions north of Elliot Lake and each year sportsmen come from all over North America to spend 5 - 7 days fishing and exploring thearea's vast wilderness. Many fishermen book float plane charter flights north of Elliot Lake to some of the remote outpost camps and fly-in fishing lodges. The Elliot Lake Airport has a 4,495 foot asphalt runway and is located less than 5 milessouth east of the city.
