### Location

Gatineau is located in south western Quebec, just across the river from Ottawa

### Gatineau Airport

There are no scheduled flights into Gatineau, but it is a busy charter and general aviation airport. There are some services available at the airport, such as car rentals, etc. The airport is located 1 mile northeast of town and has a single 6,000 foot asphalt runway, making it accessible to all types of charter aircraft including large passenger jets.
