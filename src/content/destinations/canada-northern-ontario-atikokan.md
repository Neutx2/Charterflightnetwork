### Location

Although there are no wheel plane charter services based at the Atikokan Airport, you can easily arrange a charter flight to or from the Atikokan airport from departure points across Ontario, throughout Canada as well as USA. If you are planning a fly-in fishing in the Atikokan area, you can fly direct to the Atikokan Airport with seamless connections with the area float plane charter service who will fly you direct to the lodge or outpost of your choice.

If you are driving, Atikokan is located on Hwy 11 just an hour and 45 minutes east of Fort Frances. Then just 18 miles east of Atikokan you will find the turn off to Eva Lake and Kashabowie Outposts and Sapawe Air. They specialize in flying groups in to remote fishing lodges and outpost camps throuhgout the entire Northwestern Ontario region.
