## **About Gods River:**

Gods River is a remote Manto Sipi Cree Nation community on the north shore of Gods Lake. Gods River Airport sits beside the settlement and features a crushed rock runway that can handle many northern wheel aircraft, while seasonal float plane service opens additional access to nearby lakes, lodges, and outpost areas.

### Gods River Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Gods River Airport is located right beside the community, making it practical for passenger and freight flights using northern wheel aircraft. The local runway gives direct access to the settlement, and float aircraft can supplement service when lake conditions permit.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* For visiting anglers and wilderness travelers, Gods River Lodge is the best-known accommodation in the area. Community services are limited compared with larger northern centres, so most visitors should confirm lodging, meals, and transfers before departure.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* The Gods Lake and Gods River region is known for remote fishing, wilderness scenery, and fly-in outdoor travel. Anglers, eco-adventure visitors, and groups heading to outcamps often use charter aircraft to reach lakes, rapids, and lodge-based excursions.
