**Airport and Flight Access**

* Charter flights to Dauphin use Lt. Col. W.G. (Billy) Barker VC Airport, YDN / CYDN, a certified public airport about 3 miles south of the city. Public airport data and the City of Dauphin confirm the airport status, while the city notes the airport location south of town.

## Flights to Dauphin, MB FAQ

## What airport serves Dauphin, Manitoba?

Dauphin is served by Lt. Col. W.G. (Billy) Barker VC Airport, with airport codes YDN and CYDN. The City of Dauphin notes that the airport is about 3 miles south of the city.

## Can business travelers charter directly to Dauphin?

Yes. Private charter flights can be arranged directly to Dauphin from Winnipeg, St. Andrews, and other prairie departure points, making same-day business travel much more efficient for meetings and site visits.

## What types of aircraft can use Dauphin Airport?

Dauphin Airport has three runways, including a 5,003-foot asphalt runway, so it can accommodate a range of charter aircraft including turboprops, light business aircraft, and other suitable wheel-equipped planes.

## Are there hotel options in Dauphin for overnight stays?

Yes. Current Dauphin-area listings include Best Western Plus Dauphin, Dauphin Super 8 Motel, Prairie Mountain Inn, Dauphin Inn Express, Canway Inn & Suites, and Sir Edgar House Bed & Breakfast.
