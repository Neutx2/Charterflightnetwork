### About Flights To Foleyet

### Location

The small town of Foleyet is located in Northern Ontario East of Wawa and South of Timmins. Float Plane charter flights are available in the region and specialize in providing fly-in fishing trips, sight seeing flights as well as air support for regional exploration companies.

### Airport

The closest airports to Foleyet are Timmins and Chapleau

Air Ivanhoe is located 7 miles west of Foleyet and 50 miles east of Chapleau on hwy 101 and 65 miles west of Timmins. From their base at the Ivanhoe River Inn, they offer float plane charter services as well as fly-in fishing trips to remote lodges and outpost camps throughout the Foleyet region. Use our Free Charter Quote Request Service to receive charter quotes from Air Ivanhoe as well as competitive quotes from other charter services in the region.
