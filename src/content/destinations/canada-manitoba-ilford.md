### Ilford Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Charter flights to Ilford use Ilford Airport, identified by IATA code ILF and ICAO code CZBD. The airport has a crushed rock runway of roughly 3,000 feet and provides a practical access point for passenger travel, service movements, and northern charter operations.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Ilford offers core community services suitable for practical travel, work-related visits, and short stays. Visitors should expect basic northern amenities rather than a large hotel market, so many trips are planned around community contacts, work arrangements, or onward regional transportation.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Ilford appeals mainly to travelers who need access to a remote northern community and the surrounding landscape. Nearby lakes, northern waterways, and the community's rail-and-air access role make it relevant for regional travel, field work, and northern Manitoba logistics.
