### Lynn Lake Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Lynn Lake Airport's long asphalt runway makes it one of the stronger northern access points for fixed-wing charter traffic, with the town only a short drive away after landing.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Lynn Lake offers practical visitor services including lodging at The Bronx Motel, along with local fuel, food, and town services that support business travelers, contractors, and anglers.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Lynn Lake is known for sport fishing, regional outfitting, the Lynn Lake Mining Town Museum, and nearby parks such as Zed Lake and Burge Lake that attract outdoor-minded visitors.
