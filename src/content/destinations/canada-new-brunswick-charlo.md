Charlo is located in the far north central region of New Brunswick, on the south shore of Chaleur Bay with the Province of Quebec on the north shore. The town has a public use airport located 5.5 miles southeast of town and is classified as an airport of entry by Nav Canada. The single asphalt runway is 6,000 feet long, making it accessible by all charter aircraft in the region.
