### **About Likely**

Book direct float plane charter flights to the small town of Likely BC located northeast of Williams Lake on the western arm of Quesnel Lake. Because of the town's location on the shores of the largest lake in the Cariboo Region of British Columbia, it is a very popular tourism and recreational destination

There is no airport located adjacent to Likely, however visitors to the region can fly private charter flights to Williams Lake and then take ground transportion to Likely.Float plane charter flights direct to Likely can be arranged however from Kelowna.

## Likely Wheel Plane Charter Flights

If you have 1 - 3 passengers with light baggage, the closest available wheel plane charter aircraft is based in 108 Mile Ranch which is 60 air miles south. Contact us for competitive quotes or for larger aircraft.

## Likely Float Plane Charter Flights

There is a De Havilland Beaver float plane based in Quesnel which is located 50 air miles northwest of Likely. Receive up to 3 competitive quotes when you use our Quote Request Service.

## Likely Helicopter Charter Flights

If you need helicopter charter support in Likely, we can arrange to send a helicopter that meet your needs up from Williams Lake located 45 air miles southwest. Let us know your requirements and we will source out for your best options.
