Grand Falls is located in the north west region of New Brunswick near the US border with Maine. The local airport is located 3 miles northeast of town and is operated by Grand Falls Aviation. It is classified as an airport of entry by Nav Canada. The runway is 4,000 feet long, however since the surface is a combination of turf, gravel and asphalt - not all charter aircraft in the region may be able to operate in and out of this airport.
