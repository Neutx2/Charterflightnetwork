### [Charter Flights To Leo Creek](#collapseOne1)

Book direct float plane charter flights to Leo Creek located north east of Smithers in the heart of the Omineca Provincal Park at the far southern tip of Takla Lake near the mouth of the Middle River which flows into Trembleur Lake. The quickest and most convenient travel option is to take a float plane charter flight from Smithers direct to Leo Creek landing at Takla Lake.

---

## Leo Creek Wheel Plane Charter Flights

There is no land airport at Leo Creek. Smithers BC is your closest wheel plane charter base. You can fly wheel plane charter direct to Smithers and then take a float plane charter 65 air miles east to Leo Creek.

## Leo Creek Float Plane Charter Flights

Your most cost efficient float plane charter option to Leo Creek will be from Telkwa located just south of Smithers and is 65 air miles west of Leo Creek. Depending on the number of passengers and gear, you have the following float plane options: Cessna 208 Caravan / Cessna 185 / Cessna U206.

## Leo Creek Helicopter Charter Flights

There are two helicopter charter services operating out of Smithers which is just 65 air miles from Leo Creek. If you need helicopter charter services you can choose from the following types: Aerospatiale AS350 B2 / BA / BA / Bell 206 B / Eurocopter AS350 B2
