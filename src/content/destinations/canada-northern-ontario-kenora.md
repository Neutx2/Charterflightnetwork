### Location

Kenora Ontario is the gateway to the Lake of the Woods and is one of the best known tourist destinations in Northwestern Ontario. You can book direct charter flights to The Kenora airport which is located 6 miles east, northeast of the town and features a 5800 foot asphalt runway. The airport is also classified as an airport of entry for general non commercial flights with 15 or less individuals on board. You can book float plane charter flights from Kenora to any of the dozens of remote outpost camps or wilderness fishing lodges north of Kenora. If you choose to spend a few days in the town of Kenora you will find unlimited activities and attractions from exceptional fishing, top rated lodges and accommodations, boat tours, fine dining and much more.

### Kenora Airport

You can book direct charter flights to The Kenora airport which is located 6 miles east, northeast of the town and features a 5800 foot asphalt runway. The airport is also classified as an airport of entry for general non commercial flights with 15 or less individuals on board.

The main base for River Air is located on Pistol Lake near Minaki which is a 40 minute drive north of Kenora. River Air also operates a satellite float plane base in Kenora conveniently located at the second street docks in downtown Kenora.
