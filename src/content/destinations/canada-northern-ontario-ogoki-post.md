### Location

Ogoki Post / Marten Falls is a remote First Nations Community accessible only by air. You can book scheduled or charter flights to Ogoki Post which is also known as Marten Falls. Air Travel is the only access to this remote community located along the Ogoki River, north of Armstrong and Nakina. Since this is a remote community, there are very limited services available to the traveling public. There are no hotels, restaurants or taxi services. There is telephone service to Ogoki Post, however your cell phone will not work here

The town of Nakina is the closest air charter base to Ogoki Post / Marten Falls with both wheel plane and float plane charter aircraft available. Use our Free Charter Quote Request Service to receive up to 3 competitive price quotes.

### Ogoki Post / Marten Falls Airport

The Ogoki Post / Marten Falls airport is located just over 2 miles north east of the community and has a 3,500 foot gravel runway
