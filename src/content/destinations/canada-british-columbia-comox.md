### **Comox Airport**

You can book direct charter flights to the Comox Airport which is actually a Canadian Forces Base which allows civilian use. The airport is 3 miles northeast of Comox with a 10,000 and 5,000 foot asphalt runway ensuring that any type of charter aircraft available is capable of using this airport.

The closest wheel plane charter serice is located in Campbell River which is a 45 minute drive north. Our featured charter service in Campbell River is BC Air and operates a single engine Cessna 172

### **[Charter Flights To / From USA](/directory/wheel-plane-charters-usa-bc)**

If your charter flight to Comox originates in the USA, you will first need to stop at a designated Airport of Entry to clear Canadian Customs. The closest AOE to Comox is Victoria Airport
