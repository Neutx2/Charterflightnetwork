## **About Silver Falls:**

Silver Falls Airport serves this Winnipeg River community with a runway suitable for smaller wheel aircraft, commuter-type charters, and select turboprop operations depending on payload and conditions. The airstrip sits just south of the settlement, and seasonal float plane access adds flexibility for waterfront cabins, fishing parties, work crews, and remote drop-offs throughout the surrounding lake-and-river country.

### Silver Falls Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Silver Falls Airport is positioned just south of the community and can handle wheel-equipped charter aircraft for passenger and freight movements. In the warmer months, float planes also expand access to nearby lakes, river camps, private cabins, and other shoreline destinations.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Silver Falls is best known as the home base of Blue Water Aviation rather than a major hotel centre, so many visitors arrange day travel, cabin stays, or accommodations elsewhere in the Whiteshell and Pine Falls area before continuing onward.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* The Silver Falls area appeals to outdoor travelers heading for boating, fishing, canoe routes, wildlife viewing, and scenic trips through the Winnipeg River and nearby Whiteshell country. It is also a practical jumping-off point for remote cabin, lodge, and outpost access
