### About Whitehorse Yukon and Location

Whitehorse, the vibrant capital of Yukon, serves as the gateway to the stunning wilderness of Northern Canada. Renowned for its breathtaking landscapes, rich history, and abundant wildlife, Whitehorse offers a unique blend of natural beauty and cultural experiences. From the mesmerizing Northern Lights to the challenging trails of the nearby mountains, Whitehorse is the perfect starting point for adventure seekers and nature lovers alike.

**Whitehorse to Faro, Yukon**

**Whitehorse to Anchorage, Alaska**

### Whitehorse Airport

Whitehorse Airport, with its 9,500 foot runway, easily accommodates all types of charter aircraft from across Canada. The airport is located just a few miles from downtown. It serves as a crucial hub for charter flights to remote destinations throughout Yukon, offering essential services like fuel, maintenance, and comfortable lounges for travelers.

### Other Nearby Airports

**Dawson City Airport**

Located in the heart of the Klondike Gold Rush country, Dawson City Airport features a runway length that accommodates various aircraft, providing access to historical sites and wilderness adventures.

**Old Crow Airport**

Situated in the only Yukon community north of the Arctic Circle, Old Crow Airport is a vital link to the Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation, offering unique cultural and wildlife experiences.

**Mayo Airport**

Mayo Airport serves as the gateway to the stunning landscapes of the Silver Trail and the pristine wilderness of central Yukon, with services tailored to adventurers and explorers.

**Haines Junction Airport**

Nestled at the doorstep of Kluane National Park, Haines Junction Airport offers unparalleled access to some of North America's most stunning mountainous terrain and outdoor adventures.
