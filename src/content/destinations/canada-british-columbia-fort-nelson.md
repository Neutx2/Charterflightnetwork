### **Fort Nelson Airport**

The local airport is called the Northern Rockies Regional Airport and is located four and a half miles northeast of town. It is a vital air transportation center for the north eastern region of British Columbia with two asphalt runways the longest of which is 6,400 feet long making it accessible to virtually all charter aircraft in the region.

If you are flying by charter flight or by scheduled airlines to any region in the far north eastern region of British Columbia, your best airport choice is the Northern Rockies Regional Airport located in Fort Nelson, BC. The Northern Rockies Regional Municipality includes not only Fort Nelson, but Toad River and Muncho Lake and is a popular destination for hunters and fishermen coming to Northern BC to experience the exceptional outdoor opportunities throughout the region.

### **Scheduled Flights To The Northern Rockies Regional Airport**

The airport is also served by two regional airlines, Central Mountain Air and Flair Airlines Charter. You can book direct flights between Fort Nelson and Dawsson Creek, Fort St John, Prince George, Vancouver, Kelowna and Comox.
