### Gore Bay - Manitoulin Airport

Located on the north end of Lake Huron at the mouth to Georgian Bay, you can fly direct to the Manitowaning / Manitoulin East Municipal Airport located about 9 miles north of town. The local airport has a 3500 foot asphalt runway which can easily accommodate a wide variety of charter aircraft and corporate jets.

There are no air charter services based on Manitoulin Island. We can however connect you with wheel plane, float plane or helicopter charter services out of Elliot Lake or Sudbury.
