About Lac-à-la-Tortue Quebec

Discover The Convenience and Affordability of Flying Direct to or From Lac-à-la-Tortue Quebec In The Speed Comfort and Security of A Private Charter Flight
Lac-à-la-Tortue is located in south central Quebec, near Shawinigan and north of Trois Rivieres. There is a privately owned airport, operated by Bel Air Laurentian Aviation located 3 miles east of town. The airport has two asphalt runways the longest of which is 3,000 feet. The airport is classified as an airport of entry and is staffed by the Canadian Border Services Agency.
