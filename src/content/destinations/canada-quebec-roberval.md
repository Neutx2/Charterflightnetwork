### About Flights To Roberval

### Location

Roberval is located in south central Quebec - north of Saguenay and Bagotville and can be found on the western shores of Lac Saint Jean.

### Roberval Airport

The town has a public use airport just 2 miles to the west of Roberval. The runway is 5,000 feet with asphalt surface. A wide range of charter aircraft are capable of using this airport.
