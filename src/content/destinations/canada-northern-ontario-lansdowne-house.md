### Location

Lansdowne House / Neskantaga is a remote and isolated First Nations Community located in North Western Ontario north of Nakina. The only travel option to Lansdowne is by aircraft - either scheduled or charter airline. Wasaya Airways offers scheduled service from Thunder Bay and Sioux Lookout. If however scheduled airlines cannot meet your travel needs you can book direct charter flights from numerous departure airports across the North including Nakina, Armstrong, Pickle Lake and Thunder Bay.

If you are looking for a cost efficient charter flight option, compared to flying out of Thunder Bay - you could drive to Nakina from Thunder Bay which is just under 4 hours and then connect with Nakina Air Services for the 150 air mile flight to Lansdowne House.
