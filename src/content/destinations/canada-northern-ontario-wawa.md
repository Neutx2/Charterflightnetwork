### About Flights To Wawa

### Location

The Wawa area is about a 2 hour drive north of Sault Ste Marie via highway 17. The Wawa area is an extremely popular destination for hunters and fishermen from across Canada and USA. There are hundreds of remote lakes north of the Wawa area which are accessible only by float plane and there are several Air Charter companies in the area who specialize in providing charter flights to remote lodges and outposts.

Once you decide to book your flight, you contact and deal directly with the charter operator of your choice. There is no cost nor obligation when you use our Free Charter Quote Services

### Wawa Airport

The Wawa Airport is located 2 miles south west of town and features a 4,430 foot asphalt runway.

### Wawa Float Plane Charters

Watson's Skyways is based in Wawa and operates a Cessna 208 Caravan on amphibious floats which means the aircraft can operate from both land airports as well as lakes and rivers. They can for example, meet you at the Sault Ste Marie airport and then fly you direct to any number of lodges, outposts or exploration sites in the Wawa region. Use our Free Charter Quote Request Service to receive a no obligation price quote directly from Watson's Skyways.
