### You can take private float plane charter flights to Telegraph Creek which will drop you off about 1 mile southwest of town. There is also a little used gravel air strip which can be accessed by a small number of aircraft such as the single engine Otter designed for rough strip operations. Charter flights are available from departure points throughout British Columbia and the Yukon.

If your passion is unique and interesting outdoor photography - then Telegraph Creek may be the perfect place for you to visit and spend a few days. The miles of steep river banks, rocky gorges and surrounding mountains make the perfect backdrop for spectacular photography. If you go, take your own camping gear and supplies as you won't find much in the way of accommodations, however there is a general store.

## Telegraph Creek Wheel Plane Charter Flights

We can arrange wheel plane charter flights to Telegraph Creek from Vancouver, but it is almost 700 air miles, so it will be a very expensive flight. The other options are charter flights out of Smithers which is 270 air miles or a charter flight out of Whitehorse which is 240 air miles. Let us know you requirements and we can get you price quotes on all options.
