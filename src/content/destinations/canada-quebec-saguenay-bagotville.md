### Location

Saguenay is located along the Saguenay River in south central Quebec and north of the St Lawrence River. There is no airport in Saguenay - the closest airport is Bagotville (see Bagotville for Details) There is however a busy float plane charter base here run by Air Saguenay.

### Saguenay Airport

The Bagotville Airport is a Canadian Forces Base which is used by civilian aircraft and is classified as an airport of entry by the Canadian Border Services Agency. The airport is located about 5 miles west of Bagotville in the city of Saguenay. It has two main runways, the longest of which is 10,000 feet in length.
