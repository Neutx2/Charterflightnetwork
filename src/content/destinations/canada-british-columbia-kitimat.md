### **Kitimat Airport**

If you plan to book a private charter flight direct to Kitimat, BC - you have two airport choices. Depending on the number of passengers and the type of aircraft that your charter company provides, you may be able to land at the Kitimat Airport located 8 miles north of town. The runway here is gravel surface and is 3,000 feet in length so if the aircraft is approved for gravel operations and the weather is co-operative you may be able to arrive at the Kitimat Airport.

If the charter company does not operate to and from this airport, your alternative is the much larger and nearby Northwest Regional Airport in Terrace located 35 miles north of Kitimat. This airport is served by several scheduled airlines such as Air Canada Express / Canadian North Charter / Central Mountain Air / WestJet. There are direct flights between Terrace and Vancouver, Prince George and Smithers.

The closest wheel plane charter base is located in Smithers. We refer charter quote reuquests to Tsayta Air for any wheel plane charter requests out of the Kitimat area.
