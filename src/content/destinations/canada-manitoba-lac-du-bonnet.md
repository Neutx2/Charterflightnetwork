### Lac du Bonnet Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Lac du Bonnet Airport is just northeast of town and works well for private charter arrivals, pilot training, and regional aviation traffic. Its paved runway can handle many light charter aircraft, making it a practical alternative to busier Winnipeg-area airports.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Lac du Bonnet has a useful range of visitor services, including restaurants, shops, marinas, campgrounds, and local accommodations. Travelers can often find lodging in town or nearby cottage-country properties, including inns, resorts, and seasonal cabin rentals.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Lac du Bonnet is known for boating, fishing, beach time, golf, hiking, biking, and snowmobiling, with the Winnipeg River and surrounding lakes shaping the experience. Nearby trails, heritage stops, and scenic waterfront areas also support weekend and adventure travel.
