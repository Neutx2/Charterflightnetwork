### About Flights To Marathon Ontario

### Location

Marathon is located along the north shore of Lake Superior on Highway 17, about half way between Terrace Bay and White River. There are no scheduled flights to Marathon, however you can book direct charter flights to this community from numerous airports across northern Ontario.

### Marathon Ontario Airport

The Marathon Airport has a 3,933 foot asphalt runway and is lcoated 3 miles northeast of town.

## Marathon Wheel Plane Charter Flights

There is no air charter service based in Marathon, Ontario, we can however connect you with direct charter flights from Toronto, Sudbury, Timmins, Thunder Bay, or anydeparture point you choose. There is a 3,900 foot asphalt runwa making it accessible to a wide range of charter aircraft that will seat 1 - 35 passengers or more.

## Marathon Float Plane Charter Flights

White River is the closest float plane charter service to Marathon, which is just over an hour's drive east of Marathon via hwy 17. White River Air specializes in fly-in hunting and fishing trips, but can supply general charter flights as well. Contact us if you would like to receive quotes tailored to your needs.

## Marathon Helicopter Charter Flights

With the extensive mining and exploration activity going on in the Marathon area, there are helicopter charter services operating in the area - from time to time. This would be cheaper than bringing one in from Thunder Bay or Cochrane. Let us know your needs and we will source out and hopefully find you an option that meets your needs.
