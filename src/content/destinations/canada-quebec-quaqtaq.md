### Location

Quaqtaq is a small remote community located in the far northern regions of Quebec. It can be found along the southern shores of Hudson Strait and at the northwest tip of Ungava Bay.

### Quaqtaq Airport

The local public use airport has a gravel surface runway which is 3,520 feet long. Charter flights to this region are operated by professional companies with years of experience in arctic operations.
