### Location

White River is located in Northern Ontario between Wawa and Thunder Bay along Highway 17. The town is a well established destination for sportsmen who travel from all over North America to enjoy the regions spectacular outdoor adventures 12 months a year. There is no airport, but you can book float plane charter flights from White River to many of the remote outpost camps and fly-in fishing lodges

### White River Airport

There is no airport in White River. The closest airport is Wawa which is one hour drive south east on Highway 17.

Since the 1950's to present day, White River Air is the exclusive provider of high quality float plane charter services out of White River, Ontario. Whether you are seeking a remote fly-in fishing adventure to a secluded outpost camp, or need to fly men, fuel and equipment into an exploraion site accessible only by float plane, White Rive Air can provide the solution.
