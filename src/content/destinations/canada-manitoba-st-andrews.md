Winnipeg / St. Andrews Airport is a fully controlled public airport in the heart of St. Andrews, just north of Winnipeg. With multiple asphalt runways around 3,000 feet long, it can handle a wide range of charter, training, business aviation, and utility aircraft used for northern Manitoba and Northwestern Ontario travel.

### St Andrews Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* St Andrews Airport is a strong charter base because it is close to Winnipeg yet designed for general aviation use. Multiple asphalt runways, a control tower, fuel, maintenance, and charter operators make it well suited to northern departures.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Most visitors using St Andrews Airport stay in Winnipeg or nearby Selkirk rather than at the airport itself. The area does, however, offer practical aviation services, food, accommodations on the aerodrome, and car rental within 30 nautical miles.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* St Andrews works best as a departure hub, but the surrounding area also offers heritage and recreation options such as Lower Fort Garry, Selkirk, golf, and day trips along the Red River corridor before or after a charter flight.
