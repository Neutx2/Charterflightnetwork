### About Red Deer Alberta

Red Deer is a progessive and growing community of just under 100,000 residents and located about halfway between Calgary and Edmonton on Hwy 32. Amongst vital area industries located here, are Oil and Gas, Grain, Cattle and Petrochemical Production. There are many area attractions which keep visitors coming back to spend time in the area. With a dozen golf courses all within a 20 mile radius of downtown, golfers are particularly blessed with plenty to keep them busy. the 18 hole Balmoral golf course located right in town and open to the public is an area favorite.

### What to See and Do While In Red Deer

Whether you are travelling to Red Deer for business or vacation, here are some of the local attractions you won't want to miss.

**Heritage Ranch:** A recreational facility offering horseback riding, wagon rides, and dining experiences.
**Bower Ponds:** A picturesque park with walking trails, picnic areas, paddleboat rentals, and a natural amphitheater for events.
**Discovery Canyon:** A man-made canyon featuring a natural river for tubing and swimming during the summer months.
**Alberta Sports Hall of Fame and Museum:** Showcasing Alberta's sporting history through exhibits and interactive displays.
**Kerry Wood Nature Centre:** A nature reserve with interpretive trails, educational programs, and wildlife viewing opportunities.
