### About Holman Northwest Territories and Location

Located in the heart of the Northwest Territories, Holman is a picturesque community known for its stunning landscapes and rich cultural heritage. This remote destination offers a plethora of attractions including traditional art, wildlife viewing, and the chance to witness the mesmerizing northern lights. Holman serves as the perfect gateway for those seeking adventure and exploration in the untouched wilderness of the Northwest Territories.

### Distance From Regional Cities and Towns

**Holman to Yellowknife**

The distance from Holman to Yellowknife is approximately 700 miles (1126 km), showcasing the remote yet accessible nature of Holman for travelers seeking solitude and adventure.

**Holman to Edmonton**

Connecting Holman with Edmonton, the journey spans about 1,350 miles (2,173 km), illustrating the vast landscapes and scenic beauty en route to one of Canada's northernmost communities.

**Holman to Norman Wells**

From Holman to Norman Wells, the distance is around 500 miles (805 km), a testament to the diverse and expansive geography of the Northwest Territories.

**Holman to Fort Simpson**

The trek from Holman to Fort Simpson covers roughly 600 miles (965 km), offering travelers a glimpse into the remote and rugged beauty of the region.

### Holman Airport

Holman Airport is a vital hub for accessing the remote wonders of the Northwest Territories. With a runway length of 3,500 feet, it accommodates various sizes of aircraft, facilitating the journey to this secluded paradise. Located just a few miles from the town, the airport offers essential services, making your arrival and departure as smooth as possible.

Located in Inuvik, offering a runway of 6,000 feet and services including car rentals and accommodations booking, it's a gateway to the western Arctic.
