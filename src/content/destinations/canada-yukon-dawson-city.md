### Dawson City Airport

Dawson City Airport serves as the gateway to this historic town, located just a short drive from the center. With a 5,000-foot runway, it accommodates a range of charter flights, offering services like fuel and parking, making it an essential hub for visitors and residents alike.

### Other Nearby Airports

**Erik Nielsen Whitehorse International Airport**

Located in Whitehorse, this airport boasts a 9,500-foot runway. It's a key connector to other parts of Canada and offers comprehensive services including car rentals and dining.

With a 5,500-foot runway, Watson Lake Airport is a crucial access point for travelers heading to the southern parts of Yukon, providing basic amenities and fuel services.

**Mayo Airport**

This airport features a 4,000-foot runway, serving the Mayo region and nearby communities. Though small, it offers essential services for those exploring the central Yukon.

**Old Crow Airport**

As the only airport in the northern Yukon community of Old Crow, this facility has a 4,000-foot runway and is vital for residents and tourists seeking to explore the Arctic wilderness.
