### About Flights To Muskrat Dam

### Location

If you need to travel to Muskrat Dam Ontario and you cannot find suitable scheduled flights to meet your travel needs, you can arrange private charter flights to Muskrat Dam from a variety of airports across northern Ontario However, if you have heavy or bulky cargo to transport, there are larger twin engine cargo aircraft based in the region which operate in and out of these northern strips. This isolated community is located on the northwest shores of Muskrat Dam Lake which is part of the Severn River system

It is 340 air miles from Winnipeg to Muskrat Dam. Our Charter Quote Request Service can source out quotes for wheel and float plane charters which include King Air Aircraft, Pilatus PC12 Aircraft, Piper Navajo as well as Amphibious Caravan Aircraft.

### Muskrat Dam Airport

There is an airport at Muskrat Dam, however there are no roads to this isolated community. The runway is a 3500 gravel airstrip suitable for most charter aircraft in the light to medium twin engine category
