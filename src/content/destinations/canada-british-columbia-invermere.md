### Invermere BC

Book Direct Charter Flights To Invermere located in south eastern British Columbia on the northwest shore of Lake Windermere, near the border with Alberta. You can book private charter flights direct to the Invermere Airport which is located just under 2 miles northeast of town. The airport has a 3000 foot asphalt runway and is restricted to the use of smaller propeller aircraft. Larger charter aircraft need to use the airport at Fairmont Hot Springs located 12 miles to the south.
