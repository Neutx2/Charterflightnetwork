### [Charter Flights To Quesnel](#collapseOne1)

Private charter flights are available direct to Quesnel BC which is located in the central interior region of British Columbia. The Quesnel airport is located two and a half miles north of town and has a 5500 foot asphalt runway making it accessible to virtually all types of charter aircraft in the area.

## Quesnel Wheel Plane Charter Flights

We can arrange wheel plane charter flights direct to Quesnel out of Vancouver which is located 265 air miles south. Your most cost efficient charter departure however would be Prince George which is just 65 air miles north.

## Quesnel Float Plane Charter Flights

Fawnie Mountain Outfitters operate a float plane charter service out of Quesnel. The aircraft type: DeHavilland Beaver. Contact us for competitive quotes and availability.

## Quesnel Helicopter Charter Flights

If you need helicopter charter services in the Quesnel area, there are helicopter operators based in Prince George as well as Williams Lake, both of which are 65 air miles from Quesnel. Submit your details and we will get you competitive quotes for the best options and availability.
