### Location

Trois Riviere is located on the north shore of the Saint Lawrence River 80 air miles north east of Montreal / 80 miles west of Quebec City and 260 air miles south of Chibougamau. There are several industries that contribute to the local economy such as pulp and paper mills, electronics, wind turbine towers and aeronautical industry

### Trois Rivieres Airport

The Trois Riviere Airport is located near the northwest corner of town and is designated as an airport of entry for general aviation for flights with 15 passengers or less. The airport has a single, 9000 foot asphalt runway allowing aircraft up to the size of a Boeing 767 to operate to and from the airport.
