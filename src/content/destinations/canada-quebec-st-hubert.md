### St-Hubert Airport

St-Hubert was once the only airport serving Montreal. It is now a major alternate airport for passengers who prefer to avoid the busier Montreal Trudeau International Airport. There is a considerable amount of scheduled as well as charter traffic originating from St-Hubert which is located 10 miles east of the downtown area. The airport has three asphalt runways - the longest of which is 7,800 feet.
