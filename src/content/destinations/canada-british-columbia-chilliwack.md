### Location Of Chilliwack

This page is designed to help you find a charter service and book Charter Flights to Chilliwack which is located in southwestern British Columbia 38 km east of Abbotsford along the Trans Canada Highway which is about a 30 minute drive. Request a no obligation charter quote

Chilliwack Wheel Plane Charters
Based in Chilliwack, just 50 km east of Abbotsford, Upper Valley Aviation offers a diversified air charter fleet consisting of their 8 passenger, twin turbine Beechcraft King Air and their single engine Cessna 182 skylane perfect for 1 - 3 passengers
Chilliwack Hotels
If you will be staying in the area for a few days or overnight and you need accommodations, there is a good selection of hotels to choose from such as the Hampton Inn By Hilton which is rated #1 in Chilliwack by TripAdvisor. Other hotels include the Coast Chilliwack Hotel , Best Western Rainbow Country Inn, The Travelodge Chilliwack and the Royal Hotel
Chilliwack Airport
You will arrive at the Chilliwack Airport (YCW) which is located adjacent to the town. The Airport has an asphalt runway which is just under 4,000 feet in length making it accessible by a wide range of charter aircraft throughout the Region.
If your charter flight originates in the USA, you will need to first land at an airport of entry to clear Canadian Customs prior to landing in Chilliwack. You can clear customs in Abbotsford and then your flight can carry on for the short flight to Chilliwack, or you can just take a rental car from Abbotsford to Chilliwack.
There are no scheduled flights to Chilliwack and if you are looking for a scheduled flight to any destination throughout North America or around the world, you would need to take a flight from Abbotsford or from Vancouver - which would be a little over a 100 km drive from Chilliwack.
Book A Charter Flight To Adventure
If you are a golfer and in the area on vacation or on business and want to take clients out for a round of golf, you have a few good choices such as the Chilliwack Golf Club, The Falls Golf Club and the Meadowlands Golf and Country Club. When you fly Golfer Friendly Charter flights to Chilliwack there is never a hassle bringing along your clubs, your fishing tackle or skis and never an extra charge

Another reason for visiting the region is the spectacular fishing along the Fraser River and its many tributaries with the main species being giant sturgeon. Other species include Steelhead on the Vedder River and Trout fishing on the nearby Pitt River, plus 5 species of Pacific Salmon. There are several professional fishing charter guides available such as Great River Fishing Adventures / Cascade Fishing Adventures and Tightline Fishing Adventures.
