### About Flights To Ignace

### Location

Ignace is located in North Western Ontario along highway 17 just over an hour east of Dryden and just over 2 hours west of Thunder Bay. Ignace is the starting point for hwy 599 which heads north to Pickle Lake. Ignace is a popular destination for hundreds of eager sportsmen who come to the area each year to enjoy the exceptional hunting and fishing opportunities. You can book a fly in charter flight to numerous outpost camps and first class lodges.

### Ignace Airport

The Ignace Airport is located 3 miles northwest of town and has a 3,500 foot asphalt runway
