## **About Nejanilini / The Lodge at Little Duck:**

The Lodge at Little Duck benefits from a private airstrip located beside the property, allowing fixed-wing charter aircraft to arrive directly at camp. That setup makes this remote destination more practical than many far-north lodges, because guests can avoid additional water transfers and settle into their cabins quickly after landing.

### The Lodge at Little Duck Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* The lodge uses an approximately 4,000-foot private gravel runway, allowing direct fixed-wing charter access into a remote part of northern Manitoba without extra water transfers.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* The Lodge at Little Duck is a full-service fly-in operation offering private guest cabins, meals, internet access, and personalized service for small fishing and hunting groups.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* The area is known for trophy northern pike, lake trout, and arctic grayling fishing, along with wilderness scenery and access to a remote sub-Arctic landscape.
