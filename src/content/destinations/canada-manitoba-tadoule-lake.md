### Tadoule Lake Services / Accommodations / Contacts:

**Airport and Flight Access**

* Tadoule Lake Airport is located a short distance south of the community, making transfers fairly direct after arrival. The local crushed rock runway supports many wheel-equipped charter aircraft, while helicopters and seasonal floatplane options may also be arranged when required.

**Accommodations and Local Services**

* Visitor services in Tadoule Lake are limited and practical rather than tourist-oriented, so accommodation should be arranged well in advance through local contacts, employers, or community hosts. Travelers should expect basic northern services and bring any essential personal items with them.

**Things to Do And Local Attractions**

* Tadoule Lake appeals mainly to visitors seeking remote northern scenery, wilderness travel, and access to the subarctic landscape. Fishing, wildlife viewing, photography, and seasonal aurora viewing can all be part of the experience for travelers heading into this far northern part of Manitoba.
