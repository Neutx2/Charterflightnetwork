### About Calgary

Calgary is a vibrant city located in the province of Alberta, Canada with a population of about 1.3 million people. It is the largest city in Alberta and the third-largest municipality in Canada.
**Major Economic Industries:**
Calgary's economy is diverse, but historically, it has been strongly tied to the oil and gas industry due to its proximity to the oil sands in northern Alberta. However, the city has diversified its economy over the years. Some of the major economic industries in Calgary include:
**Oil and Gas:** Calgary is often referred to as the "energy capital of Canada" due to its significant presence in the oil and gas sector. The city is home to numerous energy companies, including major players in the oil sands, natural gas, and petroleum industries.
**Finance and Banking** Calgary has a robust financial sector, with many major banks and financial institutions having a significant presence in the city. The Calgary Stock Exchange, now part of the Toronto Stock Exchange, was historically based in the city.
**Technology:** Calgary's technology sector has been growing rapidly in recent years, with a focus on areas such as software development, information technology, and telecommunications. The city is home to numerous tech startups and innovation hubs.
**Healthcare:** Calgary has a strong healthcare sector, with several hospitals, research institutions, and medical centers serving both the city and the broader region.
**Tourism:** Tourism is also a significant industry in Calgary, driven by attractions such as the Calgary Stampede, Banff National Park, and the city's vibrant arts and cultural scene.
**Head Offices:**
Calgary is home to many corporate head offices, particularly in the energy, finance, and transportation sectors. Some notable companies with head offices in Calgary include:

* Enbridge Inc. (energy)
* Suncor Energy Inc. (energy)
* TC Energy (formerly TransCanada Corporation, energy)
* Canadian Pacific Railway (transportation)
* Shaw Communications Inc. (telecommunications)
* Nutrien (agriculture)
* Encana Corporation (energy, now Ovintiv Inc. after relocating its headquarters to Denver, Colorado)
