### About Fort Smith Northwest Territories and Location

Fort Smith is a picturesque town located on the border of the Northwest Territories and Alberta. Known for its stunning natural landscapes, including the Slave River and Wood Buffalo National Park, Fort Smith is a haven for outdoor enthusiasts. The town's rich history, vibrant indigenous culture, and welcoming community make it a must-visit destination for anyone looking to explore the Canadian North. With access to remote destinations made easier by charter flights, Fort Smith offers an unforgettable adventure into the wild.

### Distance From Major Cities

**Fort Smith to Yellowknife**

The distance from Fort Smith to Yellowknife is approximately 735 miles (1,183 kilometers), offering a unique opportunity to experience the vast landscapes of the Northwest Territories.

**Fort Smith to Edmonton**

Traveling from Fort Smith to Edmonton covers a distance of about 870 miles (1,400 kilometers), connecting adventurers to the northern wilderness.

**Fort Smith to Norman Wells**

The journey from Fort Smith to Norman Wells spans roughly 620 miles (998 kilometers), showcasing the diversity of the region's ecosystems.

**Fort Smith to Tuktoyaktuk**

### Fort Smith Airport

Fort Smith Airport is a critical infrastructure piece for the town and its visitors. With a runway length of 6,000 feet (1,829 meters), it accommodates both commercial and charter flights, facilitating access to this remote region. Located just a short drive from downtown, the airport offers various services, including car rentals and shuttle services, making it convenient for travelers to explore the area.

Located strategically to serve the south of the Northwest Territories, Hay River Airport provides essential connections to the rest of Canada.

**Yellowknife Airport**

### Fort Smith Outdoor Adventures

Fort Smith is the ultimate destination for outdoor enthusiasts. From kayaking on the Slave River to hiking in Wood Buffalo National Park, the area is ripe with opportunities for adventure. Winter offers snowshoeing and aurora viewing, while summer invites fishing, bird watching, and canoe trips. Local companies like Northern Adventure Tours specialize in guided tours, ensuring visitors have a safe and memorable experience exploring the natural beauty of Fort Smith and its surroundings.
