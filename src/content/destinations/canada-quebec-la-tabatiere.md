### Charter Flights To La Tabateire from Goose Bay

Because the town has a very short runway, only 1600 feet, the only aircraft in the area that can operate in and out with passengers would be a twin otter, which might be available from [Goose Bay](/canada/newfoundland/goose-bay) which is located 185 air miles north.

If the twin otter is not a practical aircraft for your travel needs, we can source out availability of an aircraft to pick you up in either [Chevery](/canada/quebec/chevery) or [Saint Augustin](/canada/quebec/saint-augustin). Both these locations have runways suitable for a wide variety of aircraft including light to medium size jet aircraft.

We can arrange flights from Chevery or Saint Augustin that will connect you with commercial scheduled flights in Goose Bay, [Halifax](/canada/nova-scotia/halifax) or [Quebec City](/canada/quebec/quebec-city).

### Location

La Tabatiere is located in southeastern Quebec, on the north shore of the Gulf of St Lawrence, between 38 air miles west of Chevery and 32 air miles south west of Saint Augustin.

### La Tabatiere Airport

The town has a very short 1,600 foot gravel runway - which restricts the types of aircraft able to access the airport. Twin Otter aircraft for example may be able to operate in and out of the strip. Charter flights would be more flexible, if arranged into Chevery or Saint Augustin.
