### La Macaza Airport

La Macaza is classified as an International Airport and serves the resort community of Mont Tremblant which is a popular year round outdoor recreation destination and is especially popular in the winter because of the exceptional downhill ski mountains and resorts. The airport is almost 30 miles from Mont Tremblant but can accommodate a wide range of aircraft including large passenger jets, with its 5,900 foot asphalt runway.
