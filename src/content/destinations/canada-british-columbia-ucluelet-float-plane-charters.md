Once you are here, there is plenty to see and do. You can spend the day hiking the Wild Pacific Trail, explore the Ucluelet Aquarium or the Amphitrite Point Lighthouse. There are numerous service firms ready to take you out for a great day of adventure on the water. There are Nature and Wildlife Tours, Boat Tours, Dolphin and Whale Watching Tours as well as Kayak, paddleboard and Canoe rentals. If you want even more adventure, you can take a deep sea fishing charter where you will catch Salmon and Halibut.
