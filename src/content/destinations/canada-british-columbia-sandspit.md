### **About Sandspit**

Private charter flights are available direct to Sandspit BC which is located off the Pacific Coast north of Vancouver Island on Moresby Island which is part of the island chain that was previously called the Queen Charlotte Islands. The Sandspit airport is located 2 miles north east of town and has a 5100 foot asphalt runway, making it accessible to a wide variety of charter aircraft
