### About Flights To Fort Frances

### Location

Located just across the Border from International Falls Minnesota, Fort Frances' proximity to the US market makes it a very popular jumping off point for wilderness trips to the north. You can book a variety of charter flights out of Fort Frances including float planes and wheel planes to remote outpost camps and fly-in fishing lodges. If you are traveling from the USA, you will find that International Falls, Mn is served by a Delta Airlines feeder airline with direct connections out of Minneapolis.

### Fort Frances Airport

The Fort Frances Airport is located 2.5 miles northwest of town and has 4,493 foot asphalt runway

In addition to their main float plane air base in Fort Frances, Rusty Myers also operates a satellite float plane base at Savant Lake which is located on hwy 599 north of Ignace. This is a popular departure point for groups who don't mind the extra drive in order to start their fly-in vacation from this far northern base.
