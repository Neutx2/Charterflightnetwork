About Grand Manan New Brunswick

Discover The Convenience and Affordability of Flying Direct to or From Grand Manan New Brunswick In The Speed Comfort and Security of A Private Charter Flight
Grand Manan is an island located in the Bay of Fundy, south of New Brunswick's mainland and off the coast of Maine, USA. The local public use airport is an airport of entry - staffed by the Canada Border Services Agency Staff which can handle up to 15 passengers. The single asphalt runway is 3,000 feet long, making it accessible by many of the charter aircraft in the region.
