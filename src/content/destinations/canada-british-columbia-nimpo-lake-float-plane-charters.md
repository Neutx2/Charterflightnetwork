The closest airport accessible to wheel plane charter flights is located at Anahim Lake just a few miles to the north of Nimpo Lake. The quickest and most convenient transportation option is to take a private charter flight to Anahim Lake from Vancouver or other departure point and then its just a short drive to Nimpo Lake where you can connect with a float plane charter

Float Plane Charter Flights are available to Nimpo Lake which is a large body of water that extends over 7 miles long and is located 185 west of Williams Lake via highway 20 and 100 miles east of Bella Coola.

Nimpo Lake is a popular starting point for many visitors who come to BC seeking outdoor wilderness adventure such as hunting and fishing. Float plane charter flights are the most popular transportation choice for sportsmen heading out to remote wilderness regions of the province. There is no land airport here, but a very high concentration of float plane charter flights.
