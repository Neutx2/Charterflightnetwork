Jenpeg is served by Jenpeg Airport, identified by the codes ZJG and CZJG. It provides practical air access for charter traffic, utility travel, and work-related operations in this remote part of northern Manitoba.

## **Can I arrange a direct charter flight from Winnipeg to Jenpeg?**

Yes. Winnipeg is one of the most practical departure points for direct charter flights to Jenpeg. Charter Flight Network can also help arrange departures from other Manitoba, Ontario, Canadian, and US airports.

## **What type of runway does Jenpeg Airport have?**

Jenpeg Airport has a gravel runway of roughly 3,800 feet. That makes it suitable for many northern turboprop charter aircraft commonly used for remote Manitoba air service.

## **Are helicopter and float plane charter options available for the Jenpeg area?**

Yes. Depending on the season and the exact destination, helicopter and float plane services may be arranged for nearby waterways, remote access points, work camps, and other specialized travel needs in the wider region.
