### Location

Lindsay Ontario is located in the heart of the Kawartha Lakes region of southern Ontario between Lake Simcoe and Peterborough. With plenty of top rated accommodations and outdoor recreation activities, this might be a destination to consider for a small corporate meeting or bring clients or key employees for a day or two of golf.

### Lindsay Airport

You can fly direct private charter flights to the Lindsay Airport which has a 3500 foot asphalt runway. Passengers arriving by private charter flight at the Lindsay Airport can arrange to have limousine service meet their flight and take them direct to area hotels, golf courses, meetings or special events.

There are no air charter services based in Lindsay. You can however, arrange charter flights out of Peterborough, Muskoka, Toronto or Ottawa
