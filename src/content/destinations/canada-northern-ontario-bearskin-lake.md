## Location

Bearskin Lake is a remote First Nations community located about 425 km north of Sioux Lookout.
There are no charter aircraft based here; however, you can charter flights to Bearskin Lake from almost any airport in North America.
Most charters originate from Pickle Lake, Sioux Lookout, and Red Lake. Since essentials such as groceries and fuel are flown in, local prices are higher than down south.
