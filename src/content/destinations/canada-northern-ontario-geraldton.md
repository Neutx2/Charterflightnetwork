### About Flights To Geraldton

### Location

Geraldton Ontario is located a few hours drive north of Thunder Bay and is in the heart of some of Northern Ontario's finest hunting and fishing regions. There are plenty of opportunities to arrange float plane charter flights to remote outpost camps and fly-in fishing lodges located north of the town. You can also arrange wheel plane charter flights direct to the Geraldton Ontario Airport. If you will require overnight accommodations while in the Geraldton area, we recommend that you make your reservations well in advance. Use our hotel links on the map to find a selection of hotels and then book your accommodations directly with the property of your choice.

The Geraldton area north of Thunder Bay is experiencing an upsurge in mining activity in the past year. This has created numerous requests for charter flights to the Geraldton Airport from numerous exploration and mining companies and mine service companies from departure points such as Sudbury, Timmins, Toronto and Thunder Bay.

The Geraldton Airport is located 3.5 miles north of town and features a 5,000 foot asphalt runway
