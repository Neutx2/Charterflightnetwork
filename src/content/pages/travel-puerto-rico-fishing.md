### [Backwater Tarpon PR:](https://royalisabela.com/golf/)

Royal Isabela Golf Resort is a luxurious and exclusive retreat located on the northwest coast of Puerto Rico, near the town of Isabela. It offers a unique blend of world-class golfing, luxurious accommodations, and exquisite dining experiences amidst breathtaking natural surroundings.

### Golf Course:

The centerpiece of Royal Isabela is its stunning golf course, designed by the renowned golf architect David Pfaff. The course is set atop dramatic cliffs overlooking the Atlantic Ocean, providing golfers with spectacular views at every turn. The layout features 18 challenging holes, each strategically designed to incorporate the natural terrain and offer a memorable golfing experience. With its rugged coastal landscape, lush vegetation, and ocean breezes, the course provides both a challenging and visually stunning round of golf.

### Accommodations:

The resort offers luxurious accommodations in the form of private casitas, which are spacious and elegantly appointed villas nestled within the lush tropical landscape. Each casita boasts panoramic views of the ocean or the golf course and comes with upscale amenities such as plush bedding, oversized bathrooms, private terraces, and modern technology. Guests can choose from a variety of room configurations to suit their preferences, whether traveling as a couple, family, or group.

### Dining:

Royal Isabela prides itself on offering exceptional dining experiences that showcase the best of Puerto Rican cuisine and locally sourced ingredients. The resort's main restaurant, typically named something like "La Casa" or "The Clubhouse," offers a refined yet relaxed atmosphere where guests can enjoy gourmet meals prepared by talented chefs.

## Other Puerto Rico Golf Courses

### [Bahia Beach Golf Club:](https://www.bahiabeachpuertorico.com/golf)

Designed by Robert Trent Jones Jr., the Bahia Beach Golf Club is part of the St. Regis Bahia Beach Resort in Rio Grande. This championship course winds through lush tropical landscapes, mangrove forests, and along the scenic coastline.

### TPC Dorado Beach:

The TPC Dorado Beach boasts two 18-hole golf courses: the East Course and the Sugarcane Course. Designed by Robert Trent Jones Sr. and Robert Trent Jones Jr., respectively, these courses offer challenging play and beautiful scenery.

### Coco Beach Golf and Country Club:

Located in Rio Grande, Coco Beach Golf and Country Club features two championship courses: the Championship Course and the International Course. Both courses were designed by Tom Kite and showcase the natural beauty of the area.

### Punta Borinquen Golf Club:

Situated on the northwest coast of Puerto Rico in Aguadilla, Punta Borinquen Golf Club offers breathtaking ocean views and challenging play. This public golf course is known for its dramatic cliffs and stunning vistas.

### El Conquistador Golf Course:

Part of the El Conquistador Resort in Fajardo, this Arthur Hills-designed golf course offers panoramic views of the Caribbean Sea and the lush El Yunque Rainforest.

### Rio Mar Country Club:

Located at the Wyndham Grand Rio Mar Puerto Rico Golf and Beach Resort in Rio Grande, the Rio Mar Country Club offers two championship courses: the Ocean Course and the River Course. These courses feature scenic landscapes and challenging holes.

### Palmas del Mar Golf Club:

Situated in Humacao, Palmas del Mar Golf Club offers two 18-hole championship courses: the Flamboyan Course and the Palm Course. Designed by Gary Player and Rees Jones, respectively, these courses provide diverse challenges and stunning scenery.
