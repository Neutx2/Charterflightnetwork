## Montreal Charter Flight Airports

## Montréal–Trudeau International Airport (CYUL)

Trudeau International lies approximately 20 km west of downtown Montreal, in Dorval. The airport hosts world-class private aviation facilities including Signature Flight Support and Skyservice Montreal, both offering VIP lounges, hangar space, and direct ramp access.

## Ground Transportation / Nearby Hotels

From Trudeau International, taxis, limousines, and rideshares reach downtown Montreal in about 25–30 minutes via Autoroute 20. Major hotels nearby include Sheraton Montreal Airport, Aloft Montreal Airport, and several business-class accommodations within walking distance of the FBOs.

## Features

* Suitable for all aircraft categories, including long-range jets.
* 24-hour customs and international arrival capability
* Seamless connections with dozens of Domestic and International Scheduled Airlines
* Direct highway access to downtown and West Island corporate centers

---

## Montréal–Metropolitan Airport (CYHU – formerly St-Hubert)

Located in Longueuil, about 15 km southeast of downtown, this secondary airport caters primarily to general aviation and air charter services. Charter passengers are welcomed through FBOs such as Pascan Aviation and Aéroports de Montréal’s private terminals, offering quick boarding and minimal congestion.

## Ground Transportation / Nearby Hotels

From Metropolitan (St-Hubert), travelers can reach central Montreal in roughly 20 minutes using the Route 132 / Jacques Cartier Bridge. Rental cars, executive shuttles, and pre-arranged limousine services are available directly from the FBO terminals. The Sandman Hotel Longueuil and Holiday Inn St-Hubert provide convenient overnight stays for business crews and passengers.

## Features

* Less traffic and faster turnaround times
* Lower landing and handling fees than Trudeau
* Convenient access to Montreal’s South Shore industrial corridor
* Direct highway access to downtown and West Island corporate centers

## Toronto ⇄ Montreal Charter Flights — FAQ

How long is a charter flight from Toronto to Montreal?

Typical block time is ~1:00–1:20 depending on aircraft type (light jet vs turboprop) and winds.

Which Toronto airport is better for charters—Pearson (YYZ) or Billy Bishop (YTZ)?

**YTZ** offers fast access to downtown and quick turns for turboprops/lights. **YYZ** suits larger jets, hangarage, and connections. We’ll quote both when it helps cost/schedule.

Should I land at Montréal–Trudeau (YUL) or Montréal–Metropolitan (YHU)?

**YUL** is closest for West Island/corporate campuses and large jets. **YHU** often has lower fees and faster turns; it’s convenient for South Shore and quick access to downtown via Jacques-Cartier Bridge.

Do I need to clear customs on this route?

No—Toronto to Montreal is a domestic Canadian flight. Standard security procedures apply at the FBO.

What aircraft are common on this route?

Popular picks include Pilatus PC-12 and King Air 200/350 (turboprops) and light/midsize jets such as Citation CJ/Excel and Lear 45/60, depending on passenger count and budget.
