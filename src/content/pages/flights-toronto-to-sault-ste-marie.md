## This Page Lists The Airlines That Provide Private Charter Flights From Toronto To Sault Ste Marie

Sault Ste Marie is located in Northern Ontario on the far eastern shores of Lake Superior and shares the Canada / USA border with Michigan. Because of its close proximity to the USA, the city is a popular destination for thousands of US sportsmen and tourists each year who come to Northern Ontario to experience a Fly-In Fishing Adventure. The **Sault Ste Marie Airport (YAM)** is an airport of entry with customs services provided by the Canadian Border Services Agency. The airport features two 6000 ft ansphalt runways making it accessible to all air charter aricraft in the region.
