### [Jade Mountain Resort](https://jademountain.com/)

Jade Mountain Resort is a luxury resort located in Soufrière, Saint Lucia, renowned for its stunning architecture, breathtaking views of the Pitons, and unparalleled level of luxury and service. Here's everything you need to know about Jade Mountain Resort:

### Location:

The resort is situated on the southwest coast of Saint Lucia, overlooking the Caribbean Sea and the iconic Pitons, which are UNESCO World Heritage-listed twin peaks.

### Architecture and Design:

Jade Mountain is celebrated for its innovative and eco-friendly architecture, designed by owner and architect Nick Troubetzkoy. The resort features a bold and striking design with open-walled suites, called "sanctuaries," that blend seamlessly with the natural surroundings. Each sanctuary is uniquely designed and adorned with locally crafted furnishings and artwork, showcasing the rich culture and heritage of Saint Lucia.

### Accommodations:

The resort offers 29 spacious sanctuaries, each with its own infinity pool and unparalleled views of the Pitons and the Caribbean Sea. The sanctuaries are designed without a fourth wall, creating a seamless connection with nature and maximizing the breathtaking vistas. Guests can choose from various categories of sanctuaries, ranging from Star, Moon, and Sun sanctuaries to Galaxy and Sky suites, each offering its own unique features and amenities.

### Dining:

Jade Mountain Resort is home to several dining options, including the renowned Jade Mountain Club, which serves a fusion of Caribbean and international cuisine prepared with locally sourced ingredients. Guests can also enjoy private dining experiences in their sanctuaries or at scenic locations around the resort, such as on the Celestial Terrace or on the beach.

### Activities:

The resort offers a range of activities and amenities to keep guests entertained and relaxed throughout their stay. Some of the activities include:
**Spa and Wellness:** The Kai en Ciel Spa at Jade Mountain offers a variety of holistic treatments and wellness experiences, including massages, facials, yoga, and meditation
.
**Adventure and Excursions:** Guests can explore the natural beauty of Saint Lucia through guided hikes, snorkeling trips, sailing excursions, and visits to nearby attractions such as the Sulphur Springs and Diamond Botanical Gardens.
**Infinity Pool:** In addition to the private infinity pools in each sanctuary, the resort features an infinity-edge pool overlooking the Caribbean Sea, providing a tranquil space for relaxation and leisure.
