## This Page Lists The Airlines That Provide Private Charter Flights From Toronto To Sudbury Ontario

---

Sudbury, Ontario—known as the nickel capital of Canada—is a vibrant Northern Ontario city surrounded by over 300 lakes and framed by the rugged beauty of the Canadian Shield. Located approximately 220 air miles (355 kilometers) north of Toronto, Sudbury is a key business, mining, and education hub for northeastern Ontario. Charter flights from Toronto to Sudbury offer fast, convenient travel—typically under one hour—connecting directly to Greater Sudbury Airport (YSB), located about 20 kilometres northeast of the downtown core. The airport features a 6,600-foot paved runway, full-service FBOs, car rentals, and U.S. Customs on request for private flights. Sudbury serves as both a business gateway and a jumping-off point for northern outdoor adventures.
