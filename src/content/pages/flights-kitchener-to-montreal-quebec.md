### Private Jet Charters to Montreal, Quebec

A jet charter between Kitchener and Montreal will average just over 1 hour flight time. Depending on the number of associates you will be flying with, you can choose between a light jet that will seat up to 4 passengers or larger mid aize to large jets that will seat up to 10 passengers.

### Turboprop Aircraft Charters

Flight times between Kitchener and Montreal will average 1hour and 25 minutes and may be a more practical and cost efficient option vs a jet charter. The corporate configuration of these aircraft are designed to allow passengers to collaborate and conduct business in a safe and secure environment enroute.

### Twin-Engine Piston Aircraft

Generally a more cost efficient charter option is the twin engine Piper Navajo. On a short flight of 350 air miles to Montreal, flight times will be about 15 minutes more than the turbo prop, but charter costs may be about 10% less. While some models will seat up to 8 passengers, the aircraft is ideal for 1 - 6 passengers.
