## Getting to Churchill

Churchill sits on Hudson Bay with no road in — every visitor arrives by air or
rail. Every year Charter Flight Network helps clients find the most convenient
and competitive charter flight options to Churchill, whether that's a group
travelling together for polar bear season or crews and cargo moving on their
own schedule.

## Why groups charter to Churchill

- **Season timing** — polar bear viewing runs on a narrow seasonal window, and
  scheduled capacity is tight when it opens. A charter flies when your group
  is ready.
- **Group economics** — a chartered aircraft is priced for the aircraft, not
  the seat, so a full tour group or family party often compares well against
  individual fares.
- **Gear and cargo** — camera equipment, expedition gear and freight travel
  with you rather than being routed separately.
