## Flying north takes the right aircraft — and the right crews

Arctic charter work is its own discipline: long legs, gravel and ice strips,
limited alternates and weather that decides the schedule. The Canadian Air
Charter Directory lists charter services with the aircraft and flight crews
experienced with flying into the Canadian Arctic, and our quote request
service puts your trip in front of the ones best positioned for it.

## What Arctic charters are used for

- **Community access** — dozens of remote communities rely on Charter Flight
  Network to find the most competitive air charter options. Network members
  fly scheduled and charter passenger service plus bulk cargo throughout
  Northern Ontario, Northern Manitoba and Nunavut.
- **Resource and government work** — crew rotations, survey flights and
  project logistics for sites accessible only by air. See
  [mining &amp; exploration charters](/travel/mining-exploration).
- **Wildlife and adventure travel** — Churchill, Manitoba is the best-known
  northern destination for visitors; see
  [Churchill charter options](/travel/churchill-polar-bears).
